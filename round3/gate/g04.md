# G04: Humanness Arena (G:agent_economy:2). Verdict: KILL

## Why in one line
The "Gen Z taste panel" is already sold by funded specialists, the per-response price is near zero, and the venture-scale path (public arena plus lab datasets) is occupied by $1B-class players while the last entrant (Yupp) shut down.

## Direct competitors found (all searched Oct 2026)
- **Prolific**: ~$350M annualized revenue (Sacra estimate, April 2026). Sells "taste" evaluation of AI audio, image and video. Its HUMAINE study used 23,404 participants stratified across 22 demographic groups, and found age was the primary axis of disagreement. So the idea's "no Gen Z calibration study at all" is wrong. An 18-24 filter is a checkbox on Prolific. Corporate fee is 42.8% on participant pay; a 100-rater, 5-minute blind test costs the buyer roughly $150.
- **Rapidata** (Zurich): $8.5M seed, Feb 2026. Pay-as-you-go from $4 per 1,000 responses, up to 100k responses per hour, and its SDK has a side-by-side compare job. Named customers are Rime and Uthana (voice and motion AI), the same buyers this idea targets.
- **Podonos**: $2.4M pre-seed, Sept 2025. Human listener evaluation for voice AI, with a claimed 150,000 evaluators and results in under 12 hours. Customers include Resemble AI, Play AI and Sanas. It also has an AI judge trained on its human data.
- **Askable Labs** (VOICE-H, mid-2026): about 4,500 blind comparisons of nine TTS models against human recordings, using identity-verified panelists, published as a free benchmark.
- **Arena (ex-LMArena)**: $1.7B valuation, ~$100M annualized run rate reported June 2026, with enterprise and lab customers.
- **Design Arena / Intelligence**: $7.9M seed, ~$60M ARR self-reported, customers are frontier labs.
- **Taste Labs**: $18.5M seed, preference data for subjective domains.
- **Contra Labs**: creative-expert RLHF.
- **Mercor and Surge**: expert human data, billions in run rate.
- **Craze**: ".edu-verified" Gen Z student panel for AI interviews (tiny traction).
- **Tavus and Hume**: both run their own blind studies in-house and publish them.
- **Synthetic research**: Simile raised $200M at a $2B valuation (July 2026), Aaru is at ~$1B. Simile's human ground truth came from stratified, representative samples (Bovitz, now Gallup), not a Discord panel.
- **Graveyard**: Yupp, $33M seed from a16z crypto, shut down March 2026. Founders said demand for crowdsourced chat-preference data was not growing, and labs moved to paid experts.

## The five findings that decide it
1. **Price floor.** Rapidata charges $4 per 1,000 responses and Prolific is about $1.50 per 5-minute rater. A "100-rater mini-study at $2.5k" sells an input the buyer can get for $5-150. The only thing left to sell is analysis, which is consulting.
2. **Who pays big money.** The $30M, $60M and $100M run rates come from frontier labs buying at scale. Yupp (1.3M users, labs as customers) still died. Labs now buy experts, not Gen Z consumers. Seed-stage consumer AI teams are the ones he can reach, and they are the ones with $0 to a few thousand dollars of budget.
3. **Small buyer pool.** AI companion consumer spend was ~$120M for 2025 (Appfigures via TechCrunch) and 89% of it goes to the top 10% of apps. Voice-AI startups are already served by Podonos, Rapidata and Askable.
4. **Detector-as-fraud-control edge is weak.** Pairwise A/B clicks are not text, so LLM-cheating risk is mostly in open-text answers. Prolific's own research puts LLM use in text tasks near 33%. Prolific and Rapidata already run extensive fraud checks. No evidence found that AI-detector firms (GPTZero ~$24M ARR, Pangram) buy human-judgment data. Pangram says it trains on licensed or owned data.
5. **Credibility.** A third-party "Humanness score" is only worth money if the buyer trusts the panel. An 18-year-old's classmates and Discord is not a stratified, identity-verified panel, and Arena's lab-favoritism critics show how fragile benchmark trust is.

## Can he reach and close the first customers?
- Reach: yes. Cold outreach to Product Hunt AI makers is reachable, but cold reply rates are about 0.5-3.5%, so 100 DMs gives roughly 1-4 replies.
- Close at $2.5k: unlikely. The buyer's alternative is a self-serve tool or a run it themselves in a day. Realistic: 0-3 paid studies in the first 90 days, often at $500-1,500.

## Legal and age
No license wall. He is 18 and can contract. Issues that are real but manageable:
- **18+ only.** Many seniors are 17, so panelists must be verified adults. His school network alone cannot supply 500-2,000 adults, so he needs college and Discord recruitment.
- **Panelist pay is taxable income.** The 1099-NEC reporting threshold is $2,000 per person for 2026 payments, but W-9 collection and payout handling are still required.
- **PII, consent and data-handling terms.**
- **Customers that drift toward "humanizer" or detector-evasion tools** are an academic-dishonesty risk, which the idea already excludes.

## Numbers
- Cost to first revenue: ~$2,000 (rater incentives for 5-10 free mini-studies at about $300 each, plus tooling). The idea's $1,500 is slightly low.
- Weeks to first revenue: ~8.
- Median case: ~$0.05M a year (10-20 studies at $2.5k). The reachable-buyer pool for a boutique tops out near $1-3M a year (about 60 customers at $15k).
- Upside: ~$3M a year as an independent Podonos-style shop. Venture-scale outcomes (Design Arena or Arena style) require lab contracts he cannot reach and a consumer flywheel that Yupp could not make work.

## Sharpened version (a test, not a rescue)
**Gen Z Retention Lab.** Stop selling "which clip sounds human" (a commodity at $0.004 a response). Sell what Prolific, Rapidata and the arenas cannot: whether verified 18-24 users will keep using a consumer AI product. Run multi-day, in-context tests with identity-verified young adults, then calibrate panel scores against the customer's real retention data. Every study adds to a proprietary dataset of pre-launch panel judgments versus post-launch retention across many consumer AI apps. That dataset, not the rater labor, is the asset. It could become the standard that consumer AI product leads and investors doing consumer AI diligence buy.

Kill test before building anything (3 weeks, under $600): DM 40 consumer AI founders, offer a free 50-rater multi-day study, and ask for a $1,500 purchase order or a letter of intent. If fewer than 3 commit to pay, stop.
