# S:I0289 IEP Chief of Staff for Special-Ed Parents: gate report

**Verdict: WEAK, close to kill as pitched.** The deadline tracker, paper-trail log and AI IEP parsing at $10-20 a month are already shipped by at least five tools, the build has no moat, and the founder's Gen Z network cannot reach 35-50-year-old parents. One piece is not shipped and has a dollar anchor: reconciling what the IEP promises against what the school delivered (a missed-minutes ledger and compensatory-services packet). That is the only version worth testing.

## What I found (about 20 searches and fetches, late Oct 2026)

**The "gap" is mostly not a gap. Direct and near-direct competitors:**
- **IEP Compass: Parent Advocate** (iPhone, solo dev). Reminders for "IEP review dates, evaluation deadlines, response windows," a calendar, a school-contact communication log, document storage and AI chat. $9.99/mo or $79.99/yr. It already does the pitched core, but it has 4 App Store ratings, so traction is tiny. It lists no service-minute tracking and no export. https://apps.apple.com/app/id6761731246
- **Undivided** (undivided.io, formerly Special X). Parent platform with an IEP Assistant that extracts goals, services and accommodations, plus human "Navigators." Membership from $9/mo (annual promo $99 cut to $45), Navigator messaging $49/mo, $999/yr for a dedicated Navigator. CB Insights lists about $5M raised (unverified). Free service-delivery log and communication log templates. "Thousands of families" since 2020, which is the scale a funded incumbent reached in six years.
- **My IEP Hero** (solo builder). AI IEP analysis, meeting prep, letter generator, document vault, 504 support, advocate marketplace with a 4% fee. Free tier of 3 analyses, $29/mo Premium (cut from $49), $99/mo top tier. No deadline tracker or service log listed. PeerPush shows 539 AI reads in 30 days. https://peerpush.com/p/my-iep-hero
- **Expert IEP** (expertiep.com, founder Antoinette Banks). Grant-funded (about $1.3-2M, Black Ambition prize, NewSchools, Ballmer). Claims 4,800+ families served and 17+ nonprofit partners. Planning a district product.
- **AdvocateIQ** (DFW Advocacy, Texas, launched Mar 2026). IEP/504 compliance report from about $49 per review.
- **IEP Says**, **IEP&Me** (SF, $775k seed per Dealroom), and a **Skool** paid parent community ($37/mo, 50 members).
- **Free substitutes:** a federally funded Parent Training and Information Center in every state (about $25.8M a year in the FY2025 competition), Wrightslaw, WAPAVE, Undivided's printable service log, Etsy IEP binders, and ChatGPT or Claude. Georgia Appleseed tested the three chatbots on real parent scenarios. They explained law well but sometimes cited the wrong law or drafted letters that wrongly accused the school.
- **School side (not competitors, but they own the data):** AbleSpace, SyncIEP and Frontline log service minutes for districts. Parents see none of it.

**Price discipline:** the market sits at $9-10/mo for trackers and assistants and $29+ only for deeper AI analysis. $12-20 is above the closest tracker competitors, and free templates cap it from below.

## Can he reach and close the first customers?
- The buyers are parents aged about 35-50, mostly mothers, under stress. His classmates, gamers and sports-betting friends are not in this market. His mentor (AV integration) is irrelevant. The family network helps only for a handful of intros.
- Realistic channels are small and fragmented. The largest relevant Facebook groups are Special Needs Support Circle (about 64K, general) and IEP Parent Advocates (about 25K). r/specialed (about 55K) is mostly teachers. r/SpecialNeedsChildren is about 7K. These groups usually restrict promotion. The group-admin route and creator affiliates are the real route.
- A first dollar is easy: a free tier plus a $10 plan can be live in 3-4 weeks. A first dollar is not proof, because churn will be high. Use is episodic (annual meeting, a crisis) and parents cancel afterward.

## Legal and age issues
- **Age:** none. An 18-year-old can form an LLC, take payments and publish to the App Store.
- **Unauthorized practice of law:** a tracker is fine. The danger is AI output that says "your school violated IDEA" or drafts demand letters. New York S7263 (advanced by the Senate as of Mar 2026; I could not confirm it passed) would make chatbot proprietors liable for advice that would be UPL if a human gave it. Keep to documents, dates, citations and templates, with state-agency links.
- **Deadline accuracy is a liability.** The federal 60-day evaluation clock is overridden by state rules: Connecticut 45 school days, North Carolina 90 days, Tennessee formerly 40 school days, Arizona 15 school days to respond to a request. A wrong date from an app a parent trusted is a real harm. This also means ongoing content maintenance across 50 states.
- **Sensitive child data:** FERPA and HIPAA generally do not reach a parent-held copy in a consumer app, and COPPA does not apply because the user is the parent. State consumer-privacy and health-data laws can apply. An 18-year-old holding diagnoses and evaluations is a trust problem even though it is legal. Use on-device redaction, minimal retention and a clear no-sale policy.
- **Compensatory-services contingency fees** are not available to a non-lawyer. Subscription and per-report pricing only.

## Size, bottom-up
- 7.5M students in IDEA services in 2022-23 (NCES), about 7.9M in 2023-24 (secondary source). About 15% of public enrollment.
- The pitch's $9.6M needs 40,000 paying subscribers at $20, which is about 0.5% of students. Observed paid scale across all incumbents is thousands of families, at $9-10, not $20. Net of 15-30% app-store fees, a $10 sub nets about $8.50.
- At 40,000 subs and $10, the revenue is about $4M. That is already a heroic share of the paying pool.
- Median: 3,000 paying subs at about $8.50 net per month is about $0.3M a year by year 3.
- Upside needs a second engine. A professional-advocate tier ($49-99/mo): COPAA has about 3,600 members (attorneys, advocates and parents), and I found no purpose-built practice software for independent advocates. Add per-report sales at $49 and referral from advocates and PTIs. That reaches roughly $4-8M.
- Due-process volume is a rough upper bound on the intense-conflict segment: about 19.5K due process complaints, 11.7K mediation requests and 5.4K state complaints in 2017-18.
- Why-now: the 2025 layoffs at the Office of Special Education Programs were partly reversed. In June 2026 the Education Department moved special-ed oversight to HHS. Advocates say this raises uncertainty about enforcement, but I found no data that complaints are rising.

## Deciding reason
Everything pitched is already live at $9-29/mo, replicable by ChatGPT, and built by solo developers in weeks, so AI parsing is not an edge. The unreachable-payer problem and the small observed paid market make a $10M+ outcome unlikely. The one uncontested layer is the school-delivery ledger and claim packet.

## Sharpened version (does not shrink the market)
**Title:** IEP Ledger, the receipts for every minute your child was promised.
Parse the IEP into a service ledger. Auto-draft the records request for the school's provider service logs (parents have inspection rights under IDEA/FERPA). Ingest those logs by photo or PDF, compute the shortfall by service and quarter, price the missing hours at local private rates, and produce a dated, state-cited compensatory-services request packet. Free tier does deadlines. $9.99/mo or $79/yr is the going rate. $49 one-time "Shortfall Report". Advocate Pro at $49-99/mo gives multi-family dashboards and branded packets, and is the distribution engine: each advocate brings 20-50 families. Never states legal conclusions. Add districts' data and state rule packs later.

**Test before building:** 2-week fake-door landing page and 15 advocate interviews. Proceed only if 5 advocates agree to pay for Advocate Pro and 100 parents give an email for the Shortfall Report.

## First customers
Independent special-ed advocates (COPAA advocate members), IEP Parent Advocates FB group via admin, Special Needs Support Circle, r/SpecialNeedsChildren, TikTok/IG IEP educators on affiliate (Adriane Gay, Kimberly Kizito "IEP strategist"), state PTIs as free-seat partners, local Decoding Dyslexia/CHADD/Autism Society chapters (named from background knowledge, not searched), special-ed attorneys' intake staff, private SLP/OT clinics, paid parent communities on Skool.

## Cost and timing
About $1,200 to first revenue (Apple developer $99, hosting/LLM, a lawyer review of terms and privacy, small test ads). About 6 weeks.

## Priority
Do not pursue ahead of the freight audit. Run only the fake-door test, because the advocate channel is cheap to test.
