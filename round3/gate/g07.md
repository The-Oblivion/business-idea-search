# G07 gate: Usage-Bill Savings Share for LLM, cloud and observability bills (G:contingency_audit:1)

**Verdict: WEAK.** Not dead, not strong. A good lead-gen and credibility experiment, but not a $10M business as pitched.

## What the search found (22 searches, late Oct 2026)

**Nobody sells exactly this to startups, but every lever is free or commoditizing.**
- No startup-focused vendor sells LLM-bill savings share. Closest is Redress Compliance (25% contingency, but enterprise contract negotiation, not code). Flat-fee audits exist and are cheap: ActiveWizards $3-6k, Contra from $1.8k, Upwork $200-1,200.
- Ramp Router (router.com, Aug 2026): free managed gateway routing each request to the cheapest model that clears a quality bar. Ramp claims 30% internal savings; routing is free through 2026.
- Provider-native: OpenAI caching is automatic (50% off cached input). Anthropic auto-caching is one top-level field. Batch is 50% off at the big providers. Bedrock offers prompt routing (up to 30%, AWS claim).
- Free coding-agent skills (e.g. llm-cost-optimizer in the Claude skills registry) already encode caching, routing and batching. A customer's own coding agent can produce much of the "diff" for free.
- Funded or backed: Mentlio (YC S2026, claims ~30% off AI spend), Revenium ($13.5M seed, AI FinOps), CloudZero and Vantage (OpenAI/Anthropic connectors, AI optimization suggestions), Finout.
- Gateways are consolidating: Portkey bought by Palo Alto Networks (May 2026), Helicone absorbed by Mintlify (maintenance mode). Pure routing was not a standalone business.
- Inference clouds (Fireworks, Together, Inference.net, OpenPipe) pitch 50-95% cuts by moving narrow tasks to cheaper models, and capture the spend themselves.
- Cloud and observability expansion is already crowded with software: Vantage Autopilot takes 5% of savings, Zesty 10-20%, CloudKeeper 18%, Usage.ai, nOps, Antimetal, Pump. ProsperOps (the pitch's proof point) was acquired by Flexera on Jan 6, 2026 (price undisclosed).

**Size problem.** Ramp data (Sept 2026): the top 1% of customers produce 80% of OpenAI and Anthropic enterprise revenue. Anthropic says ~6,000 customers spend $100k+/yr (all sizes, secondhand figure). So the $5k-$200k/month band is a few thousand organizations worldwide, many with account reps, committed-use discounts and in-house infra teams. The pitch's 2,000 customers is a 30%+ share of the reachable pool.

**Unit economics.** $15k/month bill x 30% savings x 20% share = $900/month, about $10.8k/year per customer, for 12 months, then zero unless re-optimized. Savings also shrink as model prices fall, and credit-funded spend (startup credits) produces no cash to share.

## Founder-specific blockers
- Age/legal: not real. 18 can sign contracts and form an LLC; technical buyers do not care. No license wall.
- Trust/security: real. "Read-only" is not literally true: Anthropic's Admin API key is broad (manages users and keys); I found no narrower scope, so he would rely on CSV exports. Prompts hold customer data, so Series A-B buyers will ask for a DPA or SOC 2.
- Terms of service: real for one lever. Anthropic and OpenAI terms bar using outputs to train competing models, so "distill to a cheaper model" is risky; routing and caching among existing models is fine.
- Capacity: real. Eval-gated PRs are hand work; 10-15 hrs/week supports about 3-5 live customers.
- Capital: not a blocker (about $500).

## Numbers
- First revenue: about 9 weeks via the $1,500 flat sprint; first contingency dollar about 13 weeks.
- Cost to first revenue: about $500.
- Median case: about $0.4M/yr (about 40 customers at ~$10k/yr). Upside: about $8M/yr (500 accounts at $20k/month average bill, 30% savings, 20% share), and only if it becomes software with a data asset.

## Sharpened version (does not shrink)
**Model-Price Treadmill: a continuous, eval-gated LLM cost agent paid on verified savings.** Build a private regression eval from each customer's own prompts, then open a PR every time a cheaper model, cache rule or batch window passes it. Locked baseline, a savings meter, 20% of verified savings while the meter runs (repriced yearly). Falling model prices become the engine instead of the threat. The asset is an anonymized cross-customer benchmark of which cheaper model matches which task type. Move up to $30k-$500k/month accounts once there are 5+ merged-PR case studies. Drop the cloud and observability expansion.

## Month-one test
Publish the teardown of his own pipeline; 10 free X-rays; goal 3 merged PRs and 1 signed meter. Kill it if fewer than 3 founders will share billing exports.
