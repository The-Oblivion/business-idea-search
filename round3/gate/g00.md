# G:agent_economy:1 AgentFit (agent-run audits for devtools): gate report

**Verdict: KILL as pitched.** The "nobody sells this" premise is false, the core harness is free and MIT-licensed, and the idea's own ceiling is $3-8M.

## What I found (about 28 searches and fetches, late Oct 2026)

**The gap claim is wrong. Direct and near-direct competition:**
- **Netlify AXIS** (axis.run, MIT, launched June 2026). "Lighthouse for agent experience." Runs Claude Code, Codex and Gemini (plus a custom adapter) on a prompt and rubric. Supports repeat runs (odd counts, 1-19, median headline), baselines, and `--compare-baseline` for regression detection. Auth0 and Resend are founding contributors. This is the harness and the CI regression layer, free.
- **Amplifying** (amplifying.ai/for-vendors). Sells devtool vendors "Benchmarking" (private dashboards of how coding agents recommend your tool vs competitors) and "Ground Control" (change docs or tooling, rerun the same install/auth/workflow tasks, recorded runs with inspectable tool calls). Pricing is call-only. Their Feb 2026 "What Claude Code Actually Chooses" study (2,430 responses) got wide pickup. This is the closest paid competitor.
- **Auth0 Agent Experience Score** (Apr 2026). In-house. Claude Code, Copilot and Gemini CLI run integration tasks in isolated environments, with and without MCP and skills. Public scoreboard (63% without tools, 97% with). One run costs about $0.17 and 57 seconds. Free.
- **Microsoft DevAX** (microsoft.github.io/agent-experience). Free methodology and open-source tools, 5-10 runs per condition, baseline then lift/drag verdicts.
- **Vercel next-evals** (nextjs.org/evals). Public agent leaderboard for Next.js, with and without AGENTS.md, pass@4.
- **Free static scorers:** Fern Agent Score and AFDocs (MIT), GitBook Agent-Readiness Checker, Document360 readiness score, Postman AI-Ready APIs, Cloudflare isitagentready, StartupHub index, Rhumb AN Score. Scores are a free lead-gen asset for docs platforms, so the price anchor is $0.
- **Free DIY:** Browserbase agent-experience skill, mblode ax-audit skill.
- **Services:** Tech Stackups / Ritza (hands-on AX audit of PostHog, 28 sessions, technical-writing agency), Evil Martians (no verified measured-suite offer).
- **In-house:** AgentMail (YC S25) is hiring an "Agent Experience Engineer" at $110-175k.

**Budget evidence:** Agent share of Mintlify docs traffic is 66% (Aug 2026) and about 78% by October, per Mintlify's own series (vendor-reported). Mintlify is at about $21.4M ARR (Sacra estimate). So attention and a docs/DevRel budget line exist, but no paid AX audit with a public price turned up anywhere. Every visible supplier gives the score away. DevRel budgets are thin: in a 2024 survey about 40% of respondents reported no set budget (secondary source).

**Cost reality:** The idea's "$300 for 50 devtools at 200 runs" does not hold. At $0.17-$0.50 a run, 50 tools x 4 agents x 200 runs is 40,000 runs, or $7k-$20k. About 5-10 runs per tool-agent costs $170-$1,000. A full 800-run audit is about $140-$400 of compute, fine against $2,500. Monthly release-by-release monitoring at $750 with 800 runs per release loses money unless run counts drop sharply.

## Founder fit
- **Reachable?** Yes for seed to Series A devtools. Developers judge artifacts, so a failure video is a real credential and age is not a blocker. Series C and enterprise buyers are not needed and not the target.
- **Mentor network does not map.** Marty Schaffel founded AVI-SPL (audio-visual integration), so warm intros to devtool DevRel heads are unlikely. The "warm intros" line in the pitch is unsupported.
- **Real friction:** (1) Thousands of automated signups on third-party devtools can breach their ToS and trip CAPTCHA, email verification and fraud systems. He needs vendor test modes or disclosed test accounts. (2) Anthropic's consumer terms bar automated access except via API key, so subscription plans are out and API costs apply. (3) Publishing named failures needs a right of reply to avoid a hostile launch. (4) Results decay with every model release (Amplifying saw Prisma 79% flip to Drizzle 100% across model generations), which turns the index into ongoing weekly work at 10-15 h/week.
- **Not blockers:** license (none needed), capital (under $2k), age for LLC and contracts.

## Numbers
- Cost to first revenue: about $1,200 (compute, LLC, domain, hosting).
- Weeks to first revenue: about 9 (2 build on AXIS, 2 run index, 5 outreach and close).
- Reachable payers: roughly 1,500-3,000 seed-to-C API/devtool companies with a DX owner (Crunchbase's developer-API hub lists 757 organizations; Mintlify customer counts range from 583 detected to 10,000+).
- Median case: about $0.4M a year by year 3 (3-5 audits a quarter plus 15-25 recurring at about $1k a month).
- Upside: about $8M a year (matches the idea's own $3-8M devtool ceiling, plus lab sponsorship and SaaS expansion). $10M at $1k a month needs 830 customers, over a quarter of the reachable base.

## Deciding reason
The harness, repeat-run statistics and CI regression layer are free from Netlify, the biggest names are building their own, and Amplifying already sells the paid version to the same buyers. The whole category gives scores away as marketing, so $0 is the price anchor. Under the lessons rule "defensible and big, or dead," this is neither.

## Sharpened version (only as a 6-week, 45-day-kill test)
**AX Drift Index.** Do not build a harness; run AXIS. Re-run a fixed 40-devtool task bank within 72 hours of every major Claude, GPT or Gemini release, publish free with vendor right of reply, and sell a $2,500 audit (cash), a $1,500-$4,000 a month drift feed (alerts when a model update breaks onboarding or drops pick rate), and a data license to investors and agent vendors. Kill if fewer than 3 paid audits or zero unprompted feed requests by day 45. The audit alone is a $10-30k side income, not a big business.

## First customers
Outreach to seed to Series A API/devtool founders and DX heads with their own failure video: YC S25/W26/S26 devtool and infra batches (AgentMail-type), the lowest scorers on Fern, Postman and StartupHub lists (Postman scored Postmark 62/100, StartupHub scored Speakeasy 62/100), plus Show HN, X DMs, r/ClaudeAI, Latent Space and DevRel Collective communities. These are targets, not verified buyers.
