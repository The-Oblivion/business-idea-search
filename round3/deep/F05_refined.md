# F05 Refined: Credit Concierge (federal scholarship tax credit, IRC 25F)

Written 2026-10-25 after the dossier and two fatal critiques. **Verdict: alive = false.** The strongest honest version of this idea has no revenue path, and the version with a revenue path is already free or paid per donor by funded incumbents.

Source key: [V] = I fetched or searched it myself today. [C] = reported by a critic from a page they opened. [D] = from the dossier. "Inference" = my own arithmetic.

## 1. What I re-checked today

- [V] EducationSuperHighway's calculator is free, with an iframe embed (520x780), a small attribution link, "no separate license", and no fee stated. It is aimed at donors and invites tax advisors, giving blogs and financial-planning sites to embed it. It covers a State SGO field that reduces the federal credit, the five-year carryforward and the $1,700 / $3,400 caps. It has a name/email/phone/state sign-up and an optional "introduce us to your employer's HR team" box.
- [V] ACE's advisor page says "3% referral fee" and nothing else. It lists no eligibility, tiers, payment timing, toolkit, or publisher terms. So even the only price signal is not shown to be open to a website.
- [V] A search for any donor-facing platform, marketplace or "donor engagement" startup for 25F returned none. Given three live SGO-side vendors, I read that as no proven demand, not an open gap.
- [D][C] No sourced price exists for an SGO sponsorship slot, a white-label embed, or a tax-software or payroll integration.

## 2. Critiques, answered one by one

### Critique 1 (fatal)

| Point | My answer |
|---|---|
| The engine already exists free and is packaged for the same channels | **Concede.** Verified above. The moat left is a maintained table of about 20 states' SGO-credit rules plus filing-status handling. That is a weekend of copying. |
| The headline "$1,700 vs $3,400" claim is wrong; 46.5M has no source | **Concede.** The proposed reg 1.25F-2(c)(1) makes the allowed credit the lesser of (gift minus state credits) or $1,700. State credits reduce the federal credit; they do not stack. The dossier already said so. I drop the claim and the 46.5M. Treasury's release says 11M+ taxpayers and about $26B by 2030. |
| Flat sponsorships have no demand evidence and face free substitutes | **Concede.** ACE EmpowerEd (no platform, markup or transaction fees), FACTS Compass (120+ SGOs, Oct 15), CSF turnkey services and ClassWallet (38 states) all exist. The likely buyers have no gift income before Jan 1, 2027, and an SGO's 10% admin cap is already tight. "Three paid pilots by Mar 31" asks cash-less buyers to prepay a teenager. |
| The funnel and the only payer are the same incumbent, and it pays per donor | **Concede.** 3% of $1,700 is about $51. Paid donor acquisition runs about $46 / $86 / $178 per donation (search / social / display, M+R via Ortto, not opened by me). Paid traffic cannot fund itself at $51, which leaves organic SEO against IRS.gov, EdChoice, ESH and ACE's eight-figure campaign. |
| Tax prep, advisers and payroll are occupied or blocked | **Concede.** Workplace Scholarships (Jan 2027, free to employers) and AFC are already working payroll and preparers. Tax vendors must build Form 8525 anyway, so a what-if estimator is a trivial in-house add-on. The 7216 consent constraint on using return data to pitch non-tax services is search-synthesized, so I treat it as a risk, not a fact. |
| Demand ceiling is 4-17x lower; Gen Z does not apply | **Concede.** Brookings (Ohio claim rates) gives about $1.5B a year. Urban gives $2.7-6.1B after a ramp. Donors need $1,700+ of federal liability, which classmates lack. Revenue is deferred to 2028 filings. |
| Durability and neutrality on one statute | **Concede.** Regs are proposed (comments due Dec 1, hearing Dec 15). HI, MN, NM and OR have declined, NY is undecided, and a repeal bill exists. A sponsor-funded "neutral" tool will read as an advocate's tool to half the audience. |
| Legal friction (MA co-venturer bond $25k, MD, CA AB 488, about 20 co-venturer states) | **Concede as unresolved.** No source says a disclosed flat fee is safe. The only low-exposure version is free with no steering, which earns nothing. Counsel at $2-10k is a large bet against a $51 price. |
| Opportunity cost and sequencing | **Concede. This is the decisive point.** A freight-audit service is built and unsold. Build-first-sell-later is backwards here. |

### Critique 2 (fatal)

| Point | My answer |
|---|---|
| First dollar not before late 2027; the stop test trips | **Mostly concede.** A flat sponsorship could be paid in Q1 2027 by an SGO with seed money, but there is zero evidence of one. Referral income cannot arrive before donors give and the 2027 returns are filed in 2028. I call it unproven, not impossible. |
| Founder fit is weakest where the revenue sits | **Concede.** The SGO, diocese and coalition network is closed and relationship-driven. His mentor is AV integration. CPAs get paid by ACE directly. |
| Solicitation law is a multi-state wall | **Concede.** About 26 states register fundraising counsel (search-level). MA co-venturer $25k, FL solicitor $50k, OH $25k, CA AB 488. The "disclosed, flat, not per donor" fix is an inference, not a legal finding. |
| Pay-to-play destroys the neutrality the model needs | **Concede.** CPAs quoted in trade press are lukewarm. The first sponsors would be the newest SGOs, and a neutral page cannot take their money. |
| Steering donors creates liability he cannot insure | **Concede.** The reg text I have seen does not say what happens to a January 2027 gift made before the SGO is state-listed (lists due Feb 15, 2027). A donor losing $1,700 after a sponsored recommendation is a real grievance. |
| Net-cost engine already shipped free | **Concede.** Verified. |
| Durability and timing | **Concede.** Demand concentrates in Q4 2027 giving and early-2028 filing, during his first college semester. |
| Platform and distribution risk | **Concede.** Google Ads limits charitable-solicitation ads to tax-exempt charities. Per-donor economics lose to acquisition cost. |
| Reputational spillover during admissions | **Partly push back.** A neutral calculator is not an endorsement, and the cost is modest. But it is a real cost with no revenue to offset it, so I do not argue it away. |

**Net:** I could rebut about 1.5 of 18 points, and neither rebuttal changes the verdict.

## 3. Pivots I tried before calling it dead (and why each fails)

| Pivot | Payer / budget | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Donor-drive kit for private-school development offices | School head or development director, advancement budget | SGOs hand schools toolkits and EmpowerEd free. PA schools already build their own 100-150 donor pools. Tight budgets. |
| CPA client-scanner ("which clients should hear about 25F") | Small CPA firms | Advisers call it niche. 7216 consent limits. Tax vendors build it in. Seasonal. |
| SGO back-office compliance software | SGO executive director | Treasury projects 600-700 SGOs by 2030. At $10k each and 100% share that is $7M. Against free ACE EmpowerEd and Nelnet. |
| Family scholarship matching and application | SGO, inside its 10% cap | FACTS and ClassWallet own the family side. Payer is capped. |
| Found my own SGO | Donors | A nonprofit with a 10% admin cap, no equity, a political identity, and compliance he cannot staff. Not a business. |

Inference on the only "big" scenario: at 3%, Treasury's $26B is a $780M national referral pool, so $10M is about 1.3% of it. Brookings' $1.5B is a $45M pool, so $10M is about 22%. The large case needs Treasury's high take-up and a distribution deal with a payroll or tax vendor, which is the deal AFC and ACE are already chasing and which he has no warm path into.

## 4. If he still wants to test it: a sell-before-build test, 10-12 hours total

Do not build the calculator first. Test the one thing nobody has shown: that someone will pay.

- **Day 1-3:** Write a one-page mock offer (no code): free neutral calculator, disclosed flat sponsorship of $500/month from Jan 2027, billed only if the page sends 100 donor starts. Draft one attorney question for a nonprofit-law clinic or a state bar referral: does a disclosed flat-fee placement make me fundraising counsel or a solicitor in MA, OH, CA or MD?
- **Day 4-7:** Email 12 SGOs and partners (list below) asking for a signed paid-sponsorship LOI. Email ACE's partner team for written publisher terms: rate, eligibility, disclosure. Email Workplace Scholarships asking whether they want an HR calculator embed. Output: 14 asks sent.
- **Day 8-14:** Follow up once. Use the 10/27 mentor call (Marty Schaffel) for one ask only: any warm intro to a school-advancement or nonprofit-vendor buyer. Output: reply count and any LOI.
- **Day 15-21:** Get the attorney answer. Output: a yes / no / needs-registration note.
- **Day 22-30:** Decision gate. If there are fewer than 2 signed LOIs and no written ACE rate of 3% or better with eligibility terms, archive F05 and move the hours to freight-audit outreach (10 diagnostic offers sent). Hard stop at the Dec 15 hearing.

## 5. Numbers

- Cost to first revenue: about $3,000 (LLC, domain, one attorney consult), only if the test passes.
- Weeks to first revenue: 40 or more. Referral income waits on 2027 gifts and 2028 filings.
- Median revenue case: about $0.02M a year (about 250 paid donors at $51 plus a couple of small sponsorships).
- Upside case: about $2M a year (a payroll or tax-vendor deal sending about 40k donors at $51). Still short of $10M.

## 6. Quit conditions

- No signed paid LOI from any SGO, and no written ACE publisher rate of 3% or better, by Dec 15, 2026.
- Counsel or an AG says a disclosed flat-fee placement needs fundraising-counsel or solicitor registration in MA, OH, CA or MD.
- Final regs change the ordering rule, or the IRS or Treasury publishes an official calculator.
- A repeal bill moves out of committee.
- If a calculator is ever built: fewer than 500 donor starts by Mar 31, 2027.

## 7. Decision

Kill as a venture. Do not shrink it to a free calculator for its own sake. Route the 10-15 weekly hours to the freight-audit service that is already built and unsold.
