# G10: Documentation-to-Cash Desk (G:mhc_revenue:1)

Date: 2026-10-26. Verdict: **WEAK** (as a venture it is close to dead; as a family-pilot experiment it is cheap and legal).

## Verdict in one paragraph
The pain is real (unsigned orders and unanswered records requests cause most Palmetto denials and held claims), a family agency is a real test bed, and no license wall blocks him. But the idea fails the founder's own tests 5 and 6. CMS's own paperwork-burden numbers put the whole Review Choice labor pool at about $42M a year, roughly one quarter of the pitch's $150M. Denials are tiny in count. SimiTree, Enzo, Forcura, Axxess and offshore virtual assistants already cover the job. The second act (a 50-state order-release network) is the exact turf of Berkshire-backed Forcura and $26M-funded Enzo.

## Competitors and substitutes
- SimiTree: outsourced RCD functions, RCM, coding/OASIS, SARA AI chart review. 750+ active clients, 16,000+ agencies served (self-reported). No prices published.
- Enzo Health: AI-native EHR with QA, order tracking through signature, claim block until orders are signed. $26M raised ($20M Series A, May 2026), says revenue is up 40x.
- Forcura + Medalogix (Berkshire Partners, 2025): orders tracking, Circle e-signature, a physician network, Best in KLAS. This is the "second act" already built.
- Axxess (EHR): Pre-Claim Review Center, orders management, physician portal, UTN claim validator. It does not judge document quality or auto-submit yet.
- AutomationEdge CareFlo: RPA for the whole RCD workflow (form checks, portal upload, status monitoring).
- Claim Health (YC, $4.4M seed, Jan 2026): AI revenue ops for post-acute, claims 30-50% fewer preventable denials. Adjacent.
- In-house coordinators and offshore virtual assistants: the real price ceiling. The CMS burden implies about $9K per agency per year of RCD labor.

## Size
- RCD labor pool: about $42M a year at 100% share. Denial dollars: about $5-10M. Held-claim release in RCD states: about $6-12M at the pitch's $15-25 per claim (my estimate, assumes 10-20% of periods held).
- $10M a year is about 17% of the entire pool even if every agency outsourced. Realistic outsourcing rate is 10-25%, so $10M is not reachable inside RCD, which also sunsets around May 2029.
- Median case about $0.4M a year (about 40 agencies at about $10K). Upside about $6M, and only if the order-release network pivot works against Forcura and Enzo.

- Target postpayment-choice agencies below 90% first. Agencies in pre-claim at 97% have little pain.

## Teen blockers
- Age: not a legal wall (adult, can sign BAAs and contracts). Credibility with PHI is real, especially during the 2026 fraud crackdown.
- License: none needed for documentation QA, but "is the F2F related to the primary reason" is a clinical call. He needs contract RN/HIM reviewers. Real cost, real.
- Legal: flat per-item fees avoid the Medicare billing-agent rule (42 CFR 424.73) and Florida's ban on percentage arrangements. Do not tie fees to dollars. Never alter or backdate orders. Give physicians nothing of value (Anti-Kickback). Pay-per-affirmation needs counsel review (about $1-2K).
- Security: BAA, HIPAA risk analysis, E&O and cyber insurance. Non-family buyers may ask for SOC 2 (an estimated $10-30K). This is the hidden wall after the family pilot.
- Time: a daily-SLA operations desk does not fit 10-15 hours a week without a paid bench.

## Numbers
- Cost to first revenue: about $5,000 (LLC, E&O/cyber, counsel for BAA and contract, contract RN hours). The pitch said $3,500.
- Weeks to first revenue: about 8 (family). Non-family: about 20-30.

## Sharpened version
**Signed-Order Release Desk (held claims to cash) for home health and hospice.** Widen, do not shrink: sell the thing that drives most 2026 denials and held claims (missing plan-of-care/certification signatures and unanswered records requests), make the RCD packet check the free lead-in, price per claim released, start in Florida postpayment agencies, then hospice and all 50 states. Treat Forcura and Enzo as partners or acquirers, not as rivals to out-build.
