# Briefing: Aging Populations, Eldercare, Caregivers, Silver Economy (as of 2026-09-28)

Note: web search budget ran out mid-run (about 30 searches and 6 page fetches completed). Some figures come from aggregator or vendor blogs and are flagged "(secondary)".

## 1. Biggest shifts, last 12-18 months

- **The oldest boomers turned 80 in 2026.** The US 80+ population is projected to grow about 36.6% from 2025 to 2035, against 5% total population growth (secondary: [MMCG](https://www.mmcginvest.com/post/u-s-senior-housing-market-report-2026-outlook-occupancy-cap-rates-and-forecasts-through-2031)). Care needs move from optional to essential at this age.
- **Senior housing supply has collapsed while demand is at a record.**
  - Occupancy is 90.1% across the top 99 metros, the highest since late 2007. 57 of 99 markets are above 90% ([NIC MAP 2Q26](https://www.nicmap.com/blog/senior-housing-construction-trends/)).
  - Units under construction are below 24,000, the lowest since mid-2012. Inventory growth was a record-low 0.4% in Q1 2026.
  - New supply is unlikely to arrive before 2030. NIC MAP estimates a need for about 549k more units by 2028 and 806k by 2030, a $275B shortfall ([Greystone](https://www.greystone.com/insights/senior-housing-market-q1-2026-occupancy-nears-90-supply-hits-historic-lows-investment-activity-accelerates/)).
- **Labor is being squeezed from both sides.**
  - There are 2.3M direct care workers. 30% are immigrants (60% in New York), 66% earn under $35k, and 32% are on Medicaid themselves ([KFF](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/who-are-direct-care-workers-and-how-might-federal-policy-changes-impact-the-workforce/)).
  - At least 1 in 5 immigrant direct care workers come from countries hit by the 75-country visa pause. About 350,000 Haitians lost TPS on Aug 4.
  - In Wisconsin, agencies are "just not able to take new clients" and nursing homes have "closed beds and closed wings" ([Wisconsin Watch, Sep 2026](https://wisconsinwatch.org/2026/09/wisconsin-caregiver-shortage-federal-immigration-policy-home-health-nursing-workers/)).
  - The federal nursing-home staffing mandate was repealed Feb 2, 2026 ([Fierce](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/regulatory/hhs-closes-book-controversial-nursing-home-staffing-mandate)). A proposed DOL rule would roll back minimum wage and overtime protections for home care workers.
  - Direct care is still projected to add 772k home care jobs over ten years, with 9.7M total direct-care openings from 2024 to 2034 ([search summary of KFF and BLS](https://newrepublic.com/article/207658/cuts-medicaid-home-health-care)).
- **Medicaid retrenchment.** About $911B in federal Medicaid cuts plus work requirements will push more seniors onto unpaid family care ([KFF](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/who-are-direct-care-workers-and-how-might-federal-policy-changes-impact-the-workforce/)). 41 states have HCBS waitlists with over 600k people. Texas alone has 342,575. Average wait for senior-targeted programs is 15 months ([KFF](https://www.kff.org/medicaid/a-look-at-waiting-lists-for-medicaid-home-and-community-based-services-from-2016-to-2025/)).
- **Medicare Advantage is shrinking benefits.** 35M+ are enrolled ([Trella](https://www.trellahealth.com/resource-center/blog/six-months-in-what-the-cy2026-home-health-final-rule-is-telling-the-market-and-what-to-watch-for-in-2027/)). Insurers exited counties, forcing 2.6M enrollees off plans for 2026, and Humana expects about 600k affected in 2027. OTC rollover benefits fell from 9.6% to 2.4% of plans ([Fierce](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/regulatory/cms-proposes-flat-ma-rates-risk-adjustment-updates-2027)).
- **Alzheimer's is moving toward blood tests and home dosing.**
  - FDA cleared Lumipulse pTau217 in May 2025 and a second primary-care blood test in Oct 2025, though Lumipulse had a Class II lot recall ([AAFP](https://www.aafp.org/afp/2026/0600/diagnostic-tests-lumipulse-blood-biomarker-test-alzheimer-disease)).
  - Leqembi got a subcutaneous at-home starting dose. Q1 sales were up 74% to $168M, and Eisai and Biogen expect over $1B this year ([pharmaphorum](https://pharmaphorum.com/news/fda-clears-home-start-dose-alzheimers-drug-leqembi)).
  - Oral semaglutide failed in two Phase 3 Alzheimer's trials (3,808 patients) ([Science](https://www.science.org/content/article/popular-obesity-drug-fails-hotly-anticipated-alzheimers-trials)).
- **Hospital-at-Home waiver extended 5 years, to Sep 30, 2030** ([AMA](https://www.ama-assn.org/public-health/population-health/lawmakers-extend-cms-hospital-home-waiver-five-years)).
- **Older adults are adopting AI fast.** 23% of Americans 65+ use ChatGPT, up from 10% a year earlier ([Pew, Jun 2026](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/how-opinions-and-use-of-ai-differ-by-age/)). But 69% of 50+ are uncomfortable sharing health records with an AI tool ([AARP](https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/technology/internet-media-devices/artificial-intelligence-health-tools/)).

## 2. Acute, quantified pain points

- **Family caregiving is a $1T unpaid workforce.** 59M caregivers gave 49.5B hours in 2024, valued at $1.01T ([AARP, Mar 2026](https://www.aarp.org/press/releases/2026-03-26-AARP-Economic-Value-Of-Family-Caregiving-Report.html)).
  - Average out-of-pocket spend is $7,242 and 26% of income (AARP 2021 data, the latest available).
  - Caregivers lose about $21k a year in income, and 29% are sandwich-generation ([AARP](https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/ltss/family-caregiving/caregiving-in-the-us-sandwich-generation/)).
  - About 15% provide 60+ hours a week ([U.S. News](https://health.usnews.com/home-care/articles/the-financial-and-social-costs-of-caregiving-2026-u-s-news-survey)).
- **Care is unaffordable and unpredictable.** 2025 cost-of-care medians (CareScout/Genworth, released Mar 2026) are $114,975 a year for a semi-private nursing home room, $74,400 for assisted living, and $80,080 for 44 hours a week of home care. The non-medical home caregiver median is $35 an hour ([secondary](https://caringvillage.com/blog/family-caregiving/in-home-care-cost/)). Genworth has approved roughly $31.8B (NPV) of long-term-care premium hikes, and 2023 hikes averaged 51%.
- **Agency turnover is 77-80%, and nearly 4 in 5 caregivers quit within 100 days** (secondary vendor sources: [Teambridge](https://www.teambridge.com/blog/ai-caregiver-matching-software-2026), [AxisCare](https://axiscare.com/blog/addressing-home-care-recruitment-challenges-with-ai/)). Voice-AI screening is claimed to pay back in under 60 days for a 75-caregiver agency (vendor claim).
- **Home health reimbursement is squeezed.** The CY2026 rule is a 1.3% aggregate cut (about $220M), down from a proposed 6.4%, with a -3.0% temporary adjustment still in place ([Home Health Care News](https://homehealthcarenews.com/2025/11/cms-finalizes-2026-home-health-medicare-payment-rule-with-1-3-aggregate-reduction/)).
- **Elder fraud is exploding.** Americans 60+ reported $7.7B in losses in 2025, up 59%. The average loss was over $38k, and 12,400+ people lost $100k+ each. Investment and "pig butchering" fraud is over a third of losses (secondary: [HCSK summary of IC3](https://seniors.hcsk.org/elder-fraud-statistics-research/)).
- **Solo agers lack a decision-maker.** They have no spouse or kids, and many lack healthcare proxies, so the state may appoint a professional guardian ([NPR, Jul 2026](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/09/nx-s1-5886348/solo-agers-demographics-caregiving-family-aging)). 27% of Americans 65+ live alone (Pew, via secondary).

## 3. Where money is flowing, and what is crowded

- **Payer-provider consolidation.** UnitedHealth/Optum closed its $3.3B Amedisys deal after a DOJ settlement that required divesting 164 locations in 19 states ([Fierce](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/payers/unitedhealth-amedisys-sell-facilities-settlement-doj)). It had earlier bought LHC Group for $5.4B.
- **Senior housing investment is accelerating.** Occupancy is near 90% and supply is at historic lows ([Greystone](https://www.greystone.com/insights/senior-housing-market-q1-2026-occupancy-nears-90-supply-hits-historic-lows-investment-activity-accelerates/)).
- **Software is PE-consolidated.** WellSky is backed by TPG, Leonard Green and Thoma Bravo. AxisCare took LLR Partners money. Homecare Homebase is Hearst-owned ([summary](https://www.guardiansmartcare.com/blog/wellsky-competitors/)).
- **AgeTech venture funding is thin and mostly old.**
  - The top 15 elder-care startups have raised only about $515M combined ([Seedtable](https://seedtable.com/best-elderly-care-startups)).
  - The famous names date from 2021 rounds: Honor at $1.25B valuation, and Papa at $1.4B after a $150M Series D ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/papa/__2nJIZX3D6z37kohrgjNw1Eg8jBNxDWXmlCnOWl0KRv8)). Both still have 1,000+ employees.
  - New specialist funds exist: one of $47.3M and an advocacy-backed $40M (secondary).
- **Crowded categories:** fall-detection wearables and medical alert, home care scheduling and CRM software, marketplaces (Care.com, Papa, Honor), senior-living CRM, and generic "AI for home care" scheduling and recruiting.

## 4. Underserved segments and structural gaps

- **Solo agers and surrogate decision-making.** Hospitals hit discharge and consent bottlenecks when a patient has no family. Incumbents (hospitals, agencies) cannot serve as trusted agents because of conflicts of interest.
- **Middle-income squeeze.** They earn too much for Medicaid and cannot afford $75-130k a year. 41 states have HCBS waitlists and 32% of care workers are on Medicaid themselves.
- **Paid family caregiving.** All 50 states have Medicaid self-direction, and many pay relatives ([ACL/NCA summary](https://www.caregiving.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Medicaid-Self-Directed-Care-Services_April-2026.pdf)). Navigation, enrollment and fiscal-intermediary tooling for the family-as-employer model looks thin. Agencies structurally do not want to help families avoid agencies.
- **Senior housing in the gap through 2030.** Nothing new lands before then, so alternatives are structurally favored: aging in place, home modification, and ADUs. Seniors hold a record $14.92T in home equity ([NRMLA](https://www.nrmlaonline.org/about/press-releases/17828)). Proprietary reverse mortgages now take 52% share versus 48% for HECM.
- **Estate settlement.** Probate and estate cases rose about 32% from 2020 to 2024. A $6T transfer is under way in 2026 and Cerulli projects $124T through 2048 (secondary: [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wealth_Transfer)). Heirs are Gen X and millennials who are unprepared executors.
- **Dementia care navigation.** CMS GUIDE closed enrollment after one 2024 application window and runs 8 years ([Alz. Assoc.](https://www.alz.org/help-support/caregiving/financial-legal-planning/medicare-guide-program-for-dementia-care)). Families outside GUIDE participants, and post-diagnosis blood-test patients, have little navigation.

## 5. Weird signals

- **China's "Are You Dead?" check-in app topped paid charts in Jan 2026.** It requires daily check-ins, and two missed check-ins alert an emergency contact. It monetizes a function families used to provide free. China's singles economy passed $1T in 2025, up about 50% in two years ([Asia Times](https://asiatimes.com/2026/09/asias-lonely-economy-of-one/)).
- **Governments are giving away AI companions.** NY, FL and NJ distribute ElliQ free. NYSOFA reported a 95% loneliness reduction after 30+ days (self-reported, 2023) and about 20 interactions a day. The retail price is $40 a month plus $250 ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/03/gen-z-elliq-intuition-robotics-ai-companion-robot-older-adults/), [NYSOFA](https://aging.ny.gov/news/nysofas-rollout-ai-companion-robot-elliq-shows-95-reduction-loneliness)). The buyer is the public agency, not the senior.
- **Americans are exporting eldercare.** Assisted living and memory care in Mexico, the Philippines and Thailand runs $800-3,000 a month versus about $6,300 in the US. Staff ratios of 1:3 to 1:5 are claimed, and Medicare does not work abroad ([RetireFinder](https://retirefinder.com/care-abroad/assisted-living-abroad/), secondary).
- **Wisconsin nursing homes spent $100k+ on apartments for overseas workers**, then abandoned the plan when visa access closed (Wisconsin Watch). This is a stranded-asset signal for offshore or "care worker housing" strategies.
- **A blockbuster failed and a sleeper won.** Semaglutide flopped in Alzheimer's while home-injected lecanemab is heading toward $1B. Diagnosis is decentralizing, so the bottleneck shifts to post-diagnosis coordination and caregiver support.
