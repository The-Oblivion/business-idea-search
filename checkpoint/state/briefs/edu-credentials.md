# Education, credentialing and skills after AI: scout briefing (as of 2026-09-28)

Method note: the WebSearch budget was exhausted (200/200), so this is built from about 60 WebFetch pulls of primary and trade-press pages. Wikipedia-derived figures are marked (W) and are lower confidence. The NSC enrollment figures are Spring 2025 and stale.

## 1. Biggest shifts (last 12-18 months)
- **Entry-level white-collar hiring has cracked.** A Census-affiliated working paper (Sept 22, 2026) finds AI-exposed majors (CS, accounting, journalism, engineering) have 5 points lower initial employment and 13% lower earnings than the pre-ChatGPT cohort. About half the earnings loss comes from grads landing in restaurants and retail. Nursing and education are the least exposed. [IHE](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2026/09/22/ai-created-recession-job-market-some-new-grads)
- Stanford's ADP-payroll study (data through June 2026, revised Aug 2026): employment of 22-25-year-olds in AI-exposed jobs is **19% below trend**, driven by reduced hiring rather than layoffs. The gap has widened steadily since Aug 2025. [Stanford](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publications/canaries-in-the-coal-mine/)
- NY Fed 2026 Q2: recent-grad unemployment is about 5.6% and underemployment is 42%. [NY Fed](https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/college-labor-market)
- **Federal aid regime reset on July 1, 2026.**
  - Grad PLUS is gone. Grad loans are capped at $20.5k/yr and $100k total.
  - Only 11 "professional" programs get $50k/yr and $200k total. Nursing, OT, PT, counseling, psychology and social work were excluded.
  - Parent PLUS is capped, and loans are prorated by credit load.
  - Final guidance landed May 2026 and vendors were still unready in August. Disbursements ran 6 weeks late for some students, with no recourse. [Hechinger](https://hechingerreport.org/new-student-loan-changes/), [Hechinger](https://hechingerreport.org/grad-students-health-care-fields-federal-loan-limits/)
  - Private lenders are tightening ("responsible underwriting"), and predatory loans reach 16% APR.
- **Workforce Pell launched July 1, 2026 but is barely alive.** Only **5 programs were approved** as of Sept 22. The first was a 14-week EMT program at Iowa Central. Ivy Tech's 8-week medical assistant program followed on Aug 21. Colleges call the process "hard and complicated." Registered apprenticeships with college instruction auto-qualify as "in-demand," and up to 49% of instruction can be outsourced to employers or unions (25% for other programs). [IHE](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/government/student-aid-policy/2026/09/22/how-apprenticeships-can-help-colleges-access), [IHE](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2026/08/21/ivy-tech-receives-approval-workforce-pell-program)
- **Federal scholarship tax credit starts in 2027.** It is capped at $1,700 per donor and flows through nonprofit scholarship-granting organizations (SGOs).
  - 31 states have opted in. About 51.7M K-12 kids are eligible at 300% of area median income or below, and about 31M live in opted-in states.
  - Treasury rules are still under White House review. A realistic estimate is $25-50B/yr, against Title I at $18B.
  - 40% of adults haven't heard of it. Homeschool and microschool eligibility depends on state definitions. [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/as-white-house-reviews-new-education-tax-credit-rules-questions-remain/), [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/52-million-u-s-kids-eligible-for-new-scholarships-but-california-hasnt-opted-in/)
- **Demographic cliff meets choice.**
  - K-12 enrollment fell from 50.8M to 49.4M (2019-24) and is projected at 46.9M by 2031.
  - More than 1,000 public schools closed in 2025-26, the fourth straight year of rising closures.
  - Chicago lost 11,500 students this fall and has 135k+ empty seats. Its births fell 37% from 2010 to 2023. [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/fewer-students-too-many-schools-communities-confront-closures-consolidations/), [Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/chicago/2026/09/24/chicago-public-schools-loses-another-11500-students/)
  - Portland is weighing up to 18 closures, Minneapolis up to 16 buildings, and Albuquerque is down from 84k to under 64k students. [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/more-school-closure-decisions-likely-ahead-for-the-2026-27-school-year/831059/)
- **AI backlash in schools.** NYC and LAUSD banned or paused AI for 2026-27 (Sept 2-3). Three in five teachers use AI, yet over half say it harms critical thinking. Four more states now require district AI policies. [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/a-summer-of-ai-bans-in-schools/829862/)

## 2. Acute, quantified pain points
- **AI tutors underdeliver.** A 2-year study across 18 Tennessee middle schools found Khanmigo added no benefit over Khan Academy alone. Struggling students abandoned it when it refused to give answers. [Hechinger](https://hechingerreport.org/proof-points-khanmigo-math-ai-tutor/)
- **Practice looks better while exams get worse.** ALEKS data covering millions of interactions show proctored word-problem accuracy falling from about 80% to about 60% after ChatGPT, while unsupervised practice accuracy rose. Graphing problems, which are hard to paste into AI, were unchanged. (Working paper, June 2026, not peer-reviewed.) [Hechinger](https://hechingerreport.org/proof-points-ai-eroding-math-skills/)
- **Assessment integrity is broken.** At Harvard, 67% of professors say AI hurts their courses (up from 42% a year earlier), and 89% received suspected AI work. Only 12% referred cases to the honor council. [IHE](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/quick-takes/2026/09/17/2-3-harvard-professors-say-ai-has-negative-impact) Detectors are unreliable and biased against non-native writers (W).
- **Loan-gap students.** Tuition crowdfunding on GoFundMe is up 20% y/y, and about 40% of Americans can't qualify for private loans. [IHE](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/students/financial-aid/2026/09/28/gofundmes-college-tuition-are-year)
- **Screen fatigue.** 61% of teachers want less device use, and two-thirds would back paper-based classes (Walton/Gallup, Sept 2026). [K-12 Dive](https://www.k12dive.com/news/1-in-4-elementary-school-teachers-say-students-use-devices-for-main-lessons/830828/)
- **ESA homeschoolers are second-class.** Texas got 200k+ applicants. Private-school kids get $10,474 (up to $30k with disabilities), homeschoolers get $2,000, and spending is limited to a closed marketplace. More than 70% of homeschoolers in non-ESA states say they'd take public funds. [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/exclusive-many-homeschoolers-want-esas-but-texas-awards-more-funds-to-private-school-kids/)

## 3. Where the money is flowing (and what's crowded)
- **Consolidation of the old edtech.**
  - Coursera is buying Udemy for about $930M in equity (combined value $2.5B, Dec 2025). Udemy's consumer segment is shrinking. (W)
  - Chegg's revenue fell to $618M in 2024. It cut 22% of staff in May 2025 and 45% in Oct 2025, and sued Google over AI Overviews. (W)
  - Duolingo did $1.04B revenue and $414M net income in 2025 despite the AI-first backlash. (W)
- **Expertise as labor.** Mercor raised $350M at a $10B valuation (Oct 2025) and manages about 30k experts who train AI models. (W)
- **AI labs building schools.** The a16z-led Horowitz Andreessen Academy has raised $42M, with Anthropic, Google, Meta, Nvidia, Palantir and OpenAI as backers. [IHE](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/tech-innovation/teaching-learning/2026/09/24/silicon-valley-ai-academy-seeks-main-character)
- **Alpha School** has about 50 campuses, and Texas alone grew to 30+ via vouchers. Charter approvals mostly fail, with only Arizona approving. It is a political darling, and Education Secretary McMahon praised it. [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/inside-alpha-schools-bootcamp-heart-monitors-unpaid-labor-many-many-tweets/)
- **Crowded (avoid):** AI tutors, teacher copilots and lesson planners (Khanmigo, Claude for Teachers launched July 2026, Gemini in Classroom Aug 2026), homework helpers, language apps and generic course marketplaces.

## 4. Underserved segments and structural gaps
- **Workforce Pell "approval-as-a-service".** Five approvals about 12 weeks after launch (my inference: a large latent backlog of short programs). Colleges lack capacity to prove employer demand, outcome metrics and governor sign-off. Apprenticeship-linked programs get a fast lane.
- **SGO and ESA back-office for 2027.** 31 states, $25-50B of flow, unresolved rules, and about 1,000 microschools "in limbo." [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/some-microschools-in-limbo-as-they-await-new-federal-tax-credit-rules/) Compliance, vendor marketplaces and microschool-in-a-box are open lanes.
- **Financing gap for excluded grad programs** (nursing, OT, PT, counseling). Incumbent lenders are retreating, and a healthcare-association lawsuit is pending (suspension deadline Dec 2026).
- **Empty school buildings.** Chicago alone has 135k unused seats. Districts are structurally slow to repurpose them, and closures are politically toxic.
- **Human-verified competence.** Universities have no scalable way to prove a student can do the work without AI. Oral exams and handwritten essays are being improvised (W).
- **Trades supply chain.**
  - Electricians: 821k jobs, median pay $63,190, 9% growth, and about 72.7k openings a year, boosted by data-center demand. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/electricians.htm)
  - Plumbers: $63,800 median pay and 42k openings a year. [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/construction-and-extraction/plumbers-pipefitters-and-steamfitters.htm)
  - The entry path is a 4-5 year apprenticeship, so the bottleneck is employer sponsors and pre-apprenticeship screening.
  - Vocational-heavy public two-years grew 11.7% in Spring 2025 (NSC), now 19.4% of public two-year enrollment. [NSC](https://nscresearchcenter.org/current-term-enrollment-estimates/)

## 5. Weird signals
- AI labs are funding an unaccredited, no-grades school for ages 16-22 (about 50 students, fall 2027, free first year, elite-private tuition after that). Critics call it "a money grab."
- Alpha School's marketing budget is about $1,000 per student against about $2,000 for academics. Two-thirds of its "guides" lack teaching credentials, and its 8-week bootcamp uses heart-rate monitors. The state pushback is real: Pennsylvania cited "significant deficiencies."
- The AI-skills labor market is not a CS market. Two-thirds of candidates with AI experience on Handshake aren't CS majors (12.4M profiles). [IHE](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/student-success/college-experience/2026/09/22/5-ways-ai-reshaping-student-success)
- The offshore-wind training bust is a warning about policy-dependent credentials. Programs report renewals exceeding new enrollees. [Hechinger](https://hechingerreport.org/offshore-wind-job-training-built-for-a-boom-is-facing-down-a-bust/)
- Microschool data is thin but interesting. About 400 students in 27 schools, 48% with special needs, and over 50% beat expected reading growth on i-Ready. Weaker results for low-income and Black students in math. [The 74](https://www.the74million.org/article/microschool-students-keep-pace-academically-on-national-test-new-data-shows/)
- Homeschooling is transient. Only about 10% of families do it K-12 exclusively, and 30% do it for 1-2 years. This suggests hybrid and part-time products fit better than full-time ones. (W)
