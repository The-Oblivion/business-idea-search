             Key Takeaways


             ERCOT, in collaboration with stakeholders, is developing requirements for Large Loads
              o   NOGRR 282: Large Computation Load Ride-Through Requirements in Operations
              o   PGRR 144: Dynamic Model Submission & Review Requirements

             The review process helps ensure consistency in model data and performance
              characteristics throughout the interconnection process.

             Ongoing industry engagement is critical to enhancing modeling approaches and
              reliability frameworks
