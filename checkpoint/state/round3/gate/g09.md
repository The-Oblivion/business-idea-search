# G09: Phantom Income (tax pack for sports bettors) (G:pool_wedges:1)

**Verdict: WEAK (leans kill as a venture; fine as a cheap December test).** Gate run 2026-10-25.

## Deciding reason
Three problems stack up. (1) The headline hook, the 90% loss cap, has a real chance of being repealed retroactively. Ways and Means passed the fix 38-5 on Sep 16, 2026 (H.R. 10357, applies to tax years after 12/31/2025), and a prediction market priced restoration by Apr 1, 2027 at about 48%. If it passes, "phantom income" disappears right when he planned to sell. (2) Direct competitors already shipped the exact "import statements, get tax numbers, export for CPA" product. (3) The affected pool is small. The cap only bites itemizers, and the only IRS-derived count I found is about 662k filers claiming gambling losses as itemized deductions (2020 data, mostly casino players, about 0.4% of returns). The expansion to "TurboTax for the attention economy" runs into Keeper, Intuit and funded crypto-tax vendors, so $10M is not credible.

## Competitors found (assume all live)
- BetTax Pro (bettaxpro.com): client-side tool for bettors and prediction-market traders. Imports Kalshi, Pikkit, Juice Reel and generic sportsbook CSV/Excel, tracks W-2G/1099, splits section 165 from section 1256, outputs a CPA report plus Excel. One-time license via Gumroad, price not shown. This is basically the product.
- "Gambling Tax" (Capterra listing): W-2G/1099/statement/IRS-transcript reconciliation into audit-ready workpapers, aimed at accounting firms and gamblers. No reviews, so traction unknown.
- Monaco CPA (NJ): productized CPA service for sports bettors with 2026 phantom-income planning. $350 per individual return listed.
- Keeper (keepertax.com): AI plus CPA filing for 1099 earners, publishes Kalshi/Polymarket guides, 14-day free trial, about $15-18M raised.
- Prediction-market side: CoinTracker (Kalshi API integration, beta), Summ (Polymarket and Kalshi sync), PolyTax Chrome extension, CoinLedger and Koinly guides. Funded crypto-tax vendors are already there.
- Free calculators that already apply the 90% cap: ustax.tools, casinos.com, tax47.app, taxstra.com, nationaltaxtools.com. Plus TurboTax, FreeTaxUSA, TaxSlayer and NerdWallet explainers.
- Adjacent threats: Pikkit and Juice Reel (BookSync, about 300 books) own the data connection and could ship a tax export in one sprint. Column Tax and April are embedded e-file infrastructure with no public pricing, so the embedded-tax-center path needs them or competes with them.

## Key facts and numbers
- The cap is 90% of losses, still limited to winnings, and only itemizers deduct losses at all. Standard-deduction bettors are unaffected, since they already pay tax on gross winnings. Net losers with losses of at least about 1.11x winnings are also unaffected. The hurt group is itemizing break-even or net-winning, high-turnover bettors.
- A $100k-turnover break-even bettor owes tax on $10k of phantom income, which is about $2.2k at 22%. A $5k-turnover bettor owes about $110 or less. Willingness to pay for $29-79 exists only in the high-turnover tail, and those people already use CPAs or forums.
- The IRS wants a contemporaneous log, and win/loss statements alone are often not enough. That is real value, and it survives a cap repeal. The cap-specific urgency does not.
- Prediction-market tax treatment is unsettled (ordinary income vs section 1256 vs gambling). Kalshi's 1099 gives a net number only and Polymarket issues none. That content moat is real but already contested by BetTax Pro, CoinTracker and Summ.
- Sizing (bottom-up, my estimate): about 1-2M US bettors where the tax math matters, 5-15% reachable organically, 2-5% of those paying. Year 1 is about 300-800 buyers at about $45, so $15-40k. A mature niche is about $0.3M a year. Upside is about $3M with prediction markets plus a tracker-app licensing deal. $10M would need about 200k buyers, 10%+ of the whole affected pool.

## Can he reach and close customers?
- Reach is partly real. A free calculator plus SEO and X/Discord content is feasible, but subreddit and Discord self-promotion rules are likely hostile (I could not verify r/sportsbook rules). Winter SEO is crowded with CPAs and calculators.
- His Gen Z network edge is weak for the sportsbook version. Sportsbooks and DFS are 21+ in most states, so his classmates cannot legally be users and cannot be his interviews. The paying sportsbook buyer is a 25-45 year old itemizer. The edge is real for prediction markets (Kalshi is mostly 18+, young crypto-adjacent users).
- Trust is a real blocker for the $149 EA tier and for uploading financial statements. Mitigations are an EA/CPA partner who signs the rules, client-side processing (BetTax Pro already advertises that), and E&O cover.

## Legal and age
- Not a wall. A self-file software tool does not need a PTIN. A paid human review or return preparation needs a credentialed partner (EA/CPA), and he should not touch that part. Add disclaimers and E&O insurance (roughly $500-1.5k a year).
- Selling to 21+ bettors with an age gate is fine. Ad platforms (Google, Meta) restrict gambling-adjacent ads, so expect friction.
- Guardrails: no gambling operations, no targeting minors. The 21+ gate handles it. Not a violation.

## Time and cost to first revenue
About $1.5k (CPA/EA logic review, domain and hosting, ToS, E&O). MVP in 3-4 weeks. First sale about 7 weeks out (mid-December), if a free calculator earns traffic. First sale is plausible but the first-10-from-Reddit plan is shaky.

## Sharpened version (keeps the ambition, drops the policy-dependent hook)
**Wager Ledger**: the audit-proof record and tax-treatment engine for people whose income sits across sportsbooks, DFS and prediction markets. Lead with prediction-market traders (18+, where his Gen Z reach works), not sportsbook bettors.
- Free calculator as top of funnel. It models all outcomes: cap stays, cap repealed, and itemize-or-not. This makes repeal a feature, not a death sentence.
- Paid year-end pack (about $39-79): session-level contemporaneous log, multi-venue reconciliation, state rules (gross-winnings states vs netting states), and a side-by-side of treatment options for prediction-market profits, with partner-EA review as the upsell.
- Scale path: the normalized wager and position ledger across venues is the asset. License the engine to bet trackers and prediction-market front-ends (Pikkit, Juice Reel, Kalshi-adjacent apps). Still has no credible $10M+ path until that B2B channel is proven, and an 18-year-old will struggle to land it cold.

## Bottom line
Cheap, legal and fast enough to test in December, so not dead. But it fails "path to big" and "not crowded", and its main hook may be repealed before April. Do not advance as a top-tier idea. If he wants a tax-season side project, run it as a 6-week test with a hard go/no-go on 10 paid users by Jan 15.
