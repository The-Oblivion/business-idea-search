# g27 · S:I0355 · Renewal-Match Agent for Concession-Market Renters

**Verdict: WEAK** (not kill). Late Oct 2026. ~22 searches plus page fetches.

## Deciding reason
The letter-plus-comps layer is already free. CounterWise, FareRent, Apartment List's Rate My Rent and plain ChatGPT all do it. The only differentiated piece is pay-on-win execution. That is a $150-300-per-win business with savings-verification leakage and a concession tailwind that fades through 2027-28. The founder's own Gen Z circle (classmates, applicants) is not renewing apartment leases, so the "native channel" is weaker than the idea claims. It is not dead: no US pay-on-savings rent product turned up, it needs about $1.5k, and the model scales elsewhere (Ownwell).

## What holds up
- **Concessions are real and large.** Zillow, June 2026: 39.7% of US listings had a concession, Austin 64.3%, Phoenix 61.0%, Nashville 64.0%. RealPage, March 2026: Austin 36.2% of stabilized units.
- **Renewal gap is real.** Renewals are up about 3.5% (RealPage); Equity Residential 5.2%, MAA 5.2%. New-lease rates were -0.7% and -5.3%. Concessions go to new signers, and renewal increases are computed off gross rent.
- **Most people don't ask.** Apartments.com 2026: 47% never negotiate; of those who try, 32% succeed. Avail 2023: about 28-34% try, about 25% win.
- **Landlords have a reason to bend.** Turnover costs about $4,000 a unit; 56% of expiring leases renew (RealPage H1 2026).
- **Gen Z is a large renter group.** 25% of renters and 47% of recent renters (WSJ/Zillow, May 2025). The audience exists, but it is 22-29 year olds, not high-school classmates.

## What breaks it
1. **Commodity core.** Free tools cover the letter and comps. Paying only makes sense if the product acts or reliably produces wins.
2. **Thin per-win money.** A $50-100/mo win is $600-1,200 a year. At 25% that is $150-300. The idea's own $10M needs 40-60K verified wins a year. At about 30% win rate that means about 150K engaged users a year, which is heroic for a one-person consumer funnel.
3. **Verification and collection.** The renter negotiates and signs the lease alone, so you must catch under-reporting. Fix: bank-link rent debits, or a lease upload with a card on file. Rocket Money's fee disputes show the churn and chargeback risk.
4. **Fading why-now.** Yardi has supply bottoming in 2027 (about 444K units) and Texas deliveries falling from 93K to 40K by summer 2027. Owners expect to cut concessions. The big summer 2027 renewal wave arrives as leverage shrinks.
5. **Landlord discretion.** Large operators run system-set renewal offers with small on-site latitude. Reddit anecdotes are mixed (a 2% increase cut to 1%; some got $150-250 off).
6. **License gray zone.** Texas defines "broker" to include negotiating a lease for compensation, and TREC says owner-paid rental locators need a license (tenant-paid is not addressed). California licenses leasing and soliciting tenants for others for pay. Florida's exemptions are narrow. The "renter sends the letter" design is the safe path; an agent that contacts the landlord for a contingent fee is the risky one.
7. **Comp-data terms.** Use a licensed API (RentCast about $74-199/mo on third-party listings; HelloData per record). Do not scrape Zillow.

## Numbers
- First paying customer: about 8-10 weeks. Build 2 weeks; needs a renter with a lease ending Jan-Mar 2027; the fee is collected after the signed renewal.
- Cost to first revenue: about $1.5k (LLC, data API, SMS, one hour of lawyer time, domain).
- Median case (year 3): about $0.2M a year, roughly 800 wins at $200-250.
- Upside: about $8M a year, about 25-30K wins plus a licensed move/referral leg, or a distribution partner (rent-payment, credit or bill apps).

## Teen blockers
- **Age:** not real. He is an adult; LLC, contracts and Stripe are fine.
- **Credibility:** not real for consumers. Not needed for landlords if the renter sends the message.
- **License:** real but avoidable. Self-serve design avoids it. A Florida sales associate license (age 18, 63-hour course, about $300-500, 1-3 months, needs a sponsoring broker) would unlock the "move you if they refuse" leg legally.
- **Capital:** not real.
- **Audience mismatch (real):** classmates and applicants don't hold renewable leases.
