# g23 - S:I1337 Restock Monitors Turned Competitor Sell-Through Radar

**Verdict: WEAK (near kill).** Quick revenue is real, but the big half is already commoditized and the data has a hole.

## What I found (about 20 searches, late Oct 2026)

**Direct competitors on the B2B half (DTC brands)**
- Particl (particl.com): SKU-level sales, pricing and inventory intel; claims 600M+ SKUs, 10,000+ customers (Gymshark, Stanley, Vuori, Mejuri); third-party listings put plans at $250 / $500 / $1,000 a month. Raised about $13.5M (seed + $8.5M Series A). Growjo (a modeled estimate) puts revenue near $5.5M with 43 staff. That is the category leader, about 7 years in.
- The data layer is a commodity. Apify actors detect variant-level sold-out / restock events from Shopify's public feed at roughly $0.56 to $20 per 1,000 product checks (Shopify Sales Tracker, StoreSignal, Fourwake and others). Koala Inspector is about $22 a month; Trendtrack about $59-149; EcomScout Signals (Shopify app, launched May 2026) estimates daily sales from inventory changes and has a free plan; Store Leads $75-950.
- Funded entrants keep arriving (ShopVision, $4.1M seed, Sep 2025).

**Direct competitors on the prosumer half (resellers)**
- The Hive Index lists 18 paid reselling communities (FlipFlip 50K Discord members, Deal Soldier 43K, Sneaker Squad 31K, plus Secured, NSB and others). Typical price is $25-100 a month.
- Best proof of the ceiling: ShockedIO (Fortune, Jul 2026). Founder is 24, $100 a month, about 850 paying members (peak 1,500), about $4.4M total since 2020 (roughly $1M a year), 30-50 staff, and the article calls it a legal gray area.
- BrickSeek covers consumer inventory lookups at about $10 a month.

**The data hole.** Shopify has stripped `inventory_quantity` from public product data since Dec 2017. Public data gives only in/out of stock per size. The old add-to-cart probe is neutered by purchase caps. So "inventory-by-size time series" is really a sold-out/restock event stream, which the Apify actors already output. Real unit estimates are modeled (Particl's approach), not observed.

**Legal / age**
- He is 18, so the LLC, Discord monetization (18+ required) and Whop are all fine. No license wall.
- Logged-out scraping of public data is defensible (hiQ v. LinkedIn on the CFAA; Meta v. Bright Data, N.D. Cal. Jan 2024, a district ruling). But Shopify's terms and store robots.txt reportedly bar automated access (I could not confirm the live wording), so contract risk exists, and it grows when you resell the data.
- Funds treat web-scraped data as the riskiest alt-data category; the SEC expects written vendor diligence and provenance. A one-person teen vendor with scraped data will not clear this. YipitData (about $260M ARR est., 650+ funds) took 15 years to get there.
- Reseller audiences include 16-17 year olds. Needs an 18+ purchase gate. Manageable, but it touches the minors guardrail.

## Numbers
- First revenue: about 4 weeks, about $800 (proxies, hosting, LLC, Whop/Discord fees).
- Bottom-up: 300-600 paying resellers at $25 = $90-180k a year; 40-100 DTC founders at about $250 a month = $120-300k a year. Median about $0.3M a year by year 3. Upside about $4M (a Particl-class outcome). A $10M+ path is not visible.
- Ops load: 24/7 monitors plus Discord support do not fit 10-15 hours a week; ShockedIO needs 30-50 staff.

## Why weak
1. Criterion 6 fails: the B2B product exists at every price point from $1/1,000 checks to $1,000 a month.
2. Criterion 5 fails: the best comparable on each side is about $1M (prosumer) and about $5M (B2B), with heavy staffing.
3. The investor/acquirer leg needs a compliance moat he cannot build from scraped public data, and acquirers diligence from the seller's own Shopify admin, not a third-party scrape.

## Sharpened version ("Drop Tape")
Keep the monitor Discord only as the acquisition channel. The asset is not scrapes but a consented panel: opted-in members connect their own receipts, checkout outcomes and resale sales. That yields real size-level sell-through and resale-premium data with clean provenance (the Yipit/Earnest receipt-panel model), which scrapers cannot copy and which passes fund compliance. Sell it to hype-brand founders at $199-799 a month and later to acquirers and funds. Gate test in 60 days: 100 paying members at $15-25 and 10 brand founders paying $199 for "time-to-sell-out and resale premium by size." If either misses, kill it.
