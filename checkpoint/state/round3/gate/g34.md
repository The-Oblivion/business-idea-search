# g34: Launch Gate (security pre-flight for AI-built apps)

Key: G:pattern_transfer:0. Gated 2026-10-25. **Verdict: KILL** (already done, and the market has been measured and is tiny).

## The one-paragraph answer

The original idea says "the only dedicated competitor I found is a free tool still listed as launching soon." That is wrong. There are at least eight live URL-based scanners with the identical pitch (paste a URL, get plain-English findings, copy-paste fix prompt per builder). Two of them have public Stripe-verified revenue, and it is about $800 MRR each after 8 to 10 months. On top of that, the builders have since bundled native scanning plus a $100 pentest with an "audit-ready" report, which removes the premise for both the $29 fix pack and the $499 letter. Asher could get a first payer in 4 to 6 weeks, but the ceiling observed in this exact market is roughly $10k a year, not $10M.

## Numbers

- Best Stripe-verified comps: about $800 MRR (roughly $10k a year) after 8 to 10 months, 33 payers each, ARPU about $25 a month.
- CheckVibe converts about 0.5% of registered users to paid (33 of ~6,500).
- $10M ARR at $25 a month needs about 33,000 payers, which at 0.5% conversion means about 6.5M registered users. Vibe App Scanner gets about 1,600 visitors a month. This is not reachable by bottoms-up scanner marketing.
- The idea's own bottom-up (20k agencies at $200 a month, 150k founders at $29 a month) is unsourced, and the one real data point on agency monitoring ($99 Max tier) shows no evidence of volume.
- Market context is real but does not help: Lovable is at roughly $600M ARR and $13.3B valuation (Aug 2026), but the builders are absorbing security into the product (Aikido, Wiz, Semgrep partners), which is exactly the "builders improve their native scans" risk the idea named as its honest weakness. It has already happened.

## Legal and age

- **Age: not a legal blocker.** At 18 Asher can form an LLC, contract and take payment.
- **Credibility: a real blocker for the $499 letter.** A "customer-ready security attestation" signed by an 18-year-old with no credentials, no E&O insurance and no audit firm behind it will not satisfy a procurement team, and Lovable now sells an "audit-ready" Aikido report for $100. Issuing a letter that says an app is secure, and then having that app breached, is also real liability exposure (false assurance).
- **Scanning: grey area, manageable.** Reading public JavaScript is fine. Active probing is a CFAA grey area, and a DNS TXT record proves domain control but is a practical gate, not a legal safe harbor (no case law found treating it as authorization). Competitors mostly use a checkbox. The design in the idea (TXT or meta tag for active tests) is the right one, but it adds friction that the checkbox competitors do not have.
- Not a regulated activity. No license wall.

## Can Asher reach and close customers?

Yes for the $5 to $29 tier: r/lovable, r/vibecoding, r/replit, builders' Discords, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, X, plus Upwork and Contra freelancers who ship client apps on Lovable. Competitors prove that dozens of payers come from SEO and community posts. No for the $499 letter and the $99 to $249 agency plan: no evidence of volume, and the buyers who care get the letter from Lovable for $100 or from a CREST firm (startup pentests run $1,500 to $2,500, Vanta or Drata $6.5k to $10k a year).

## Scores against the brief

1. First paying customer in 60 to 90 days under $10k: YES (about $500, 4 to 6 weeks).
2. Buyers who would buy from an 18-year-old: YES at $5 to $29, NO for attestation.
3. No license wall: mostly yes, liability caveat on the letter.
4. Uses his edges: yes (builds fast, Gen Z distribution), but the edge is not scarce; the competitors are also fast solo builders.
5. Path to $10M+ a year: NO. Observed ceiling is about $10k ARR per scanner.
6. Not crowded: FAILS. This is the most crowded niche of vibe-coding tooling.

## Deciding reason

Crowded, with measured revenue ceilings around $10k a year, and the builders have bundled the paid part (live pentest plus audit-ready report) natively for $100.

## Sharpened version (only if he insists on touching it)

Do not build a tenth scanner. The only pieces that survive are (1) a builder-neutral, published measurement of vibe-coded app security with real false-positive rates (his AI-detector habit), as a content and credibility asset, and (2) a trust attestation that a licensed third party actually stands behind (white-label a CREST-aligned pentest firm at about $1.5k to $2.5k, resell at a markup to founders facing a security questionnaire), queried through an API by marketplaces, payment platforms and procurement. Gate: if he cannot get 30 paying subscribers at $19 or more within 60 days from a free-scan-plus-benchmark launch, stop. Otherwise this is a side hustle that tops out around $10k to $100k a year, not a $10M business.
