# G19 gate: G:creator_media:7 Player-Owned Gameplay Data Co-op

**Verdict: KILL (as written).** Checked late Oct 2026, about 22 searches and fetches.

## Deciding reason
The idea's claimed gap ("labs need a clean, consented source, and gamers aren't paid") is already filled from both sides by funded players. The two features it adds, "rights retained and revocable" and "gamers own the data", are the opposite of what buyers pay for. Buyers pay for scale and publisher-cleared, irrevocable rights.

## Can Asher reach and close the first customer?
- **Supply is not the scarce input.** Mercor pays $11-14/hr and fills gamer roles from job postings. Asher's gamer network is worth little here.
- **The "$10-25k pilot" is a guess with no evidence.** No source shows any buyer paying a sub-1,000-hour vendor. Twenty friends at a few hours a week is about 100-300 hours a month. The buyers' scale is 10,000 to 1,000,000 hours.
- **Buyers shop around.** Labs reportedly run 7+ vendors on the same data type and buy by purchase order on accepted deliveries. Offshore teams undercut on price.
- **Rights are the real gate.** Origin Lab and Datoric both sell "rights-cleared, chain-of-custody." A player cannot license the publisher's art, audio or code. A teen LLC cannot offer indemnity or provenance paperwork either. Third-party summaries say Epic's terms and the Unreal EULA bar AI training on game content. I did not read the primary texts.
- **Revocable rights break the product.** A model trained on data the contributor can later revoke is a legal liability for the lab.
- **Anti-cheat.** The titles with the most data value (Valorant, CS2, battle royales) run kernel-level anti-cheat. Input loggers risk flags and bans, and no policy I found clears it. Non-anti-cheat titles are mostly the ones publishers are already licensing exclusively.
- **Privacy.** Screen plus keystroke capture picks up Discord, notifications and passwords. It needs consent, redaction and 1099s. That is a manageable cost, but the liability sits on his LLC.

## Teen blockers
- Age is **not** a legal wall. He can form an LLC and sign contracts. Contributors must be 18+, which cuts many classmates.
- **Credibility is real.** Lab procurement wants provenance, security review and indemnity, and a teen LLC cannot supply them.
- No licence is needed. Capital under $10k is fine.

## Numbers
- Cost to a first pilot: about $8,000. Contributor pay, a legal review, hosting.
- Time to a first dollar: 14-16 weeks, at maybe 10-15% odds.
- Median case: about $20k a year. Upside: about $5M a year, as a niche vendor with a few lab contracts. That is far below the "hundreds of millions" category talk.

## Sharpened version (a hypothesis, not a rescue)
**First-party rights-clean demonstration data.** Build and own the interactive environments, so there is no publisher chain of rights and no anti-cheat. Log inputs, state and rewards natively in-engine, which is exactly what Datoric and Worldmodeldata advertise as valuable. Pay verified-adult players, and license the data irrevocably for training. The pilot is one environment and about 300 hours, priced like the roughly $20k "environment" units that labs reportedly buy in the RL-environment market (Epoch AI, secondhand).

Before any build, take two weeks of calls with 10-15 buyers. Include data buyers at the vendors above, who are reachable startup founders. Ask whether first-party environments with human trajectories are a line item. I have not searched that market, and it is probably crowded too.
