# G03: LiveLedger (Whatnot seller P&L, cost basis, Schedule C)

**Verdict: WEAK.** First revenue is fast and cheap, but the idea already exists, the price is above the market, and the Whatnot-only ceiling is about $1-3M ARR. It is a good 45-day experiment, not a $10M company as pitched.

## What I found

**Direct competitors (the pitch's "Selldeck + spreadsheets" list is badly incomplete)**
- **Selldeck (selldeck.io).** Same product. Whatnot/eBay/Depop sync; cost basis, SKUs, per-show analytics, P&L reports, IRS Schedule C reports and state-level tax breakdown. Pricing: Free, $8.99, $14.99 (P&L), $24.99 (Schedule C). Its Chrome extension has only 28 users (updated Apr 2026), so it is tiny but shipped and moving.
- **Vendoo.** Integrated with Whatnot since spring 2024 (live selling, sale detection, bulk import); profit and revenue analytics across channels; plans roughly $9-70/month.
- **Closo.** Has a "Finances" dashboard (revenue, COGS, profitability by marketplace). Free core, Pro $29, Unlimited $59.
- **Whatnot-targeted tax and bookkeeping:** Keeper (Whatnot landing page, "48% off for Whatnot", about $16/month), Jupid (AI accountant, Whatnot payouts as income, quarterly estimates), QuickBooks Self-Employed $15, Hurdlr $10, Wave free, Bench $249+.
- **Chrome extensions:** Whatnot Seller Analytics (free profit calculator, per-show reports, paid Pro coming; 48 users), Whatnot Seller Tools (break P&L to Excel), Live Boost (buyer CRM, 56 users), ResellBot Whatnot Spy.
- **Etsy Google Sheet trackers** for breakers (show-by-show profit after fees).
- **Whatnot itself.** Since June 2024, private SKU and COGS fields in inventory ("track your cost basis"); Show Stats and basic Seller Analytics. Native finance tooling is partly already shipped.
- **Cross-platform "nobody owns the ledger" is false for TikTok Shop and eBay.** A2X ($29+), Link My Books (~$21+), Synder ($52-65+) and Webgility ($29+) already reconcile TikTok Shop and eBay payouts into accounting software. The open gap is live-show unit economics and break cost allocation, not settlement accounting.

**Pricing reality.** Every Whatnot-relevant tool sits at $9-25/month (Keeper $16, QBSE $15, Selldeck top tier $24.99). The pitched $29-49 plus $79 tax pack is above all of them, and Selldeck already bundles Schedule C at $24.99. Only Bench ($249+) and CPAs ($500-2,000/month, per one vendor estimate) are priced higher, and they sell done-for-you.

**Size.** Whatnot does not disclose a seller count. Known: $8B+ live GMV in 2025, H1 2026 sales exceeded all of 2025, 1 in 8 sellers full time, sellers going live 3-4x/week average over $13,000/month (Whatnot's claim). My rough bottom-up: 2026 GMV of ~$16B divided by $156k/year per "serious" seller gives roughly 50-100k serious seller-equivalents globally, perhaps 30-60k in the US. At $20/month, 10% penetration of 60k is about 6,000 payers, or $1.4M ARR. The pitch's $10M at $39 needs 21k payers: 35-70% of the serious US cohort. That is the "heroic share" case. Growing past it means leaving the Whatnot wedge for general resellers, where Vendoo, Flyp, List Perfectly and Closo are entrenched.

**Traction signal.** Every Whatnot-specific tool found has 12-56 Chrome Web Store users after months. Either the niche is unclaimed or sellers do not search for or pay for this. Both imply outbound sales, not organic discovery.

**Legal and platform risk (real)**
- Whatnot's Seller API is "Developer Preview" and not accepting new applicants (developers.whatnot.com, per search summary; direct fetch returned 404). The general terms restrict automated use or scraping (clause last updated May 2026, per a third-party tracker). A Chrome extension reading the seller's dashboard is a gray area; Selldeck and others do it, but Whatnot can block it or send a cease-and-desist. I could not confirm that Whatnot offers a clean payout/transaction CSV export. I did not verify the primary ToS text.
- Breaks: March 2026, 15 arbitration complaints argue randomized breaks and repacks are illegal lotteries under California law. No regulator action found. The accounting-only product is clean, but the breaker segment (the highest-pain cohort) carries platform risk.
- Tax: software that computes Schedule C and quarterly estimates is TurboTax-style, not licensed advice, if it avoids "advice" framing. The pitch itself says tax logic needs CPA sign-off (about $500-1,500 one-time).
- Age: not a blocker. He is 18, can contract and take payments, and Whatnot sellers skew 18-35. Credibility with card sellers is a soft blocker: he is not one, so he needs a seller friend or co-founder who goes live.

## Can he reach and close the first customers?
Yes for the first 5-10, at a modest price. A free break-even spot-price calculator plus DMs to sellers who go live nightly is cheap and testable, and Gen Z collectibles reach is real. The 45-day kill test (under 10 payers) is sensible. First ten: full-time breakers going live 4+/week, Pokémon/TCG sellers, sneaker and streetwear live sellers, comics/coins/Funko sellers, card-shop owners who started streaming, break teams with employees, micro-creators in card breaking, card-show vendors, thrift and apparel live sellers, and bookkeepers who serve resellers. Expect Reddit and Discord self-promo friction and a $29 price that gets compared to Selldeck's free tier.

## Numbers
- Cost to first revenue: ~$1,000-1,500 (domain, hosting, AI credits, one or two micro-creators; CPA review deferred)
- Weeks to first revenue: 4-6 (concierge first, product second)
- Median case: ~$0.3M ARR (about 1,500 payers at ~$20/month)
- Upside case: ~$4M (sharpened version, or acquisition by Vendoo/Whatnot-adjacent player)

## Sharpened version (no shrinking)
**LiveBooks: the month-end close for full-time live sellers** (sellers doing $10k+/month, the cohort Whatnot says is doubling).
- Move up, not down. Price to the real anchors (Bench $249+, CPA $500-2,000/month): $29 self-serve entry, $99-149 "we reconcile every payout" tier. $10M ARR becomes about 4,200 payers at ~$200/month, not 21k at $39.
- The product is the reconciled close: every payout tied to a show and break, box-cost allocation across spots, FIFO or specific-ID COGS, 1099-K gross-to-net bridge, sales-tax-on-fees flags, quarterly estimate, CPA-ready export. Multi-user for break teams with employees.
- Data path: seller-uploaded statements and CSV first. Apply for Whatnot partner API access through the mentor and family network. Treat the extension as optional, not core.
- Moat: opt-in anonymized benchmarks of which show formats and price points make money after true fees. That is the hook feature, the content engine and a data asset.
- Distribution adds bookkeepers and CPAs who serve resellers (revenue share), alongside seller communities.
- Expansion: eBay Live, TikTok Shop Live, Fanatics Live. Payout advances only through a licensed partner, and only after the data exists.
- Test before building: sell 10 full-time sellers a manual, AI-assisted month-end close at $99 within 60 days. If under 5 buy, kill.
