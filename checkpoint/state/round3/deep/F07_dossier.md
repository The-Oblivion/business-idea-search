# F07 Dossier: Hard-Hat Stays, sharpened to "Per Diem Keeper"

Researched 2026-10-25 (about 60 searches and fetches). Tags: [P] primary or company document, [N] news, [M] listing or marketing copy (promotional), [Inf] my inference or arithmetic. Anything I could not open is marked "snippet" or listed under Gaps.

## 1. Bottom line

Demand is real and documented in 2026. The open, worker-side marketplace is also genuinely uncrowded. But the sharpened plan has four structural problems:

1. **The operators it wants to bill are full.** They have no reason to buy leads.
2. **Pay-per-confirmed-move-in is the fee structure most likely to need a real estate license** in Texas and Louisiana.
3. **The host price anchor is $199 a year**, and Furnished Finder hosts in Abilene already advertise to data-center crews.
4. **The honest ceiling is single-digit millions a year.** The only route to "big" runs through contractor-paid group bookings, where he has no credibility and no capital.

Verdict: weak. Run it only as a 4-week demand test (Section 9), not as the flagship. I put the chance of a $10M-a-year outcome below 5% [Inf].

- **Eastern Iowa.** Campgrounds are about 100% full, one has a waitlist of about 20, and a union lineman pays $26 a night. - **Pipeline.** The AIA forecast has data-center spending up 33% in 2026 and 25% in 2027 ([HPAC](https://www.hpac.com/association-solutions/news/55395043/aia-consensus-forecast-revised-downward)) [N]. - **Cyclicality.** Abilene's mayor worried about "empty RV parks all over Abilene in 5 to 6 years" ([KTAB](https://www.bigcountryhomepage.com/news/abilene-news/city-of-abilene-approves-more-rv-housing-in-response-to-ai-data-center-boom/amp)) [N]. ## 4. Prices and willingness to pay

- **Host anchor.** Furnished Finder charges $199 a year per property and $750 for complexes, rising to $1,000 from Oct 27 2026 ([Hostfully](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/furnished-finder-fees/)). It has 300k+ listings and 240k+ landlords ([Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com/remote-company/furnished-finder)). One unverified estimate puts revenue at $18.3M ([Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/furnished-finder)). The $500-1,500 a year "verified listing" is 2.5-7.5 times the anchor [Inf]. Host satisfaction is mixed: Trustpilot shows 1.5/5 across 1,294 reviews, including a "never received one lead" complaint (search snippet), so the anchor is not proof that hosts love it.
- **Per-move-in benchmark.** $50-100 against a first month of $780 (Iowa campground), $550-1,000 (Louisiana lots) or $1,550 (planned Frontier cottages) is about 3-13% [Inf]. That is in line with referral norms: LodgeLink nets 12.4%; hotel intermediaries take 10-30% ([AltoVita](https://www.altovita.com/blog/how-commissions-and-referral-fees-impact-your-corporate-accommodation-budget), promotional). Landing and Blueground reportedly take about 7-8% ([Hostfully list](https://www.hostfully.com/blog/best-corporate-housing-companies/), snippet only).
- **Traveler budget.** Data-center per diems appear at $115 (Fargo), $125 (Salt Lake City) and $175 (Des Moines) ([Energy Job Shop](https://www.energyjobshop.com/journeyperson-electrician-jobs-in-idaho-us/?page=7), [listing](https://www.energyjobshop.com/electrician-jobs-in-illinois-us/?page=9)) [M]. The FY27 GSA standard is $113 lodging plus $68 meals ([GSA](https://www.gsa.gov/system/files/FY2027perdiemhighlights.docx)). [Inf] Travelers are price-hunters who pocket the difference. They will not pay; the free side is correct.
- **Crew-desk fee ($50-150 per bed-month).** I found no price evidence anywhere. Hard Hat Housing and ComfyWorkers publish no prices.

## 5. First customers: where they gather, and who actually has a budget

- **Who can sign.** Owner-operators of RV parks and motels, small landlords such as Parish Line Properties (14 of 18 homes now serve the workforce) ([remio](https://www.remio.ai/post/meta-data-center-housing-is-booming-but-rural-louisiana-faces-a-stress-test)), and developers of new parks.
- **The fatal mismatch.** Full parks do not buy leads (Iowa 100% full, waitlist of 20). A Tye, TX park reportedly filled in 13 days ([LoopNet](https://www.loopnet.ca/Listing/212-Air-Base-Rd-Tye-TX/41825506/), sales brochure [M]). The operators who need fill are new supply, such as Elmdale RV Park (2,313 spaces; Taylor County approved the plan in September 2025) ([Modern Campground](https://moderncampground.com/usa/texas/final-infrastructure-plan-cleared-for-elmdale-rv-park-in-taylor-county/)). They are developer-run, and their lease-up status is unknown. Existing operators will only need leads once a build winds down, which is also when worker demand disappears [Inf].
- **Chains.** Extended-stay chains already run corporate sales ([WoodSpring](https://woodspring.com/blog/construction-workers/a-guide-to-managing-construction-crew-temporary-housing)). That leaves independents.
- **Traveler reach.** Postings sit on [Energy Job Shop](https://www.energyjobshop.com/per-diem-loa-electrician-jobs-in-united-states/?page=6), [TradesmenUp](https://www.tradesmenup.com) and staffing apps like TradeGig ([Tradesmen International](https://jobs-tradesmen.icims.com/jobs/355257/traveling-electrician/job)). I found no advertiser or partner program on any of them. I found no trades-housing Facebook group, and generic housing groups ban commercial promotion (e.g. [RPCV rules](https://rpcvw.org/rules), a generic example).
- **Mentor.** I found no evidence that AV-integration relationships reach electrical subcontractors [Inf].

## 6. Legal, age and platform issues

- **Age and entity.** No wall: he can form an LLC and sign contracts at 18 [Inf]. The gate's own analysis agrees.
- **Licensing is the live risk.** Under [Tex. Occ. Code 1101.002](https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._occ._code_section_1101.002), a broker includes anyone who, for compensation, "lists or offers to list" property for lease, "aids … in locating or obtaining real estate for … lease", or "procures … a prospect". TREC confirms that apartment locators need a broker or sales license "if the person seeks or has an expectation of compensation" ([TREC](https://www.trec.texas.gov/node/373)). The [exemptions list](https://texas.public.law/statutes/tex._occ._code_section_1101.005) covers owners, their employees and on-site apartment managers, with no publisher or advertiser exemption. Louisiana's definition covers "lists … for lease" and "advertises … renting or leasing", and leaseholds count as real estate ([R.S. 37:1431](https://www.legis.la.gov/Legis/Law.aspx?d=93260)). Only a narrow advance-fee publication carve-out exists.
- **Case law.** Flat-fee advertising sites won in California and New Hampshire ([Olshan](https://www.olshanlaw.com/Real-Estate-Law-Blog/Real-Estate-Websites-Broker-Regulation), [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2008/04/02/judge-rules-fsbo-company-can-operate-without-real-estate-license/); older, not TX/LA). [Inf] A flat annual listing is the defensible structure. A per-confirmed-move-in fee looks like brokerage. Whether RV pads and motel stays count is untested. Get a Texas/Louisiana attorney opinion or a licensed-broker partner before charging.
- **Lodging tax.** Texas treats a guest as a permanent resident, exempt from hotel tax, after 30 consecutive days ([Comptroller](https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/hotel/faq.php)). Flat fees, with no rent handling, keep him out of collecting it.
- **Tax-adjacent positioning.** The IRS per diem revenue procedure does not provide rules for substantiating lodging alone ([Rev. Proc. 2002-63](https://www.eitc.irs.gov/pub/irs-drop/rp-02-63.pdf)). "Keep more of your per diem" drifts toward tax advice. Market price comparison instead.
- **Local supply caps.** Richland's RV moratorium and Abilene's reluctance cap new supply. That raises incumbents' rents but lowers their need for leads.

## 7. Unit economics and ceiling

All numbers below are my arithmetic, with assumptions stated.

- **Listing model.** Assume 30-60 bulk operators per hot town ("several dozen" in Richland). At $500-1,500 a year, 100% penetration gives $15K-90K per town. At 20% penetration it gives $3K-18K per town. Across 40 towns the 20% case is about $0.1-0.7M a year.
- **Move-in model.** Assume 6,000-9,000 workers per site, 25% self-arranging, and a six-month stay. That is 3,000-4,500 move-ins a year per town. At 10% capture and $50-100 each, it yields $15K-45K a year per town.
- **Ceiling.** Even at heroic share, about $1-4M. That matches the gate's $0.5M median. Furnished Finder is roughly $18M after 12 years (unverified). LodgeLink nets about $18-22M a year after years of investment (annualized from H1 2026, my arithmetic).
- **Cost to first revenue.** $1,500 is plausible, since it is a site plus calls and ads. No capital goes to float if he stays a publisher.
- **Time.** Nine weeks to a first $500 listing is plausible. A paying operator who is not already full is the bottleneck.

## 8. What kills it

1. Full operators will not pay; new mega-parks are developer-run and have their own lease-up channels.
2. A $199 anchor with eight Abilene Furnished Finder listings already targeting this exact worker.
3. A traveler cold start. No partner program or trades Facebook group was found.
4. Pay-per-move-in may need a license; a flat fee has the lowest legal risk.
5. Each town's demand peaks and fades within 12-36 months.
6. The "big" route (contractor billing with float) requires capital, credibility and insurance that he lacks.
7. He is remote from every build town, with 10-15 hours a week.

## 9. Cheapest honest test (4 weeks, under $500)

1. Build one landing page for Abilene/Clyde: "monthly beds for data-center crews, truck and trailer parking, near the site." Run about $300 of Google search ads on worker queries. Measure cost per qualified text or call.
2. Call 30 independent operators in Abilene, Clyde, Rayville and Cedar Rapids. Ask two questions: "Are you full?" and "Would you pay $75 per confirmed 30-day move-in, or $500 a year, for verified inquiries?"
3. **Kill if:** fewer than 3 operators say yes to a paid pilot by week 4, cost per qualified inquiry exceeds $25, or a Texas/Louisiana attorney says the per-move-in fee needs a license. **Go if:** 3+ operators pre-pay and ads produce more than 20 inquiries a week.
