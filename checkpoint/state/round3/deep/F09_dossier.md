# F09 Dossier: Raise Desk (S:I1113), price-increase autopilot for recurring-route businesses

**Bottom line: the gate's "weak" verdict holds.** Cheap and fast to test, but feature-sized, crowded, and capped. Demand for raising prices is real and rising. Every incumbent is already shipping free tools to serve it. The one real whitespace sits on Jobber, which has the open API and a pending feature request. Jobber could close it itself. Run it only as a 6-week January-season test with hard kill criteria.

Evidence caveats. Jobber's developer docs and Community threads returned HTTP 403 to every fetch. Those facts come from search-result summaries and are marked (search summary). Vendor surveys and blogs are marketing, and I say so where it matters.

## 1. Competitors and free substitutes (ranked by threat)

**Profit-per-stop already exists, mostly bundled.**
- **PoolDial** says it calculates profit per stop automatically from logged time, GPS drive data, chemical costs and billing ([PoolDial](https://pooldial.com/resources/articles/guides/measuring-pool-route-profitability)). It costs $2/pool/month with a $40 minimum and was founded in 2025 ([review page](https://www.pooldial.com/resources/articles/software-reviews/pooldial-review)). It has a Developer API, broadcast email to all customers, a free price-increase calculator, and a free email-gated route scorecard spreadsheet ([scorecard](https://pooldial.com/resources/downloads/route-profitability-scorecard)). I found no scheduled price-change or staged-send feature in PoolDial.
- **Jobber** job costing sits on Grow and Plus per its [pricing page](https://www.getjobber.com/pricing/). Core is $21, Connect $70, Grow $105 and Plus $280 per month on annual billing, one user. Jobber's [job-costing page](https://www.getjobber.com/features/job-costing/) says reports sort by margin across services, crews and clients. A Community reply says Connect also has it (search summary). Jobber's Copilot AI "analyzes historical data on operational efficiency" ([Lawn & Landscape](https://lawnandlandscape.com/news/jobber-copilot-artificial-intelligence-ai)). Housecall Pro sells "Analyst AI" and "Coach AI" ([Stork](https://www.stork.ai/en/housecall-pro-ai-team)). Route cost is still a known blind spot: drive time is rarely logged against the job (Jobber Community, search summary).
- **LMN and Aspire** do landscape job costing. LMN runs about $297 and $697 per month ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/landscape-software/pricing-guide/)). ServiceTitan owns Aspire and FieldRoutes ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/06/30/servicetitan-acquires-aspire-to-move-into-landscaping-raises-200m-at-a-9-5b-valuation), [PMP](https://mypmp.net/?p=72492)).

**Bulk repricing and notices are built in or free.**
- **LawnPro** (claims 40,000+ businesses, unverified) has a percent or flat bulk update with per-record preview, price history, and a "revenue before and after" report ([LawnPro](https://www.lawnprosoftware.com/features/bulk-price-update)).
- **Service Autopilot** has Settings > Bulk Price Update with future-dated changes ([SA](https://www.serviceautopilot.com/software/service-autopilot-tools/)). **FieldRoutes** advertises automated pricing adjustments and customer messaging about price changes ([FieldRoutes](https://fieldroutes.com/blog/how-to-raise-pest-control-prices)). **Briostack** has a bulk price tool (search summary, [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/industries-software/a/briostack/pricing/)).
- **Skimmer** gives away a calculator, email templates and a pricing video ([toolkit](https://www.getskimmer.com/priceincreasetoolkit)). The toolkit page doesn't mention an in-app bulk update.
- **Housecall Pro** publishes free templates and points to bulk email and text ([HCP](https://www.housecallpro.com/resources/price-increase-letter-sample/)). ZenMaid (cleaning) pushes a new price forward per recurring series, not in bulk ([ZenMaid](https://answers.zenmaid.com/en/articles/1767436-how-to-adjust-the-price-of-a-recurring-service)).
- **Adjacent proof the idea is valid and being built elsewhere:** Ignition launched AutoPricing in Feb 2025 for accounting firms: bulk percent increases, segments, and email templates. Its renewal feature averaged 8% increases and $8.7M extra revenue ([CPA Practice Advisor](https://www.cpapracticeadvisor.com/2025/02/18/ignition-debuts-autopricing-to-automate-growth-for-services-businesses/156213/)).

**The only gap I can confirm is on Jobber.** Jobber has no bulk repricing for existing recurring jobs. A cleaning owner with 700 recurring clients has an open feature request, and edits to Products & Services only affect new line items (Community, [thread](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/finances-forum/%F0%9F%92%A1feature-request-price-increases-in-products--services-list/7481), search summary). Support's workaround is export, edit in Excel, re-import. Another Community poster says Jobber shows revenue per job but not effective hourly rate per client once drive time is counted ([thread](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/finances-forum/do-you-know-your-actual-effective-hourly-rate-per-client-%E2%80%94-once-travel-time-is-i/9842), search summary). I found no Marketplace app doing per-client profitability. That whitespace rests on one platform that can ship the feature any quarter.

**Gate corrections.** Skimmer's own release says about 29,000 pros serving 700,000 pools, not 35,000 ([Aqua](https://www.aquamagazine.com/news/community-news/business-briefs/article/15706184/skimmer-inc-skimmer-raises-74m-in-growth-capital-from-mainsail-partners)). Skimmer's $74M raise is confirmed (Oct 2024).

## 2. Willingness to pay and real prices

- **Core software is very cheap in the verticals he'd target.** Skimmer is $1/pool with a $39-49 minimum ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.co.nz/software/113919/skimmer)). PoolDial is $2/pool. Housecall Pro is $59/$149/$299 per month on annual billing ([HCP pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)). Arborgold is about $129 and RealGreen $125-300 ([GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/industries-software/a/arborgold/reviews/?page=2)). A $39-79 add-on roughly equals a small shop's whole software bill. Jobber's implied revenue per customer is only about $1.7k/yr ($167.5M third-party estimate ÷ 100k+ customers; [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/jobber), [Jobber](https://insights.munich-startup.de/news/feed/jobber-hits-100-000-customers-commits-1m-to-grants-and-trade-school-scholarships)).
- **Closest comparable: NiceJob.** Bootstrapped reviews tool for home services at $75-125/mo ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/142037/NiceJob/pricing/)). It hit about $1.5-1.7M ARR with 3,200 customers (2019), then roughly 6,000 customers when Paystone bought it in 2021 ([profile](https://blog.nicejob.co/post/nicejob-featured-on-2019-ready-to-rocket-list-profiling-high-growth-companies-in-b-c-s-technology-sectors), [Latka](https://getlatka.com/companies/nicejob)). That is a good outcome, and it is a low-single-digit $M business. NiceJob's use is continuous. Price increases happen once or twice a year.
- **The value case is real but modest.** Pros report 0-5 lost customers after 8-10% increases (anecdotes aggregated by [PoolDial](https://pooldial.com/resources/articles/business/pool-service-price-increase-guide)). A Jobber Ambassador lost 2 of 400 recurring clients after a 4% increase (Community, search summary). A 10% increase breaks even at 9.1% loss. No independent churn benchmark exists. I found only vendor claims and a 1980 anecdote.
- **I found no evidence anyone pays for a one-time route margin audit at $99-199.** Coaching prices exist at both ends: a $59/mo pool membership ([Pool Guy](https://metacast.app/podcast/the-pool-guy-podcast-show/tiNiVnUX/focus-on-what-makes-money-in-pool-service/5MsTV3iF)) and $1,597/mo for $1M+ owners ([Home Service Boardroom](https://www.skool.com/boardroom/about)). The free PoolDial scorecard and the Skimmer toolkit are the real price anchors. I found no precedent for percent-of-lift pricing in this niche ([search](https://www.whatconverts.com/?p=39696) only showed generic 5-25% revenue-share ranges).

## 3. Who the buyers are and where they gather

- **Segment sizes (different definitions, not additive):** 117,969 employer landscaping establishments, avg 6.8 employees, plus 516,572 nonemployers grossing about $36k each ([Census via LawnStarter](https://www.lawnstarter.com/blog/statistics/lawn-care-landscaping-statistics-2026/)); 78,817 pool-cleaning businesses ([IBISWorld](https://www.ibisworld.com/industry-statistics/number-of-businesses/swimming-pool-cleaning-services-united-states/)); 16,565 pest firms by NPMA or 34,076 by IBISWorld ([NPMA](https://www.npmapestworld.org/your-business/latest-news/us-pest-control-industry-sustains-steady-growth-with-6-increase-in-2025/)); 32,270 maid companies, 67% under $300k ([ISSA](https://www.issa.com/industry-news/17-billion-u-s-residential-maid-services-industry-continues-to-grow/)).
- **The buyers who can pay are not the Gen Z solos.** Solo operators gross about $36k a year. A Skimmer pro averages about 24 pools (700k ÷ 29k), roughly $65k a year at the survey's $225 average rate. The money is in 3-10 crew firms with hundreds of accounts, whose owners are older. I found no data on how many owners are young.
- **Channels:** Jobber Community finance and marketing forums, where annual price increases are a recurring thread ([example](https://community.getjobber.com/discussions/finances-forum/annual-price-increase-reminders/1079)). r/LawnCarePros (5-7k) and r/PoolPros (1-4k) per GummySearch, a closed tracker with inconsistent figures ([page](https://gummysearch.com/r/LawnCarePros)). PoolDial's Pool Nation podcast and Skimmer's Pool Deck community ([Pool Magazine](https://www.poolmagazine.com/news/skimmer-launches-the-pool-deck-a-game-changing-community-exclusively-for-pool-pros/amp/)). Jobber's invite-only Ambassadors and a Partner Program for creators with small-business audiences ([Jobber](https://www.getjobber.com/ambassador/)). I could not measure Facebook group sizes or their self-promotion rules.
- **Timing:** notice runs 30-60 days ([PoolDial](https://pooldial.com/resources/articles/business/pool-service-price-increase-guide), Jobber Community). Today is Oct 25, so January increases need notices out by mid-November. That leaves about three weeks to sell and deliver, which only works as a hand-run concierge service.

## 4. Legal, age and platform issues

- **Age is not a wall.** Majority is 18 in most states. Alabama and Nebraska are 19 and Mississippi is 21 for some purposes ([UpCounsel](https://www.upcounsel.com/legal-contract-age)). I did not verify his state. Can-a-minor-form-an-LLC is moot at 18.
- **Platform access is the real constraint.**
  - **Jobber:** open GraphQL API via OAuth. Draft apps are blocked above 5 paying accounts without approval. Marketplace listing needs review, 2FA and a logo. A single-client app is not listable ([Jobber help](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/developer-center/), [docs](https://developer.getjobber.com/docs/publishing_your_app/app_review_process), search summary). Review timelines are unpublished.
  - **Write-back is unproven.** The `jobEdit` mutation exists. I could not confirm it can change line-item prices on recurring jobs. A third-party CLI notes Jobber's API cannot email invoices ([PR #19](https://github.com/acaracappa/getjobber-cli/pull/19)). Jobber's help center says line items can be applied to future visits in-app ([Jobber help](https://help.getjobber.com/hc/en-us/articles/7924045219479-Visits)).
  - **Housecall Pro:** open API and recurring service plans are MAX-only at $299/mo annual ([HCP pricing](https://www.housecallpro.com/pricing/)).
  - **Skimmer:** public API is on the "Owning the Market" tier, sales-led, aimed at large companies with in-house tech ([Skimmer](https://www.getskimmer.com/blog/skimmer-announces-public-api-to-support-custom-enterprise-workflows)).
  - **GorillaDesk:** API is a paid Pro add-on ([help](https://intercom.help/gorilladesk/en/articles/16530507-gorilladesk-api)). I found no public Service Autopilot API.
  - **Where LawnPro, Briostack and FieldRoutes already bulk-update natively, write-back adds nothing.** The only platform needing it is Jobber.
- **Sending notices.** `gmail.send` is a Sensitive scope, so OAuth verification applies but not the annual CASA assessment. `gmail.compose`, `readonly` and `modify` are Restricted ([Nylas](https://developer.nylas.com/docs/cookbook/use-cases/build/google-oauth-scopes/)). Cost estimates for CASA conflict ($540 for one founder up to $15-75k). Email first. Text messaging carries TCPA exposure, and I found no authority classifying price-change texts ([KJK](https://kjk.com/2018/03/08/automated-text-reminder-pitfalls-get-written-consent/)).
- **Price-change notice rules vary.** New York GBL 527-a bars a price increase without affirmative consent or a 14-day cancel-with-refund right, with 5-30 days' notice ([Kelley Drye](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/ad-law-access/ny-quietly-amends-automatic-renewal-law)). Connecticut added disclosure rules in 2023 ([Orrick](https://infobytes.orrick.com/2023-07-07/connecticut-implements-measures-auto-renewals/)). Georgia bills are pending. Whether month-to-month lawn service counts is unclear. Ship a check-your-agreement line, no contract advice. I found no authority on a non-professional taking a percent-of-lift fee, which looks low-risk.

## 5. Unit economics (my arithmetic; assumptions marked)

- **Average pool pro:** 24 pools × $225 × 12 ≈ $65k. An 8% lift is about $5.2k, so a 10-15% success fee is $520-780. Too small to chase by hand.
- **100-pool route:** $192k at $160/mo (gate) or $270k at $225/mo. An 8% lift is $15.4-21.6k, and a 10-15% fee is $1.5-3.2k. That exceeds the owner's full year of Skimmer or PoolDial fees ($1.2-2.4k).
- **Subscription path to $10M ARR:** 17,000 customers at $49/mo or 10,500 at $79/mo. Against a ceiling of about 245-263k firms in the four verticals (sum of the counts above), that is 4-7% of all firms. Real software-using firms with 50+ recurring accounts are a much smaller base, and I could not size it. A 15% FSM adoption figure from a dated Software Advice survey ([Fourlane](https://www.fourlane.com/blog/trends-field-service-operations/)) and 78% for pest ([PCT/FieldRoutes](https://www.pctonline.com/article/not-just-an-advantage-but-the-backbone-of-pest-management)) bracket it.
- **Success-fee path:** at $1.8k average per customer-year, $10M needs about 5,600 customers. At $3.6k (a 250+ account firm) it needs 2,800. Realized lift must be provable after the owner stops using the tool. Revenue is lumpy around a November-January window.
- **Realistic outcome:** NiceJob's trajectory implies $0.4-1.7M ARR from software, matching the gate's median of $0.7M. The $12M upside needs a contingency model, multi-platform write-back, and a pivot into churn and collections. I cannot support it with evidence.

## 6. Why now (cuts both ways)

- Price increases are mainstream: 68% of pool businesses raised prices in 2025 and 77% planned to in 2026. 47% expect tariff-driven cost increases. Most plan 10% or less (Skimmer's own survey, vendor marketing; [toolkit](https://www.getskimmer.com/priceincreasetoolkit)).
- Jobber raised its own list prices repeatedly in 2026 ([PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/jobber), trackers conflict). Owners feel cost pressure and are looking for tools.
- But incumbents are reacting at the same time with free calculators, bulk tools and AI analysts, so the window for a standalone product is short.

## 7. Verdict and test design

- **Score:** the free-first margin check is easy to build and the ROI math is clean. Market ceiling, crowding, platform risk and episodic use rule out venture scale. As pitched it is a feature, a lead-gen wedge, or a $0.5-2M concierge business.
- **Keep it only as a test:** a hand-run done-for-you audit from a CSV or Jobber export at $99-199, sold in the Jobber Community, r/LawnCarePros and local owner groups before mid-November, and delivered with owner-approved email. Budget about $750. Do not build the Jobber app first.
- **Kill criteria:**
  - Fewer than 5 of 30 pitched owners pay.
  - Fewer than 3 of the first 10 audited owners actually send a wave.
  - Jobber ships bulk repricing.
  - A free tool (PoolDial scorecard, LawnPro) gets cited as the reason owners declined.
- **What would change my mind:** `jobEdit` confirmed to reprice recurring lines, plus 10+ owners paying a percentage on proven lift.
