# Gate report: P123 (US biofuel producers' purchases of fats, oils and grease feedstock)

Gate run: 2026-10-15. Outcome: **0 strong, 5 weak, 3 kill. No picks.**

"Revenue" below means the company's own net revenue (fees, merchant margin, revenue share, commissions). Gross sales are shown separately where an idea quotes them, because a merchant that books $1B+ of sales at 1-3 cents a pound is not a $100M-revenue business.

## Bottom line

The pool and the shock are real, but the money in this pool sits with integrated players (Darling/DGD, Neste, Chevron REG, Marathon, the soy crushers) and with trading houses that already finance independents. Every idea here is a middleman slice. Merchant margins in this market run 1-3 cents a pound, not the 5 cents the top idea assumes, and the independent "small supplier" volume is closer to 2-3B lb than the 6-7B lb claimed. The best idea, Grease Elevator, tops out near $60M of net revenue in a top-decile case. The three that fail outright do so on a wrong legal premise (Co-Pro Desk), an already-running program (Oil Check) or a data input that does not cover waste lipids (Premium Ledger).

## Pool and premise check

- **The shock is real.** EPA finalized 2026-27 RFS volumes on 27 March 2026. D4 RINs traded at $2.41 on 4 June (EIA). EIA July 2026 data show record feedstock use of about 4.05B lb in the month, with yellow grease/UCO at 610M lb and distillers corn oil at 453M lb.
  - https://www.eia.gov/todayinEnergy/detail.php?id=67765
  - https://www.fastmarkets.com/?p=137236
  - https://www.argusmedia.com/en/news-and-insights/latest-market-news/2884978-us-renewable-feedstocks-demand-sets-another-record
- **The "62B lb by 2027" headline is mostly not a domestic waste-lipid pool.** The farmdoc/OPIS projection is 62.0B lb in 2027, of which about 27.4B lb is imports and 34.6B lb domestic. Soybean oil is the largest single input (42% of July 2026 use by my arithmetic from the Argus figures). The waste-lipid slice the ideas address is roughly 7.3B lb UCO, 9B lb tallow and 5.4B lb corn oil at the July run rate.
  - https://www.opis.com/resources/energy-market-news-from-opis/biomass-based-diesel-feedstock-demand-projected-to-climb-57-in-2026-81-in-2027-analysis/
  - https://farmdocdaily.illinois.edu/2026/06/rewriting-the-rfs-playbook-the-impact-of-final-rvos-on-projected-biomass-based-diesel-feedstock-use-for-2026-2027.html
- **Independents are restarting, but slowly.** Plants ran 50-60% in 2025; the target is 75% or more. Minnesota Soybean Processors restarted Brewster; FutureFuel brought its line back in December after idling Batesville.
  - https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/AG/news/article/2026/05/28/biomass-diesel-scrambles-reach-90
  - https://hydrocarbonprocessing.com/news/2026/05/record-us-biofuel-targets-to-test-biodiesel-industry-after-slow-year/
  - https://www.biobased-diesel.com/post/despite-declining-revenue-in-2025-futurefuel-increasingly-optimistic-about-2026-biodiesel-market
- **Price level.** US Gulf tallow hit a record 90.5 c/lb on 9 June and was 78 c/lb on 27 July. USDA's July 2026 co-products report showed distillers corn oil at about 68-74 c/lb. A "5c all-in" margin is therefore about 7% of product value.
  - https://www.fastmarkets.com/insights/us-gulf-tallow-prices-stabilize-as-market-shifts-focus-from-import-surge-to-brazil-tariffs/
  - https://ams.prod.usda.gov/mnreports/AMS_3616.pdf
- **45Z is still unfinished.** Proposed regulations came out 3 Feb 2026; final rules are expected in November. Credit is limited to North American feedstock from 2026. Co-processed fuel is statutorily excluded. The USDA feedstock calculator (FD-CIC) is final but covers only four field crops and is not yet inside 45ZCF-GREET.
  - https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2026/02/proposed-regulations-issued-on-the-section-45z-clean-fuel-production-credit
  - https://ethanol.org/ace-news/ace-welcomes-usda-final-rule-to-value-and-verify-low-carbon-farming-practices-calls-on-treasury-to-integrate-with-45z
  - https://trinityconsultants.com/resources/section-45z-update-greet-model-and-usda-feedstock-guidance/
- **"Nobody funded here" is wrong at the platform level.** Veriflux (EPA-SBIR-funded traceability), Tagaddod ($26.3M Series A, MENA), Targray (ISCC-certified UCO/tallow trader with a US desk) and Eco-Energy's Iowa feedstock terminal all exist.

## Per-idea notes

### 1. Grease Elevator (weak; the best of the set)

- **Competitors.**
  - Eco-Energy (Copersucar-owned) opened a Newton, Iowa terminal in Oct 2024 with 32 tank-car spots and 1.2M gallons of storage that aggregates DCO, soybean oil, animal fat and UCO for Marathon. It is built for one buyer, so it is not neutral, but it proves the asset type is already being built.
  - Neste's Mahoney plus the Crimson/SeQuential network is a national collection and aggregation system. Darling/Valley/Griffin transfer stations are captive. Baker is a private rendering and grease-collection company. Targray trades ISCC-certified UCO and tallow. A Minnesota trader, Agri Trading, was named in a search summary as an independent UCO/tallow/corn-oil trader (I did not find a primary page).
  - Tagaddod ($26.3M Series A, Sept 2025) runs the aggregation model in MENA and is expanding into Europe and Asia. I found no US startup doing it.
  - https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241015974978/en
  - https://biofuelsdigest.com/tagaddod-lands-26-3m-series-a-for-biofuel-feedstock-platform/
- **Revenue math.**
  - 40 terminals x 50M lb = 2.0B lb. The idea's own estimate of independent UCO is about 1.8B lb. The rest has to be small-renderer and packer fat (renderers are collectors' competitors, not customers) and corn oil from independent ethanol plants (large marketers handle most of it).
  - A 5c all-in margin on a 65-90c product is about 7%. My estimate of a merchandiser's spread is 1-3c. That would put 2.0B lb at $20-60M, and the idea's own text calls 5c "an insider guess".
  - The "$1.3B gross sales" is not revenue.
  - The "30-day pay at a posted discount" pain is unverified by anything I found. It is the central unknown.
- **Substitutes.** Collectors sell to renderers, brokers and directly to biodiesel plants today. EPA has audited UCO collection locations since July 2023, so paper is a real need, but Veriflux and the QAP providers sell that paper.
  - https://www.agweb.com/news/policy/politics/u-s-epa-says-it-auditing-biofuel-producers-used-cooking-oil-supply
- **Why it is not a pick.** Even with 25 terminals at 50M lb and 4c, plus working-capital advance income, I get about $60M. $100M needs 2B lb at 5c, which needs a share of volume that integrated renderers and marketers control.
- **What survives.** A neutral, assayed, same-day-pay drop point for small collectors is a real asset and Eco-Energy's terminal shows buyers will pay for aggregation. It is a $10-60M business, not a $100M one.

### 2. Bankable Supply (weak)

- **The job is done by balance-sheet traders.** Vitol (Global Clean Energy), Macquarie (Calumet), Phillips 66 (XCF/New Rise Reno) and Trafigura (Acelen) all combine supply, offtake and credit. StoneX/VEOS handle Calumet's procurement. OMV Petrom signed a five-year pretreated-UCO contract with Astra Bioplant.
  - https://www.biobased-diesel.com/post/omv-petrom-secures-long-term-contract-with-astra-bioplant-for-pretreated-uco-feedstock
- **The credit paradox.** A lender wants a creditworthy counterparty owing ten years of delivery. A new pooled-supply company with about $150M of working capital and reinsurance is not that, so lenders will keep asking for Vitol, Macquarie, Bunge or ADM. This is the idea's earned secret turned against it.
- **Revenue math.** 20 agreements x 250M lb is 5.0B lb. The addressable buyer set is the idea's own 30-40 projects, of which most restarting biodiesel plants buy soybean oil from crushers on annual terms. Two or three buyers would be 60% of volume. At 1.2-1.5c net (not 2c) and 10-12 agreements, I get $35-45M at best.
- **Timing.** The pending 45Z final rule makes a CI true-up clause plausible, but it also lets a bank-backed trader write the same clause.

### 3. Cleared Lot (weak)

- **Already half-built.**
  - Contracts commonly carry sampling, check-analysis and independent-inspector clauses.
  - EPA's QAP already provides the "verified RIN" defense, and QAP providers carry replacement liability.
  - EcoEngineers-type QAP providers, SGS/Intertek/Saybolt/AmSpec inspection, Veriflux and the 45Z tax-credit insurers (Marsh, IMA) cover the pieces.
- **Revenue math.**
  - $34M of assay fees at $75 a lot needs about 450k lots, which is the whole waste-lipid market.
  - The $75M "warranty premium" belongs to the insurer. An MGA keeps perhaps 15-25%, so about $11-19M at full share.
  - At 25% share the idea's own figure is $28M. Counting the insurer's premium as revenue is the error.
- **Insurability risk.** The only RIN-coverage case I found went against the policyholder, and 45Z policies exclude fraud, known risks and change in law, which are the risks buyers fear.
- **Race risk.** The idea's own text says a large inspector or Darling could ship the certificate first.

### 4. Oil Yield Partner (weak)

- **The pool is real.** Average recovery is about 0.90-0.95 lb/bu against 1.3+ for the best plants (Fluid Quip at Ace Ethanol, ICM FOT at Western New York Energy at up to 1.20). Distillers corn oil is about 70c. Roughly 2B lb of extra oil is the gap.
  - https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20250213472699/en/Fluid-Quip-Technologies-DCO-System-Achieves-Greater-Than-1.3-Pounds-Per-Bushel-Oil-Yield-at-ACE-Ethanol-for-2024
- **"No revenue-share provider found" is wrong historically.** GreenShift licensed extraction to Corn LP (which built and ran it). Prairie Creek planned a third-party-owned skid paid by a share of oil sales. Green Plains leased extraction equipment with lender consent in 2011. A city bought and leased back an extraction unit to a South Bend plant. GreenShift's patents were held invalid in 2014.
  - https://biodieselmagazine.com/articles/court-rules-against-greenshift-in-corn-oil-separation-case-11590
- **Competition is intense and still shipping.** Alfa Laval says it has sold more than 100 Prodec Oil Plus decanters into US ethanol (vendor claim); ICM, Fluid Quip, Valicor, Novonesis and IFF (enzymes) are all active.
  - https://igrownews.com/alfa-laval-latest-news/
  - https://www.iff.com/?p=19624
- **Math.** 40 x 10.2M lb x 70c x 35% = $100M is correct arithmetic. At payback under a year, plants with cash buy the skid. Only plants that cannot or will not self-fund want a share, and the share costs them about $12.7M over five years against a $6M skid. I'd put the addressable group at about 40% of 189 plants (the idea's own figure), making 40 plants over half of it. Net of $6M a plant of capex, the margin is much thinner than the revenue.
- **Risks.** Oil price tied to RIN/45Z policy; lender consent and capex covenants; OEM finance arms; a 5-year clock after which the plant owns the equipment.

### 5. Second Stream (weak)

- **The customer can buy the box.** Big Dipper lists at $3,000-3,560, takes about a minute a day to empty and claims to eliminate pump-outs. Vendor payback claims are 1-4 years. A $1,200 a year service fee on top of a restaurant's own unit is hard to sell.
- **Grease value is lower than assumed.** The 35c a lb is a guess. The only sourced brown-grease value I found was about 4.5c a lb dry basis (NREL techno-economic assumption), and even fresh AGRU "orange" grease is a trap-grease pool, not UCO.
  - https://nrel.gov/docs/fy20osti/74355.pdf
- **Incumbents.** LES (Goldman Sachs Alternatives, 64 service locations, 26 facilities) and Denali (TPG, reported near $1B revenue by an aggregator estimate) own pump-out revenue and treatment-plant tipping fees. Greasezilla has bp financing for up to 40 receiving stations. Restaurant Technologies (ECP, about 32,000 customers, 363M lb UCO collected in 2024) already owns the back-door relationship.
  - https://www.waste360.com/biofuel/downey-ridge-environmental-and-bp-partner-to-recycle-brown-grease-waste-into-biofuels
  - https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220228005195/en/ECP-to-Acquire-Restaurant-Technologies-from-Goldman-Sachs-Asset-Management-as-Sustainability-Initiatives-Move-to-the-Forefront-of-Restaurant-Management
- **Math.** 46,000 sites x $2,250 is $103M of gross, of which about 29% is margin. RT took about two decades to reach 25-45k sites. 4-5k sites in six years is a fair median ($10M).

### 6. Oil Check (kill)

- **Not a gap.** SDSP has paid a protein/oil-based premium for about 20 years and now pays the top 25% of estimated processing value each quarter. AGP measures protein and oil at delivery and pays elevators on it. US Soy and the Soybean Board have promoted constituent pricing for years.
- **The size of the prize is small and old.** The 12c average gain is a 1998 Iowa State estimate quoted in a Purdue fact sheet. The idea's 4c-a-bushel fee would take a third of it.
- **Reachable customers are small.** Farmer-owned and independent crushers are roughly a fifth of a 2.6B bushel crush (USDA: 2.58-2.65B bu in 2025/26). 20% x 2.6B x 4c is about $21M. The big three profit from the status quo, as the idea says.
  - https://ers.usda.gov/media/20867/ocs-26d.pdf
- **Why-now check.** The oil-share figure (50.65% in March 2026) is unverified. DTN cited about 50% in July 2025; another analysis put it near 45% in early 2026.
  - https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/news/article/2025/07/02/current-soybean-oil-contribution

### 7. Co-Pro Desk (kill)

- **Legal premise is wrong.** EPA's BP Cherry Point determination says renewable diesel made by co-processing with petroleum is not eligible for biomass-based diesel (D4) RINs because Clean Air Act 211(o)(1)(D) makes co-processed fuel advanced biofuel only. Chevron's El Segundo soy FCC pathway is also D5.
  - https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2024-09/rfs-chevron-determ-ltr-2024-09-11.pdf
- **45Z is out.** Co-processing is excluded by statute and Congress did not change it. Phillips 66 said in Oct 2025 that tariffs and 45Z made co-processing "less attractive" and dropped plans for three refineries.
- **Who does it.** bp Cherry Point (about 110M gal a year after the 2022 expansion) and a few others, in-house. Oil and agriculture lobbies disagree on it.
  - https://www.biobased-diesel.com/amp/bp-completes-renewable-diesel-coprocessing-expansion-project-in-washington
- **Economics.** The idea's own check gives about $14M a year per refinery on $98M of feed before credits. Refiners can also just buy RINs at the market price.
- **Not verified.** I found no 2026 co-processing announcement from Par Pacific, Delek, PBF or Marathon, and no D5 RIN price.

### 8. Premium Ledger (kill)

- **Input mismatch.** FD-CIC is for four field crops. UCO, tallow and corn-oil chains have little farm-level CI to measure, and Treasury said it has not yet built the 45Z version.
- **Competitors.** Veriflux (traced more than 1B lb in 2024, EPA SBIR-funded, a claimed 850 companies) already does supplier-data ingestion; Verity/Gevo, EcoEngineers, Truterra, Indigo, Bushel and Regrow cover farm-level data.
- **Math.** $970M of "premium flow" is the idea's own arithmetic. $114M requires near-total share of the chain, and the idea's own text says it should be treated as a feature of Cleared Lot.

## What I could not verify

- The central Grease Elevator claim: collectors are paid at a posted discount to index, 30+ days out. No source either way.
- Current UCO/yellow grease prices and a current brown-grease price (Fastmarkets and Argus are paywalled).
- A D5 RIN price and the October 2026 D4 price. The $2.41 D4 figure is 4 June 2026 (EIA).
- Whether Agri Trading's scale matters (named in a search summary only).
- Whether Alfa Laval's "100+ US installations" holds (vendor claim; the page I fetched did not repeat it).
- Current SDSP ValueTrak check sizes.

## Picks

None. No idea has a believable path to $100M of annual net revenue, and none of the surviving five has a median at or above $20M. If one has to be tested anyway, test Grease Elevator: collect 30 collector settlement sheets and measure the real discount to index. If the discount plus slow pay is under 1.5c a pound, kill it; if it is over 4c, the $60M upside case becomes credible and worth a lease-one-site test.
