## 1. The Meter (searches done: 6) / 4. Clawback (shared evidence; searches: 6)
- Existing governance competitors that already do invoice validation, service credits, earn-backs for IT-services contracts: Avasant AvaSense managed governance ("validate and reconcile supplier invoices", "service credits, charge backs, earnbacks"; case study up to 25% savings across IT services portfolio) https://avasant.com/what-we-do/contract-supplier-governance/ ; ISG GovernX (vendor performance, contract compliance, invoice review for billing errors, duplicate charges, missed credits) via search result; ServiceNow Vendor Management Workspace (SLA KPI tracking, automated service credit calc, per SoftwareOne listing) https://platform.softwareone.com/product/vendor-management-workspace/PCP-0739-5645 ; BlueOptima pitches "vendor management" + "outsourcing suppliers" breakdown of coding effort https://www.blueoptima.com/blueoptima-what-and-why (funding unclear, headcount 30-105 conflicting).
- ISG $17M governance deal VERIFIED but reframed: "up to $17M", multiyear, up to 8 years, $300M tech spend, 200 vendors, ~ $2M/yr => ~0.7% of spend. That is the observed market price of a full governance service. https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/11/isg-iii-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ . So Meter's ask (20% x 4% + $200k on $47M = ~1.2% of spend) is above the observed benchmark; and the 'governance budget line' is ~0.5-1% of spend not 1.2%.
- "25% of contracts renegotiated each year" is Cognizant CFO FORECAST (Nomura call Sept 2025; past 15-20%/yr; expects 25%; 20-25% productivity-linked savings), secondary source Nearshore Americas https://nearshoreamericas.com/?p=106554 . Not a measured rate. HFS: renegotiations within 24 months, $50M+ ACV most pressure https://www.hfsresearch.com/news/ai-drives-it-contract-renegotiations-within-24-months-as-pricing-models-shift/ . 40-50% of 2025 new deals have AI clauses (Coherent Market Insights, weak source).
- Vaudit (direct Clawback competitor, AI-native, contingency): $2B+ audited, $100M+ recovered "for clients" (homepage claim; earlier $1.2B / $50M), claims 3-10% recoverable; SOC 2 Type II; covers SaaS, cloud, payments, logistics, ops vendors https://vaudit.com/ . Calibration: lifetime recoveries $100M ~ 5% of spend audited; at 25% fee that is ~ $25M lifetime fees. Clawback's $100M/yr fee pool needs $400M/yr recovered = 4x Vaudit's lifetime recoveries every year.
- PRGX: contract compliance + billing compliance audits exist, contingency; no IT-services case found by search (idea claims one) https://www.prgx.com/faq/what-are-the-stages-in-contract-compliance . APEX white paper: contract IT labor audit recovered >$2M for a manufacturer (contract-labor double-billing) https://go.apexanalytix.com/hubfs/apex%20Legacy%20Website%20Files/APEX_White_Paper_Contract_Comp_Gig_2020.pdf

Noem), First Circuit denied stay July 24; BUT Trump proclamation Sept 18 2026 extended the fee for 12 months to Sept 21 2027; litigation ongoing, DC court ruled for govt Dec 2025 => 'vacated' premise is partial/contested https://www.globalimmigrationblog.com/2026/06/federal-court-vacates-100000-h-1b-fee/

Tentative Clawback: weak; Vaudit and PRGX do contingency; cap evidence says recoverable 1-5%; rule library thin.
