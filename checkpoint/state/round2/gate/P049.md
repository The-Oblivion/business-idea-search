# Gate report: pool P049, Indonesia free-meals programme (MBG) money flowing through ~27,000 community kitchens (SPPG)

Gate date: 2026-10-15. Stance: skeptical by default. 8 ideas, about 45 searches (Indonesian and English), plus page reads of Katadata, Bisnis, JawaPos, detik, RCTI+/Okezone, Kontan and one primary source (Kemenko Pangan press release No. 267/2026, read as a PDF). Peraturan BGN 10/2026 itself could not be fetched (JDIH link is dead or a placeholder), so its eligibility rules rest on title/summary pages. Result: **6 kill, 2 weak, 0 strong. No picks.**

## Headline finding

The pool is real ($15B programme, roughly $8-9B a year of ingredient buying) but the state has deliberately fragmented it and capped every margin in it. Each layer a private company could scale is either forbidden, capped, or already assigned to a state-owned company.

- **Sellers must be small and local, by regulation.** Peraturan BGN 10/2026 (set 27 Jul, in force 18 Aug 2026) is titled as covering micro and small enterprises, individual companies, cooperatives, Kopdes Merah Putih and BUMDes. Local-first order: same kecamatan, then nearest kecamatan, then regency, then province, then outside the province (Article 5). NIB and NPWP required; producers and processors also need food-safety and halal certification. A plant doing $13M+ of sales is not a micro or small enterprise. https://veritask.ai/en/artikel/mbg-marketplace-to-be-launched-soon-who-can-become-a-supplier , https://paralegal.id/peraturan/peraturan-badan-gizi-nasional-nomor-10-tahun-2026/
- **Anti-concentration enforcement is live.** BGN's then-deputy said each SPPG must use at least 15 suppliers, one supplier per food type, and suspended kitchens whose mitra steered supply to one or two suppliers. The 15-supplier figure is a statement by a deputy who has since resigned, not confirmed in the regulation summary, so its status under the new head is unverified. https://www.jawapos.com/nasional/2603080157/bgn-temukan-bahan-baku-pangan-di-9-sppg-di-pekanbaru-dimonopoli-mitra , https://jogja.antaranews.com/berita/808790/bgn-meminta-sppg-memutus-kerja-sama-dengan-mitra-yang-mark-up-bahan-mbg , https://health.detik.com/berita-detikhealth/d-8533036/18-sppg-disetop-karena-kasus-keracunan-mbg-monopoli-suplier-ini-temuan-bgn
- **BGN has said it does not want big-company factory food.** Deputy Head Nanik Deyang (14 Dec 2025): kitchens should stop using biscuits and bread from big companies; food should come from residents and UMKM near the kitchen. https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-8259031/bgn-minta-dapur-mbg-tak-pakai-makanan-olahan-pabrik
- **Prices are capped and mark-ups banned.** Ingredient budget is Rp8,000-10,000 a portion; mitra marking up above HET are suspended without incentive; the kepala SPPG is personally exposed to BPK findings. https://www.pajak.com/ekonomi/makro/bgn-mitra-sppg-jangan-mark-up-harga-bahan-baku/ , https://jogja.antaranews.com/berita/808790/bgn-meminta-sppg-memutus-kerja-sama-dengan-mitra-yang-mark-up-bahan-mbg
- **The state is building the middle layer itself.** (a) A SIPLah-style BGN procurement portal, mandatory for seven commodities (rice, oil, chicken, eggs, beef, fish, milk), verified sellers vetted through associations, one-PO/one-tax-invoice visibility for government; launch slipped past the late-Sept target, marketplaces may be invited as partners. https://katadata.co.id/berita/nasional/6aabd4480354c/bgn-luncurkan-portal-pengadaan-khusus-dapur-mbg-buka-opsi-gandeng-marketplace (b) Kopdes Merah Putih named main supplier: Agrinas Pangan built the gudang/gerai (Rp90T spent) and employs 28,891 Kopdes managers on 2-year contracts starting 1 Oct 2026; Bulog (rice), Pos Indonesia (logistics), Telkom (Digi Koperasi) are wired in; Perpres 82/2026 signed 23 Sep. https://katadata.co.id/berita/industri/6abc8914b3850/agrinas-pangan-tanda-tangan-kontrak-pegawai-kopdes-gaji-hingga-rp8-3-juta , https://nasional.kontan.co.id/news/pengadaan-aset-kopdes-merah-putih-agrinas-pangan-sudah-serap-anggaran-rp-90-triliun , https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/terkini/5483521/bulog-tegaskan-siap-penuhi-kebutuhan-beras-kopdes-merah-putih (c) Food-safety testing runs through BPOM under a BGN swakelola (self-managed) agreement, so there is no private buyer for lab services: https://www.pom.go.id/berita/bpom-dan-bgn-perkuat-sinergi-pengawasan-keamanan-program-mbg (d) A Command Center now integrates supply-chain and food-safety data across ministries: Kemenko Pangan press release No. 267/SES.M.PANGAN/SP/7/2026, 29 Jul 2026 (read as PDF).
- **The programme is shrinking and the kitchen economics were reset.** Beneficiary target cut from 82.9M to about 63M; 2026 budget cut from Rp335T to about Rp268T; 2027 draft Rp240T. New head Sudaryono (since 22 Jul) refuses the previous leadership's profit promise; from 5 Oct 2026 the flat Rp6M-a-day facility incentive became Rp2,000 per portion; 883 kitchens closed permanently and are not paid; of 27,485 operating kitchens on 29 Jul, 8,727 lacked SLHS and were given a month. https://investortrust.id/market/106119/indonesias-massive-free-school-lunch-program-gets-a-fiscal-reality-check , https://katadata.co.id/berita/nasional/6a674115c11d4/kepala-bgn-ubah-skema-pengembalian-investasi-sppg-tak-lagi-jamin-keuntungan , https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/ekonomi/5490486/5-fakta-insentif-sppg--bukan-lagi-rp6-juta--tetap-cuan-meski-rp2-000-per-porsi-mbg , https://ekonomi.bisnis.com/read/20260727/12/1991378/833-dapur-mbg-ditutup-permanen-bgn-pastikan-tak-ada-pembayaran
- **Fact corrections to the input.** The Attorney General named ex-BGN head Dadan Hindayana and two ex-deputies as suspects on 3 Jun 2026, not 29 Sep (https://kabar24.bisnis.com/read/20260605/15/1978416/kronologi-lengkap-kasus-dadan-cs-eks-kepala-bgn-jadi-tersangka-korupsi-mbg). Kopdes physical completion was 26,661 by end Q3, not 14,442 (https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/okezone/5483215/1-oktober-kopdes-merah-putih-mulai-operasi--26-661-gerai-sudah-siap). The Rp6M-a-day incentive the ideas use no longer exists in that form. Average kitchen size is about 2,000-2,500 portions, not 3,000 (Antara, Jan 2026, via search), which cuts every per-kitchen dollar figure by 15-25%.

## Idea-by-idea notes

### 1. Siap Masak (kill)
- **Competitors and substitutes.** Japfa says it has supplied MBG kitchens since Jan 2025 and has 15 slaughterhouses, 78 breeding farms and a national cold-storage network (https://www.kabarbursa.com/insight-daily/kapasitas-raksasa-japfa-jpfa-bakal-suplai-program-mbg ; https://market.bisnis.com/read/20260224/192/1955465/peluang-jpfa-dan-cpin-pasok-11-juta-ton-ayam-ke-program-mbg). Danantara plans Rp20T for poultry in 13 provinces and an integrated poultry industry at five sites (https://katadata.co.id/amp/finansial/bursa/6967305b29375/danantara-siapkan-proyek-unggas-terintegrasi-dibangun-di-5-lokasi-mulai-februari/). Production cooperatives get LPDB money to supply kitchens (https://investortrust.id/business/84745/lewat-lpdb-menteri-koperasi-bakal-guyur-dana-untuk-sediakan-bahan-pangan-mbg). Eden Farm and Sayurbox: no MBG role found. Each kitchen already has a nutritionist inspecting incoming chicken and rejecting bad batches.
- **Math check.** The $136k per kitchen assumes 3,000 portions. At about 2,250 portions, Rp9k ingredient budget and 220 days, ingredient spend is about $280k and chicken plus vegetables at 29% is about $81k. $100M then needs about 1,230 kitchens (4.5% of 27,485) and about 12 plants at $4-6M capex each, for $12-15M of gross profit.
- **Legal check.** Adverse. Eligibility list excludes medium and large firms (small enterprise sales ceiling is roughly Rp15B under the MSME decree, from memory, about $0.9M); a coop-owned plant makes revenue the coop's and leaves us a management fee; BGN says coops created to get around the rules do not count. Chicken and eggs are also on the mandatory portal with association-vetted sellers.
- **Upside needs.** BGN accepting large certified processors as eligible sellers (against its stated direction, about 10%), kitchens tolerating a single processor for the two biggest lines, and no integrator or Danantara response.

### 2. Satu Faktur (weak)
- **Competitors.** BGN portal (state); Agrinas Pangan (builds gudang, employs the managers); Pos Indonesia (logistics for all Kopdes: https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/NxGCGgWX-pos-indonesia-backs-kopdes-merah-putih-logistics-with-modernized-distribution); Telkom Digi Koperasi (https://inet.detik.com/telecommunication/d-8029536/dukung-kopdes-merah-putih-telkom-hadirkan-inovasi-digi-koperasi); Bulog; and the one private precedent, Indogrosir's OMI Merah Putih Mart partnership with a Malang Kopdes (https://www.metrotvnews.com/read/kewCM2vn-koperasi-merah-putih-randugading-sukses-jalankan-7-unit-usaha-omzet-tembus-puluhan-juta).
- **Math check.** About 3.9 kitchens per kecamatan at about $280k gives $1.1M of GMV and $66k of fee at 6%. $100M needs about 1,500 hubs, 21% of kecamatan. The government mandates the Kopdes channel (Menko Pangan: SPPG may not buy outside the village business, sanctions up to closure), which is the only thing that makes volume non-heroic, but that volume belongs to the Kopdes, not to a private operator. https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/terkini/5446025/zulhas-wajibkan-dapur-mbg-serap-suplai-bahan-makanan-lewat-kopdes-merah-putih
- **Legal check.** Mixed. Cooperatives as aggregators are policy-aligned. Not found: any rule letting a private company run a Kopdes warehouse under a management contract; Kopdes managers are Agrinas employees; "the mechanism is still being drafted" (Menko Pangan). Perpres 82/2026 text unread. A single consolidated invoice also sits awkwardly with BGN's multi-supplier stance.
- **Upside needs.** Kemenkop or Agrinas designating a small number of private operators to run hubs at scale (the only route to about 1,000 hubs), plus a fee accepted as an allowable at-cost line.

### 3. Franchisor-in-a-box (kill)
- **Competitors and substitutes.** Polri's own food-security warehouses, farms and feed mill; Polri reported 1,376 kitchens in Jul 2026 and targets 1,500; Kadin's 742nd kitchen opened Mar 2026, target 1,000; BGN-BPOM testing and coaching agreement (83 regional units); IDSurvey (Sucofindo, Surveyor Indonesia) offered HACCP and QA support (https://www.cnnindonesia.com/nasional/20250411205541-25-1217975/idsurvey-dukung-bgn-siap-kawal-kualitas-program-makan-bergizi-gratis). ICW asked KPK to oversee Polri's kitchens; the 10-kitchen cap exemption for Polri rests on ICW's reading of the technical guide, not verified.
- **Math check.** $19.5k a kitchen needs 5,100 kitchens; Polri plus Kadin is about 2,500 at their targets, so even 100% of both gives about $49M. TNI, BIN, NU and Muhammadiyah figures are 2025 targets with no realised counts found.
- **Legal check.** A 2.5% procurement margin share is a commission on purchases in a programme whose former leadership is charged over affiliated foundations and mark-ups. This is the round-1 failure of selling something that defeats its own premise.

### 4. Java-to-3T protein corridor (weak; closest to alive)
- **What is real.** BGN's head said Papua needs about three times Java's budget (Rp25T for about 750k beneficiaries versus Rp7.5T), and BGN officials put Papua ingredient cost at Rp26-27k a portion (https://www.cnnindonesia.com/ekonomi/20251216195222-92-1307558/bos-bgn-lapor-prabowo-soal-harga-bahan-pokok-mahal-di-papua ; https://papua.antaranews.com/berita/735598/penjabat-gubernur-papua-program-mbg-perlu-penyesuaian-di-daerah). 480 kitchens activated 2 Oct (211 independent, 269 remote) (https://investortrust.id/national/117912/perluas-mbg-ke-wilayah-3t-bgn-aktifkan-480-dapur-sppg-mulai-2-oktober-2026). Kemenko Pangan's primary release covers 8,617 remote SPPG; secondary reports say 1,703 are 3T. The Finance Minister admits upstream supply and distribution reliability are the main 3T constraints, and NTT pioneer ferries stopped in July for lack of budget (https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/ekonomi/5432677/angkutan-logistik-perintis-lumpuh--distribusi-mbg-tehambat-di-wilayah-3t). The refocus toward 3T and stunting areas is the programme's one growth direction.
- **What cuts against it.** Papua's governor wants kitchens to use local produce (https://rri.co.id/jayapura/regional/2485957/gubernur-papua-minta-dapur-mbg-wajib-serap-produk-lokal); Mimika alone makes 14 t of eggs a day against 11 t of local use (https://papua.antaranews.com/berita/741549/mimika-ketersediaan-telur-ayam-cukup-untuk-program-mbg); the Finance Minister wants kitchens to buy direct from local producers without intermediaries; no numeric cost index exists (Rp30-40k was only a Papua governor's proposal); BGN is seeking CSR and foreign money for kitchens in regions with no investors (https://www.idnfinancials.com/news/64570/bgn-seeks-csr-and-foreign-funding-for-mbg-programme-in-remote-regions); the 5 Oct incentive cut hits small remote kitchens hardest; Tol Laut is subsidised with a 2022 route study showing 33% load and 12.8% backhaul (https://ejournal.unmus.ac.id/index.php/agricola/article/download/4498/2719), so empty reefer capacity may be cheap or subsidised, and Pelni, Bulog and Kopdes gudang (each with a mandated cold store) are alternatives. Idea's own warning stands: at 352 3T kitchens the pool is about $0.17B.
- **Math check.** 1,703 kitchens x about 1,500 portions x 220 days x Rp26k is about $0.9B for Papua-priced kitchens; NTT and Maluku are lower, so about $0.7B is fairer. Only the non-local share (frozen chicken, some eggs, dry goods, oil, milk) is addressable, about 35-40%, so about $0.25-0.3B. $100M of product revenue is then 35-40% of 3T addressable spend, or about 10% of the remote-8,617 pool if that pool is built and priced at index. The 12% in the idea ignores the addressable-share step.
- **Upside needs (and plausibility).** (1) BGN codifies a numeric cost index and the rent incentive for remote kitchens (about 60%); (2) a local coop or BUMDes can be seller of record for goods from Java and the portal's association vetting admits it (about 50%); (3) Pelni/Kopdes/Bulog do not pre-empt the chilled lane (about 40%); (4) the remote kitchen count reaches several thousand rather than a few hundred (about 40%); (5) a Papua operating team executes. Joint probability is single digits, which is what top-decile means; each link is plausible, none is secure.
- **Why not a pick.** Median is about $14M (80-120 kitchens at about 35% of $400k), below the $20M bar, and the $90M upside is at best at the threshold with no evidence yet on links 1, 2 and 4.

### 5. Employer-of-record (kill)
- **Competitors and substitutes.** BPJS Ketenagakerjaan insurance (BGN pays the Rp16,800 a month premium; 278,614 workers in 5,322 kitchens were uncovered in April per BGN), earned-wage-access and on-demand payroll startups: Wagely (a $5.6M strategic round per TechCrunch, later $23M equity and debt reported; free to employers), GajiGesa, Kini (https://techcrunch.com/?p=2162860 ; https://techcrunch.com/?p=2108637), plus yayasan cash payroll.
- **Math check.** Volunteer incentive is Rp100-200k a day (quoted from Keputusan 401.1/2025 in a student paper, unverified). That is $63-126k a kitchen of payroll and $4-8k of fee at 6%. Net $100M needs 13,000-26,000 kitchens, which is half to all of them. The $100M gross-billings figure is an accounting presentation, not margin. No payer: the volunteer pay line is "at cost" inside a Rp3,000-a-portion cap that also covers utilities; an EOR margin is not a cost. The Komnas HAM June statement was not found.

### 6. Resolution operator (kill)
- **Pool check.** Bank-financed kitchens are small: BNI Rp1.5T to 577 debtors (Jan 2026), BSI Rp198B to about 211 kitchens, BTN 24 kitchens, BRI Rp104B; OJK has not mapped it. Most of the 27k are owner-equity. 883 permanently closed kitchens are not paid; BGN itself opened a yayasan-switch route on 2 Oct, which removes the operator's reason to exist for many cases; BGN planned about 2,000 more closures in early September. https://finansial.bisnis.com/read/20260812/90/1995622/ratusan-sppg-ditutup-ojk-kaji-dampaknya-ke-kredit-bank ; https://news.ddtc.co.id/berita/nasional/1823012/dari-pajak-bgn-izinkan-mitra-dapur-mbg-ganti-yayasan-yang-merugikan
- **Math check.** Restored incentive is now Rp2,000 a portion: about $65k a year at 2,250 portions, so 15% is about $10k, not $12.4k. $100M needs over 5,000 kitchens, more than all bank-financed kitchens.

### 7. Telur Koperasi (kill)
- **Premise check.** The shock is real (Blitar farmers gave away a million eggs on 1 Jun at Rp20.6-21k a kg against about Rp23k cost; HAP Rp26,500; SE BGN 14/2026; East Java three-eggs-a-week deal at a Rp24,000 floor, with cooperatives delivering direct to kitchens). That deal is built on intermediary-free, direct-to-kitchen sales and BGN calls the three-a-week menu temporary. https://jawapos.com/berita-daerah/2606020132/harga-anjlok-peternak-di-blitar-bagi-bagi-1-juta-telur-ke-warga ; https://finance.detik.com/berita-ekonomi-bisnis/d-8522633/menu-telur-program-mbg-minimal-3-kali-seminggu-imbas-harga-anjlok
- **Competitors.** The government announced on 9 Oct 2026 that it will build an egg-powder and meat-powder plant, construction from 2027, to stop imports (https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/inews/5490480/pemerintah-bakal-bangun-pabrik-tepung-telur-dan-daging-untuk-setop-impor). Kementan in 2022 put egg-powder imports at about 10,000 t a year, so the industrial outlet is small (https://www.industry.co.id/read/104404/kementan-dorong-penguatan-hilirisasi-dan-penataan-rantai-pasok-telur-ayam-ras). Japfa-linked Intan Kenkomayo already makes pasteurised liquid egg (from the idea, unverified here).
- **Math check.** Eight billion eggs at about 60 g is 480 kt, but the HAP floor limits any processing premium and no source shows kitchens use liquid egg; how eggs are cooked in MBG was not found. 11% of MBG eggs through the plant needs kitchens to pay about 16% over shell egg for a product they may not use.

### 8. Dapur Utilitas (kill)
- **Competitors.** PGN/Gagas Energi CNG already serves 46 kitchens and claims 15-30% savings (https://www.rctiplus.com/news/detail/okezone/5455724/cng-mengalir-ke-46-dapur-mbg--hemat-biaya-energi-20-persen ; https://jatengprov.go.id/beritadaerah/lebih-hemat-dapur-sppg-di-rembang-mulai-manfaatkan-cng/); Pertamina Patra Niaga LPG (NTT shortage in May: https://rm.id/baca-berita/nasional/310903/bgn-lapor-20-sppg-di-nttntb-terdampak-kelangkaan-elpiji-pertamina-gercep-isi-pasokan); local IPAL contractors with regional environment offices coaching (https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5581252/satgas-mbg-banyak-sppg-di-lampung-terkendala-ipal-untuk-dapat-slhs).
- **Math check.** Rp400-600 a portion at 2,250 portions and 220 days is $12.5-18.7k a year, or $62-130k over a 5-7 year contract, against a one-off IPAL of Rp18-70M ($1.1-4.4k). Gas at Magelang was Rp12M a month for 2,179 portions, about $8k a year, and a savings share on it is $1.2-2.4k. Revenue would be mostly gas resale at thin margin with $110-180M of capex on counterparties whose incentive was just cut and whose kitchens can be closed without payment.

## Near miss: what would change idea 4

Both a written BGN 3T-office answer on (a) a numeric cost index and (b) whether a local coop or BUMDes can be seller of record for Java-sourced goods under Article 5 and the portal's verified-seller rules, and a landed-cost test on 10 kitchens against the trader price. If both come back yes and the remote kitchen count is confirmed in the thousands, the median moves toward $20M and the upside above $100M, and it should be re-gated.
