- S1 (WebSearch FHFA DTS 2026): NPRM published 91 FR 37848 on 24 Jun 2026; comments closed 24 Jul 2026; final expected effective by 1 Jan 2028, could be extended. FHFA: chattel denial rate 65.6% vs 8.8% site-built; avg chattel rate 9.24% vs 6.63% mortgage; neither GSE has bought chattel under DTS to date. - S2 (lender concentration): CFPB (2019 loans): top 5 lenders >40% of all MH purchase loans, ~75% of chattel; one opinion piece: Triad, Vanderbilt, 21st Mortgage = 76% (undated). Banks exiting. Avg chattel rate 9.7% in 2024 (x2mortgage blog, low authority) vs 7.0% MH mortgages. CFPB: consumerfinance.gov/about-us/newsroom/manufactured-housing-loan-borrowers-face-higher-interest-rates-risks-and-barriers-to-credit
- S3 Kazdin comment letter (ex-Fannie Mae MH risk analytics lead, 22 Jul 2026; fhfa.gov/sites/default/files/2026-07/Kazdin_Comment_Letter_MH_July22.pdf, read via pdftotext): FHFA's own NPRM admits chattel has limited liquidity, no securitization infrastructure, weak performance data. His model: chattel expected lifetime loss ~10% (stress 31%) vs 0.4% single-family; historical severity 80-90%; all-in GSE chattel rate could be 10.6-10.9% (above the 9.2% lender rate), i.e. GSE purchase would NOT compress rates. His view, not lender data. Banks risk-weight chattel 100%.
- S4 Pew (Feb 2026 brief + search snippets): 2018-2024 about 366,000 buyers used home-only (chattel) loans = 44% of those who financed; at least 88,000 of them owned their land and were likely eligible for a standard mortgage (=~12.6k/yr) -> retitling/conversion pool for NEW buyers is small. Only ~25% of borrowers have homes titled as real estate. Some states require paying off home-only loan before retitle (blocks refi). Virginia: 92% of land-owning borrowers got mortgage, almost none on leased land/resident-owned co-ops. WA 2025 law lets resident-owned community homes title as real estate. => conversion factory as standalone = small (kill/fold).

- S9 Title I: HUD 2024 indexing: single-section limit $105,532, multi-section $193,719, combo $148,909/$237,096, lot $43,377 (cases on/after 29 Mar 2024), annual review.
