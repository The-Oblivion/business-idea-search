## [v1] Verified facts (searches 1-8, via WebSearch summaries; primary URLs where given)
- Toriteki Act in force 1 Jan 2026: new "特定運送委託" (shipper -> carrier direct entrustment now covered, in addition to carrier re-subcontracting); employee test (300) added alongside capital test; ban on notes; ERC/factoring that cannot yield full cash by due date = violation; ban on unilateral price setting when entrusted SME asks for consultation; payment due <= 60 days from receipt; fine up to JPY 500k; MLIT+JFTC joint shipper patrols Oct-Nov 2025. Source search: logi-today 881354, atsoho.com blog, mlit.go.jp/jidosha/content/001969440.pdf
- Trucking Business Act (Act 60 of 2025) + new Act 61 of 2025: written contract + actual-carrier register since 1 Apr 2025; from 1 Apr 2026 extends to forwarders (利用運送) and a duty-of-effort to limit subcontract tiers to 2; shippers using white-trucks fined up to JPY 1M. "適正原価" (appropriate-cost floor on rates) + permit renewal: NOT yet in force, due within 3 years of promulgation (i.e. by about mid-2028). Source: MLIT jidosha_mn4_000019, wwwtb.mlit.go.jp/kinki/content/000370895.pdf
- Logistics Efficiency Act: designated shippers & chain operators = >= 90,000 t/yr, top ~3,200; 3,299 notified. CLO (executive/director level) by end Oct 2026; first mid/long-term plan due end Oct 2026, thereafter end July each year; periodic reports from end July 2027; failure to appoint CLO = fine <= JPY 1M; plan under-implementation -> recommendation/order. Source: stockcrew.co.jp article 2026-04-21, nishimura corporate_260123_ja.pdf, logi-today 1001166
- Hacobu: JPY 700M raise May 2025, cumulative JPY 5.6B; MOVO Berth at 37,000+ logistics sites (30% of major sites), 57.2% share of berth-mgmt market FY2024 (6th year #1), retention 99.8%; MOVO ID 32,000 sites, 800k driver IDs, 2.5M tx/month. Private, no revenue disclosed.
- MLIT 2015 survey: avg waiting 48 min per run; runs with waiting avg 1h45; loading/unloading avg 2h46; ~60% shipper-side time-designated, ~75% for actual carriers. (OLD data; need newer if possible.)
- Remote roll-call (遠隔点呼) between operators: MLIT trial since Jul 2024, extended to 31 Dec 2025; 23,329 inter-operator remote roll-calls by end Jan 2025; draft permit criteria Apr 2025 (permit per office, same business type, constant comms, periodic audit). Regulatory institutionalization status unknown after Dec 2025. Japan Post roll-call falsification at 82 sites (Jun 2025).
- Receivables finance: online factoring for trucking advertised at fee 2%+, funds in 2 hrs (comparison blogs only). No funded Japanese freight-specific factoring startup found in first search (to check again: Trabox, PayTech, OLTA).
- Driver shortage: JTA projection ~278k shortfall FY2030; capacity shortfall 14.2% in 2024 and 34.1% in 2030 if no action (MLIT study group).

## [v2] More verified facts (searches 9-19)
- MLIT survey published Jul 2026 (FY2025, n=1,032, Apr-Aug 2025 typical day): avg run 10h13m; waiting 1h00 + loading 41m + ancillary 21m = 2h02m. FY2024 was 3h18m (or 3h02m in an earlier press report). So waiting/loading time FELL ~1h16m in one year => waiting-claims pool is shrinking fast (gov target <= 2h per run).
- Standard fare (標準的運賃) revised 22 Mar 2024, in force 1 Jun 2024: +~8% avg; waiting fee per 30 min after first 30 min (rises only 0-20 yen per class), +50% after 2h; NEW loading/unloading fee: medium truck JPY 2,180 per 30 min (machine), 2,100 (hand); 10% "subcontract handling fee" can be charged on top; fuel above JPY 120/L separately. Notification rate (届出率) 65.2% as of end-May 2026 (MLIT doc).
- Appropriate cost (適正原価): expert panel opened 17 Jul 2026 (meeting 4 on 19 Oct 2026); fixed + variable cost formula, incl. waiting/loading fees, load factor/deadhead, inflation, safety cost; trade press: recommendation by end-2026, notification during 2027, 1-yr grace, full operation FY2028; statutory deadline Jun 2028. Duty falls mainly on truck operators (when carrying OR subcontracting must not continuously fall below). Permit renewal system comes in same window.
- Secondary (ISVD commentary citing MLIT field survey): >70% of operators re-subcontract, each tier skims 5-10%; ~30% ordered work at <90% of price they received.
- Bankruptcies (TDB): 328 in FY2024 (2nd highest after 371 in 2008), 321 in FY2025 (4th highest); labour shortage 55 (12.5%); TSR: 353 in FY2024.
- Payment mechanics: electronic clearing house for notes ends 31 Mar 2027; most banks set last note issuance 30 Sep 2026 (already passed). All-industry survey: 6.6% of transactions exceed 60 days (2.3% cash, 4.2% notes/ERC/factoring); 24.3% of factoring users say supplier bears the discount fee. Trucking 2015: 76.7% all-cash.
- Trabox (Visional group, TSE Prime parent): 20,000+ carrier members, 230k+ cases/month; runs "Finto" invoice mgmt/payment platform incl. Finto factoring, card post-pay, invoice transfer => HIDDEN INCUMBENT for freight receivable finance. CBcloud PickGo: ~70k kei-cargo, ~40k motorbike, ~3,400 general freight carriers (Jul 2025); raised from Okinawa investors Apr 2026.
- Foreign drivers: SSW auto-transport cap cut to 22,100 (5-yr to FY2028); only 151 SSW-1 in the field at end-Dec 2025. => too small for $100M; Ikusei Shuro starts 2027.
- Remote roll-call between operators: formalised May 2025; only 197 cumulative filings at end-Dec 2025 (159 trucks); same business type, per-office permit, 5-yr term. => nascent, thin demand signal.
- No Japanese freight rate/cost index vendor found (KPMG sells a one-off tool; JTA hosts free standard-fare calculator; BeyondTrucks US only).
- Only 10.4% of SMEs can fully visualize waiting/loading time (Data Wing Ark 1st survey Nov 2024, n=513).
- Japan Post: MLIT audited 119 offices, false roll-call at 82; hearing on revoking its general freight licence (2026); Japan Post expanding roll-call outsourcing.

## [v3] More facts (searches 20-23) + design decisions
- Pallets: ~500M pallets in Japan, T11 ~30% (JPR president); gov FY2030 targets: T11 share of pallet production 26% -> 50%+, rental-pallet T11 share 76% -> 85%+; loading-time target per person-year 375h -> 315h. JPR (Japan Pallet Rental): ~JPY 30B revenue, ~12M pallets, ~60 depots, 55.57M pallet-supplies FY2025 (+4.7%), has shared-collection system. UPR (TSE 7065): ~5.28M pallets (Nov 2025). Logistics Efficiency Act: shippers must make effort on loading-time cuts via palletization, ASN.
- Digital tachograph: freight carriers' installation 75% (Mar 2026 follow-up), 80% for >=4t, ~50% for <2t; MLIT target 85% by 2027; mandate expansion under consideration (not decided); subsidy review; Jan 2026 operator survey; MLIT calls DTG useful for visualizing waiting/loading.
- Forwarders (利用運送): register + written-contract duty extends to forwarders from 1 Apr 2026 (loads >= 1.5 t; retention 1 yr; operators with >= 1M t/yr forwarding need transport-use manager). Kyushu alone: 2,355 forwarders (1,671 auto) at Mar 2025.
- CLO market already contested: Hacobell CLO dashboard (launched 28 Aug 2026, ties to its "トラック簿" reservation system), Transcosmos (from JPY 400k/month), ABeam (9 Jul 2026), Sagawa Global Logistics "CLO Support 2026" (15 Jul 2026), Logisteed. => CLO software/consulting is a red ocean.
- Multi-tier survey (METI/MLIT/MAFF via JTA, 2023, n=4,401): 74% use other truckers; pass-down rate (share of received fare paid to sub): 4% >=100%, 28% 95-99%, 42% 90-94%, 14% 85-89%, 7% 80-84%, 5% <80% => avg intermediary retention ~8%.
- Load factor (積載効率) of commercial trucks: 39.08% FY2025 (-2.3 pts; relay transport + fewer backhauls), 41.3% FY2024, 39.9% FY2023.
- Trabox Finto: factoring launched Sep 2025, 2-party, fee 2-9.5%; card post-pay cumulative JPY 10B+ by Apr 2026 => freight finance has an incumbent, volume still small.
- M&A: TSR 2025 successor-absence rate in transport 57.26%; since Oct 2015, 711 trucking corporations disappeared via merger, 267 third-party successions, acquirers median 80 trucks vs industry median 12; listings ask net assets + ~0.9x EBITDA; no PE trucking roll-up found; SBS/large 3PLs acquire.
- Foreign SSW drivers: dead for $100M (cap 22,100; 151 in field).
