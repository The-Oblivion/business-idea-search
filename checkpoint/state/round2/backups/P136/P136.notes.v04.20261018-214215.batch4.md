## Batch 2 findings: primary source = Treasury/IRS NPRM REG-117199-25 (181-page PDF, text extracted to scratchpad/nprm.txt; Fed Reg public inspection doc 2026-20277) - all (S)
- (S) 30 states participating as of Aug/Sep 14 2026 (IRS list: AL AK AR CO FL GA ID IN IA KS KY LA MS MO MT NE NV NH ND NC OH OK SC SD TN TX UT VA WV WY). DC not on list.
- (S) Treasury/IRS estimate: 600-700 SGOs by 2030; >11 million taxpayers make qualified contributions totaling $26B/yr, funding ~2M full-time ($12,000) scholarships or 5M part-time ($4,500) by 2030. ~9M students eligible; 46.5M taxpayers eligible to take both state+federal credits; safe harbor for multistate SGOs adds ~450 SGOs and ~$3-4B/yr contributions. (These are the agency's regulatory-impact estimates, likely optimistic; I haircut them in sizing.) Current state tax-credit scholarship programs are far smaller.
- (S) Income verification: Treasury assumes ~$100 per household income verification (Equifax Work Number $46.95 per employer record x 2 records, June 2026); safe harbor population ~10M children; at 52% take-up, employment verification would cost $520M; safe harbor "saves $540M annually" in verification costs. Substantially all households with kids in participating states are under 300% AMI per Treasury (so verification is mostly a formality; categorical eligibility = SNAP/Medicaid letters etc).
- (S) Verification methods: direct income verification (pay stubs, tax returns, IRS transcripts, W-2s), categorical eligibility (award letter within 12 months), safe harbors.
- (S) 90% spend test: statute = SGO must spend >=90% of income on scholarships; NPRM: must meet by last day of taxable year FOLLOWING year income received; cash method - amounts "treated as spent when paid"; "income" = total gross receipts (so interest counts); 85%-of-activities safe harbor lets SGO raise separate admin $ outside the 25F account. Treasury says w/o that safe harbor SGOs would get 18% fewer contributions (a $4B/yr effect).
- (S) **Qualified digital wallet** (proposed 1.25F-1(a)(13)): third-party provider platform where families submit purchase requests, tracks approved expenses, pre-approves vendors, pays vendors directly or requires timely receipts. NPRM: provided an SGO does not retain ownership of funds transferred to a third party for disbursement through a qualified digital wallet, the SGO is treated as having SPENT the funds as of transfer date for the 90% test. => wallet provider can end up custodian of unspent scholarship balances (float). Tuition/fees must be paid directly to school; other vendors paid directly only if verified provider, unrelated to student; no money to families except qualified reimbursements w/ receipt.
- (S) SGOs must implement systems to prevent and detect DUPLICATION of scholarship awards to same student for same expense (stakeholders: primary fraud sources = duplicate awards and unqualified/extravagant spend). No shared registry exists in NPRM -> opening for a neutral clearinghouse.
- (S) Payroll deduction is explicitly an allowed method for a qualified contribution (NPRM def text "payroll deduction, or other similar method", cash USD, no digital assets).
- (S) Audit: annual independent financial+programmatic audit; SGOs with receipts >$500k pay ~$30,000/yr, others ~ $10,000 (Treasury). Tiny pool ($10-20M total across 650 SGOs).
- (S) Comments due Dec 1 2026; hearing Dec 15 2026; temp regs effective Dec 1 2026; state SGO list due Feb 15 2027.
- (S) Odyssey: $74M Series B (signalbase page, "published Sep 1 2026"; page flagged possible mismatch w/ different news item; investors incl. Katherine Boyle a16z, Tusk). Says platform powers ESA programs enrolling >80,000 students now; projects 235,000 students in 2026-27 = >$2B state funding. Founded 2021, 94 employees. POOL FILE SAYS ONLY $10M SERIES A - stale.
