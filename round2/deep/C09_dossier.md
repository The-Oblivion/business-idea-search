# C09 dossier: hold-forever Handwerk platform (idea P004 "Meisterwerk", name already taken)

Research date 2026-10-18. About 38 searches and fetches. FX: 1 EUR = 1.15 USD. "Read" means I opened the page or PDF. "Snippet" means I saw it only in a search-result summary, so treat it as weaker. [M] means from memory, not verified. "My arithmetic" marks a number I computed from sourced inputs plus a stated assumption.

## Verdict up front

The revenue path is real and proven, but the business is not differentiated. Four funded platforms already show that buying German Handwerk firms can reach EUR 180M-2B of revenue. The "hold forever, Meister equity ladder" angle is already being advertised by at least four other buyers. The unproven core, that half of a target's revenue is recurring statutory inspection and service work, has no source anywhere I looked. Reaching $100M of bought revenue yields only about EUR 4-8M of operating profit before financing (my arithmetic). Judgment: wounded, about 4.5/10. It survives as a capital-allocation business that needs a sponsor. It does not survive as a startup. The gate's "strong" is too generous.

## 2. Bottom-up market

This is a buying business, so the "pool" is sellers and what they cost, not payers.

- **Universe:** 569,898 Handwerk firms in the 2022 census. 80.2% have fewer than 10 people and 59.8% fewer than 5. https://www.zdh.de/ueber-uns/fachbereich-wirtschaft-energie-umwelt/statistik/handwerkszaehlung/handwerkszaehlung-2022/ (read).
- **Target trades (snippet):** about 63,900 electrical firms (end 2023, assignment unconfirmed) and 50,500 plumbing and heating firms, from ZDH series https://www.zdh.de/daten-fakten/betriebszahlen/handwerk-im-zeitverlauf/. The average electrical firm has about EUR 1.8M revenue (ZVEH, via https://elektro.net/file/show/159673/091e8f/DE_2024_12_55_BF31_R.pdf).
- **Funnel (my arithmetic):** 114,000 firms x about 20% with 10+ people (derived from the 80.2% figure) = about 23,000. x 33% planning a handover within 5 years (HWK Dortmund: https://www.hwk-do.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Sonderumfrage-Betriebsnachfolge-im-Handwerk-Fruehjahr-2026.pdf) = about 7,500, or about 1,500 a year. Twelve deals a year is under 1% of that. Share is not the constraint. Execution is.
- **Warning:** two-thirds of all handovers involve firms with up to 9 employees. https://bw.ermoeglicher.de/documents/2237/PM_Nachfolgemonitor_VDB.pdf (read). The firms that PE ignores are mostly too small to carry a Betriebsleiter and a share of holding costs. The gate's "sub-EUR 2M firms are open" is true but mostly because they are unattractive.
- **Seller-side shock:** Handwerk insolvencies hit 4,950 in 2025 (rate 7.3 per 1,000, highest in over a decade). https://www.zdh.de/daten-und-fakten/kennzahlen-des-handwerks/insolvenzen-und-insolvenzquoten-im-handwerk/ (read). Only 50.5% see a family solution as feasible. https://www.unternehmeredition.de/familieninterne-nachfolge-im-handwerk-wird-zum-auslaufmodell/ (read). Supply of sellers is ample.
- **Path to $100M:** 60 firms x EUR 1.5M = EUR 90M ($103.5M). Arithmetic holds. All of it is acquired, none is built.

## 3. Evidence of demand and willingness to pay

Statutory duties are real, and they are the best part of the thesis:
- Passenger lifts need a ZUeS main inspection at least every 2 years plus an interim inspection, and the operator cannot extend the interval. https://www.tuvsud.com/de-de/indust-re/betriebssicherheitsverordnung-info/betriebssicherheitsverordnung-aufzug (snippet).
- DGUV V3 requires periodic electrical testing. Intervals for fixed installations are only a 4-year guide value and for mobile equipment are risk-based. https://forum-verlag.com/fachwissen/elektrosicherheit-und-elektrotechnik/prueffristen-nach-dguv-v3/ (snippet).
- Fire alarm systems need annual maintenance by certified staff, with detector replacement every 5 or 8 years. https://www.baunetzwissen.de/brandschutz/fachwissen/brandmeldeanlagen/wartung-und-instandhaltung-von-bma-3183031 (snippet).
- 21.5M heating systems exist, 4M older than 30 years (BDH, via https://vdiv.de/news-details/vier-millionen-heizungen-in-deutschland-sind-aelter-als-30-jahre, snippet). German technical-service providers grew 9.6% in 2025 inside a EUR 68.7B facility-services market. https://www.luenendonk.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/LUE_PI_FS_DE_Studie_2026_f-1.pdf (snippet).

**The missing proof:** I found no source on what share of a micro electrical or plumbing firm's revenue is recurring service. The only splits I found are new build plus renovation at roughly 31-33% of electrical revenue, and customers 39% private, 35% commercial, 13.5% public, 11.7% housing firms. https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=368992 (snippet). Two undated SBZ articles on plumbing service contracts conflict (about 15-year versus about 4-year average duration). Private customers are low-value, churn-prone and price-sensitive. The gate's "at least 50% recurring statutory inspection" filter is a hope, not a datapoint. It plausibly holds for lift and fire service firms, and probably not for typical installers. Those service specialists are exactly where PE (svt, Waterland) is already buying.

**Contract-book retention:** the VDB monitor says about 65% of acquired firms reach or exceed prior revenue after handover, so about a third do not. (read, link above.)

## 4. Unit economics and analogs

- **Price:** FINANCE realised-deal corridor for Bau/Handwerk under EUR 20M revenue is 0.25-0.50x revenue. Electrical/digital industry is 0.75-1.15x. Mittelstand planners ask 1.2x on average and 0.6x at the median. https://www.kfw.de/PDF/Download-Center/Konzernthemen/Research/PDF-Dokumente-Fokus-Volkswirtschaft/Fokus-2026/Fokus-Nr.-526-Januar-2026-Nachfolge-Monitoring.pdf (read, page 6). Handwerk owners expect an average of EUR 453k, median EUR 375k, about 87% of revenue. https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=374071 (read).
- **Margin:** I found no good Handwerk benchmark. The 2024 Busch-Jaeger operating comparison says electrical profitability "remains at a low level" (snippet, elektro.net link above). The idea's 6-9% operating margin is therefore unverified. Assumed in this section.
- **What the buyer earns (my arithmetic):** At 0.5x revenue and 7.5% EBIT, price is 6.7x EBIT. After about 2.5% of revenue in holding and back-office cost (my assumption), EBIT is 5% and price is 10x. After about 30% German tax (my assumption) that is a 7% unlevered yield on price. If sellers hold to 0.87x revenue, the yield falls to about 4%. Two gates matter: the price must stay under about 0.6x, and the 7.5% margin must be after paying a market-rate Betriebsleiter.
- **Contrast with the PE logic:** the buy-small, sell-big arbitrage (US home services: 2-3.5x seller earnings for micro firms, 5-8x EBITDA for $3-10M revenue firms, 9-13x for platforms, 17-20x exit claimed) is how Konzmann and HWP investors made money. https://beancount.io/blog/2026/07/11/hvac-plumbing-private-equity-roll-up-guide and https://digital.bnpmedia.com/article/OPINION/4994555/847866/article.html (snippets, blog-quality). A hold-forever buyer gives up that exit. Stanford search-fund data show 6.2-7.0x EBITDA entry and 33.9-35.1% IRR, but 26% of deals lose capital. https://www.papermark.com/blog/search-fund-guide (snippet). A permanent holder's return is yield plus modest growth, roughly 7-10% unlevered. That is LP-acceptable only for an insurer, Versorgungswerk or family office.

## 5. Capital needs

- Purchase consideration: EUR 36-54M at 0.4-0.6x of EUR 90M, or about EUR 78M at 0.87x (my arithmetic). Add working capital, a Betriebsleiter successor per firm, and holding overhead. The gate's EUR 15M seed covers about 2 years of deals at half-upfront.
- Debt is the soft spot. Buergschaftsbanken cap guarantees at EUR 2M per borrower since 2023 (period to end-2027). https://www.deutsche-handwerks-zeitung.de/?p=273451 (snippet). KfW 077 is a loan to natural persons within 5 years of starting self-employment, which a holding platform is not (gate's read of https://www.kfw.de/PDF/Download-Center/F%C3%B6rderprogramme-(Inlandsf%C3%B6rderung)/PDF-Dokumente/Produktinfos/6000005231_Produktinfo_077.pdf). Realistic plan: EUR 50-100M of sponsor equity plus bank debt on EUR 5M+ EBITDA, which is institutional capital. I found no hold-forever Handwerk fund to benchmark against.

## 6. Regulatory and liability landscape

- **Premise holds:** a GmbH or partnership is entered in the Handwerksrolle if its Betriebsleiter qualifies (Meister, engineer, certain others). https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hwo/__7.html (read).
- **The bite:** an employed Betriebsleiter needs a real employment contract, normally full-time, genuine technical control, "appropriate" pay (collective-agreement benchmark), and leading several firms is allowed only in exceptions. https://www.hwk-do.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Anerkennung-von-Betriebsleitern.pdf and https://www.hwk-ulm.de/wp-content/uploads/Merkblatt-Betriebsleiter.pdf (snippets). Sham arrangements are void (BAG, case number unverified). So every acquired A-trade firm needs its own qualified person once the old owner leaves after 2-3 years. Whether a tuck-in can run as a branch of a hub under one Betriebsleiter is unresolved. The chamber decides case by case. This is the binding constraint, and it is also the "Meister equity ladder" rationale.
- **Meister supply:** about 20,040 Meister exams passed across all Handwerk in 2019 (https://ice.dzhw.eu/portal/de/Tabelle-2.7.14.pdf, snippet). I found no 2024 figure for electrical or plumbing. 60 firms need 60 Meister over 5 years, which is small nationally but hard locally.
- **Deal structure [M]:** sole proprietors need asset deals. Asset deals move staff by section 613a BGB and need customer consent to assign contracts. Share deals keep history liabilities (warranty claims) inside the GmbH. Customer-list sale raises GDPR questions. None of this was verified this run.
- **Heating demand backdrop:** the Gebaeudemodernisierungsgesetz passed on 10 Jul 2026 and drops the 65% renewable rule. Heat-pump sales still rose 40% in H1 2026 (194,500 units) while subsidy per job falls EUR 750 every half-year. https://www.pv-magazine.de/2026/08/11/waermepumpen-sind-aktuell-wichtigster-wachstumstreiber-im-heizungsmarkt/ (snippet). Policy risk for plumbing and heating firms is live.

## 7. Is the "why now" real?

Partly used up and partly real.
- **Real:** Handwerk sellers outnumber buyers (54,000 planned closures, 49,000 handovers over two years), distress is at a decade high, and the VDB says Handwerk succession problems are especially acute.
- **Used up:** institutional buyers of 20-60-person firms are active in electrical, TGA and fire (Konzmann/KLAR, HWP/GRIP, Builtech/VDK, svt, Waterland, Parcom). The only gap is sub-EUR 2M firms, which is where price expectations and owner dependence are worst.
- **The 12 months matter:** Builtech/VDK and Konzmann/KLAR both changed hands in the past year. That means large pools of capital are now chasing Handwerk add-ons, which will raise prices for exactly the firms this platform wants.

## 8. Do the upside-case assumptions hold?

| Assumption | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Buy at 0.4-0.6x, half deferred | Plausible only for sellers with no alternative. Seller ask is 0.87x average and rising competition lifts it. Partly holds. |
| At least 50% recurring statutory revenue | Unverified. No source found. Likely true for lift and fire service, unlikely for installers. This is the make-or-break test. |
| 6-9% operating margin | Unverified. Profitability described as low. Insolvency rate 7.3 per 1,000 in 2025. |
| 12 deals a year | Konzmann did about 2 a year. Even PE-backed VDK averaged about 9 a year over 12 years (my arithmetic from 107 entities since 2013). A seed-funded entrant doing 12 a year with a Betriebsleiter for each is optimistic. |
| EUR 15M seed | Too small by a factor of 3-6. |
| Hold-forever as counter-positioning | Already advertised by others. Cheap to copy. |
| Meister equity ladder | Legally workable (employed Betriebsleiter). I found no precedent in Germany. Real design risk. |
| $100M revenue means a big business | Revenue is bought. About EUR 4-8M of operating profit at $100M of revenue (my arithmetic), before financing. |

## 9. Kill tests (cheapest first)

1. Get anonymised ledgers for 6-8 target firms and measure recurring share and 3-year customer retention. Stop if recurring revenue is under 40%.
2. Three non-binding term sheets at 0.6x or lower with half deferred. Stop if sellers insist on over 0.8x in cash.
3. Ask two Handwerkskammern in writing whether a tuck-in can be a branch under a hub Betriebsleiter. Stop if each acquired firm needs its own full-time Meister.
4. Five conversations with Versorgungswerk, insurer or family-office CIOs. Stop if none accepts a 7-10% unlevered yield with no exit.

## Scorecard (my judgment)

Size 6, defensibility 3, evidence of demand 5, capital-feasibility 4, why-now 4. Overall about 4.5/10. Better than the round-1 finalists on size, worse on moat. Not dead, not strong.
