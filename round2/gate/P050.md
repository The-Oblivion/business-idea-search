# Gate report: pool P050, India gold-backed loans (banks, NBFCs, informal pawnbrokers)

Gate date: 2026-10-18. Stance: skeptical by default. 8 ideas, 24 searches (standard mode) plus one page fetch (Vinod Kothari commentary on the RBI Directions). Result: **5 kill, 3 weak, 0 strong. No picks.**

Evidence caveat: the RBI "Lending Against Gold and Silver Collateral Directions, 2025" text itself was not fetched. Paragraph-level claims (paras 17, 20, 27, 30, 34, 35, 37-43) rest on the idea authors' reading plus secondary summaries (indialaw, Vinod Kothari, Grant Thornton). Search results came back as summaries, so quantities are marked "(unverified)" where only one weak source supports them.

## Headline finding

The pool is real (about $215B of loans, roughly $20B a year of interest) and the RBI Directions did create mandatory spend. But the eight ideas share three flaws that round 1's lessons warned about:

1. **The stress catalyst is weaker than the pitches say.** Several ideas (Bullion-Bid, Renewal Wall, parts of Karat Ledger and Switch Desk) lean on "gold 23% below its January peak". At mid-October 2026, MCX/retail 24K gold is about INR 1.478 lakh per 10 g against a 29 Jan spike of about INR 1.79-1.81 lakh, so **about 18% below, not 23%**; the 22-27% figure was the June trough, and gold printed about INR 1.63 lakh on 24 Aug. Muthoot's gross stage III fell to 2.28% in Q1 FY27 (from 2.35% a quarter earlier and 2.58% a year earlier). No auction wave is visible. And para 17 values collateral at the lower of the 30-day average and the previous day, which means the one-day 29 Jan spike barely entered any loan's valuation.
2. **Every lender-facing layer already has an in-house team, a vendor, or a bank-owned fintech arm.** Lenders run image-based audit and surprise-audit teams; at least five gold-loan LMS vendors sell collateral tracking and auction workflows; Axis built its UPI gold credit line through its own Freecharge subsidiary; lenders run their own balance-transfer products and e-auctions.
3. **The only genuine $100M routes are balance-sheet businesses** (Girvi Formalizer), which are capital-heavy ($95M equity plus $550M debt for about $130M of gross interest income), face a legal premise I could not confirm (state pawnbroker rate caps), and sit next to a base rate (Rupeek: nine years, $164-210M raised, own book of INR 236 crore) that says the informal-to-formal thesis scales slowly.

## 1. Girvi Formalizer: weak (median $15M, upside $60M)

**Competitors and close analogs.** No pawnbroker-conversion programme found. But the buyers of gold books are many and funded: L&T Finance bought Paul Merchants' gold business (INR 1,350 crore book, 130 branches, about 700 staff) for INR 537 crore in 2025 ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/06/09/dcm14-biz-briefs.html)); InCred bought TruCap's gold business (115 branches, INR 650 crore) ([The Week](https://www.theweek.in/wire-updates/business/2025/02/25/dcm88-biz-incred-gold.html)); Tata Capital took about 88.6% of Yogakshemam in July 2026 and Godrej Capital took Kanakadurga ([Outlook Business](https://www.outlookbusiness.com/news/gold-loans-are-exploding-why-nbfcs-are-scrambling-for-a-bigger-slice), [AskFuzz](https://askfuzz.ai/discover/news/banking/tata-godrej-enter-indias-booming-gold-loan-market)). Muthoot, Manappuram and Kosamattam are themselves ex-pawnbroker businesses.

**Substitutes.** A retiring pawnbroker can sell to any of those buyers, or become a franchisee (IIFL lists franchise branches in Tamil Nadu, [IIFL Capital](https://locate-us.iiflcapital.com/franchisee/iifl-capital-financial-institutions-thiruverkadu-tiruvallur-508557/Map)) or a sourcing agent.

**Revenue math.** 1,000 shops x INR 6 crore = INR 6,000 crore ($645M) x 20% = about $130M of gross interest income. That needs about $95M equity and $550M bank debt (pool's figures) and 200 conversions a year. Rupeek's informal-to-formal attempt: 9 years, $164-210M raised, own book of INR 236 crore (pool figures; Caplight lists about $209.6M raised).
- Median: about 200 shops x INR 5 crore = INR 1,000 crore ($107M) x 19% = about $20M, haircut for slower ramp = **$15M**.
- Upside: 500 shops x INR 6 crore = INR 3,000 crore ($320M) x 20% = **about $60M**. What has to go right: (a) a recurring supply of willing sellers (unproven; no seller-count data), (b) ticket books pass diligence (loss under 0.5%, redemption above 85%, both untested), (c) a capital raise of $50M+ equity within three years (plausible only if (b) is proven), (d) bank lines at 10-11% (plausible). Reaching $100M needs about 1,000 shops, which is more than top-decile execution supports.

**Legal and premise check.** The savings story ("half the informal rate") rests on informal rates of 24-48%. For *licensed* pawnbrokers in Tamil Nadu (32% of the national market; five southern states about 75%) the Pawnbrokers Act caps interest at a state-notified rate tied to RBI's bank rate, and the 1992 amendment says that rate cannot exceed bank rate plus 5 points ([1992 amendment](https://www.casemine.com/act/in/5a9cce174a93265347813203), [1979 amendment](https://www.casemine.com/act/in/5a9cce174a93265347813205)). I could not find the current notified rate. If licensed shops charge near that ceiling, a 18-22% NBFC loan is *dearer*, and the 24-48% only describes unlicensed or fee-padded lending, which is the book least likely to pass diligence. An unincorporated pawnbroker is outside the RBI perimeter, so an asset or business purchase probably avoids change-of-control approval (my inference); buying a dormant NBFC does need RBI prior approval (about 7 months in one reported case).

**Deciding reason.** Big pool and no direct competitor, but the premise needs the unverified informal-rate claim, capital needs are heavy, and a competent upside is about $60M.

## 2. Bullion-Bid: kill (median $5M, upside $40M)

**Competitors.** iGOLD (Bangalore, founded 2017) already runs online auctions of pledged gold for banks and NBFCs and aggregates KYC'd jewellers ([Gust listing](https://gust.com/companies/igold); the listing says prototype stage with 7 staff and may be stale). Banks already route e-auctions through "approved auction service providers" (IndusInd notice; a West Bengal Gramin bank's Oct 2024 notice names a third-party gold auction portal). Shriram and Muthoot publish their own digital/auction processes ([Shriram](https://www.shriramfinance.in/articles/gold-loan/2026/how-the-digital-gold-loan-auction-process-works), [Muthoot](https://www.muthootfinance.com/blog/what-is-the-process-of-gold-loan-auction)). AGLC supplies a standard auction notice.

**Substitutes.** Lender-staff auctions with an empanelled auctioneer; local jewellers; refiners' scrap networks.

**Revenue math.** The pool's own stress scenario needs 3% of the stock at notice stage and a 40% share of it, plus part-sales and scrap, to reach about $100M, and says a normal year is a quarter of that. Muthoot's stage III is falling, so the stress year is not visible. Median: 1% of the $215B stock auctioned a year ($2.2B) x 20% share x 2% take = about $9M, haircut for lender caution = **$5M**. Upside: a stress year plus a scrap-marketplace extension = **about $40M**. What has to go right: (a) an actual stress year (gold at 18% below peak and recovering argues against), (b) 40% of the flow (lenders keep control of their own auctions), (c) an uplift that lenders will pay for, since the rules already set a 90% reserve floor.

**Legal check.** The first auction must be physical in the branch's district, which limits remote bidders until a second attempt (secondary sources; not verified against the text). Lender-affiliated ownership is barred from bidding (para 42 per the pitch).

**Deciding reason.** Small in every case except a stress year that the data does not show.

## 3. Karat Ledger: weak (median $10M, upside $60M)

**Evidence for the problem.** 2026 appraiser-collusion cases are real: a Bank of Baroda appraiser swapped gold across 174 accounts (about INR 10 crore, July 2026), an NBFC appraiser created 683 fake accounts in Delhi, five bank-approved appraisers were held in Chennai (Canara) ([Outlook Money](https://www.outlookmoney.com/personal-finance/gold-loan-fraud-bank-appraiser-accused-of-siphoning-off-gold-ornaments-worth-rs-10-crore), [DT Next](https://www.dtnext.in/news/chennai/five-more-gold-appraisers-held-in-chennai-for-cheating-canara-bank)). I found no shared appraiser registry, which supports the pitch's gap claim.

**Competitors and substitutes.**
- In-house audit: Aditya Birla Capital advertises an offsite gold-loan audit team doing image-based audit, continuous monitoring with analytics, trigger-based surprise audits and a surprise-auditor team on valuation and purity ([ABCL posting](https://www.adityabirlacapital.com/careers/sitecore/content/abcl/abcareers/home/jobs/job-details/team-member--gold-loan-audit-offsite--mumbai-ia-gold-loan-in-maharashtra-jobs-abg106807)). The pitch's "audit has a mandate and no tool" is wrong for large lenders.
- Third-party gold audits already exist as audit-firm engagements ([IIFL explainer](https://www.iifl.com/blogs/gold-loan/how-third-party-auditors-verify-pledged-gold-inventory-at-nbfcs)).
- LMS vendors with collateral records, images and auction workflows: Nelito FinCraft (used by Manappuram), Newgen, Finezza, Intelligrow, CloudHouse ([Nelito](https://www.nelito.com/lending-solutions/fincraft-gold-loan-management-software-solution.html), [Newgen brochure](https://landing.newgensoft.com/hubfs/_2020%20Website%20files/PES%20Website/Brochure/Gold%20Loan%20(NBFCs).pdf)).
- Hardware is a commodity: Godrej Accugold EDXRF ([Godrej](https://godrejenterprises.com/p/godrej-locks-and-security-solutions/gold-testing-machine/accugold-gold-testing-machine)); Augmont piloted an AI purity machine at a Chennai gold-loan branch in June 2026, tied to its gold-loan tech platform (single search summary).

**Revenue math.** The pool's modules sum to about $340M at 100% share, so $100M needs about 35%. The fraud-warranty module is inflated: 5 bps of INR 26 lakh crore of originations is INR 1,300 crore a year, which would imply losses far above the INR 3.8-23 crore per case reported in 2026 (my estimate: realistic premium pool 0.1-0.5 bp, or $3-14M at 100% share). Median: surprise-verification and ledger sold to about 20 lenders at about $0.5M = **$10M**. Upside: system of record for about 25% of pledges plus per-loan fees plus a real warranty = **about $60M**. What has to go right: (a) large lenders outsource evidence they currently produce in-house (low plausibility), (b) cross-lender appraiser data sharing clears DPDP Act and competition law (uncertain), (c) an insurer fronts a warranty (plausible but small).

**Deciding reason.** Real mandate, wrong sizing and crowded by in-house teams; the pool itself calls it a wedge, not a stand-alone $100M business.

## 4. Gold Float: kill (median $3M, upside $25M)

**Already done.** Axis Bank launched "Credit on UPI with Gold Loans" with Freecharge on 29 Sep 2025: an overdraft against gold, interest only on the drawn amount, aimed at existing customers and MSMEs. Freecharge is a 100% Axis subsidiary, so the first mover built it in-house ([Axis press release](https://www.axis.bank.in/about-us/press-releases/axis-bank-launches-indias-first-gold-backed-credit-on-upi-powered-by-freecharge)).

**Legal premise.** UPI credit lines were opened to scheduled banks, then small finance banks (Dec 2024); I found no extension to NBFCs ([Mondaq](https://mondaq.com/india/financial-services/1365422/rbis-upi-expansion-with-credit-lines)). A Ken report says RBI wants to stop most NBFCs offering revolving credit ([The Ken](https://the-ken.com/story/the-rbis-revolving-credit-rule-treats-every-nbfc-like-a-bad-lender/)). Bullet consumption loans are capped at 12 months and renewals or top-ups need accrued interest paid first. I found no rule permitting revolving consumption lines against gold. So the addressable base is bank gold books (about INR 5.1 lakh crore, roughly $55B), not 9.03 crore accounts.

**Revenue math.** The pool's $102M needs 4.5M lines drawing $6.8B at 150 bps. In a bank-only base that is about 12% of all bank gold loans drawn through one program manager. Median: about $30B of serviceable bank gold loans (excluding Axis and other banks that build in-house), 1% drawn ($0.3B) x 1.0% = **$3M**. Upside: 5% of the whole $55B bank base drawn ($2.7B) x 1.0% = **$27M, call it $25M**. The author concedes the moat is weak.

**Deciding reason.** Already built by the best-placed bank, narrow regulatory perimeter, and small even in the upside.

## 5. Switch Desk: weak (median $6M, upside $50M)

**Competitors.** Lenders run their own balance transfers: Shriram, L&T Finance, Bajaj, IIFL all publish "gold loan transfer" pages (Shriram says the old lender can pay the new lender directly, so gold need not move) ([Shriram](https://www.shriramfinance.in/financial-faq-how-can-i-apply-for-a-gold-loan-balance-transfer), [L&T Finance](https://www.ltfinance.com/blog/gold-loan/gold-loan-transfer)). Rupeek is a business correspondent for banks. No outcome-priced neutral desk found.

**Premise check.** The gap is real but smaller: Muthoot about 16.3%, Manappuram about 16.6%, IIFL/Capri about 18.6% against SBI about 9.6% (a 7-9 point gap; the pool says 10-20 for NBFCs and "Muthoot earns about 20%"). Muthoot's FY26 margin looks like a peak and new entrants (Tata, Godrej, ABCL, Bajaj) are pushing NBFC prices down. Banks' retail gold book is already growing 105% a year without a desk, and Shriram's FAQ says banks lend a lower LTV (about 65% vs 70%), so a bank switch often shrinks the loan.

**Revenue math.** The pool's $100M needs 3.3M switches a year at $30, which is 2% of the stock moved annually through one desk. Median: 200k switches x $30 = **$6M**. Upside: a DSA-style channel on 15% of bank gold disbursals (about INR 6-7 lakh crore a year, my estimate) x 0.5% = **about $55M**; I use $50M. What has to go right: (a) borrowers pay a success fee (Indian consumers rarely do; the pool flags this), (b) banks pay 0.5-0.75% of principal for gold (unverified; gold is cheap for banks to source), (c) DPDP-compliant maturity triggers from bureau or Account Aggregator data (feasible).

**Deciding reason.** Real mechanism, but fees are DSA-sized and the whole upside is below $100M.

## 6. Co-Lend Exchange: kill (median $4M, upside $22M)

The author's own verdict is "dead as a stand-alone; keep as a module". The pool is the NBFC book only (INR 3.41 lakh crore). Its premise cites an 80:20 split, but the RBI Co-Lending Arrangements Directions 2025 took effect on 1 Jan 2026, cover banks (excluding SFBs, LABs and RRBs), AIFIs and NBFCs, and require each lender to retain at least 10% of every loan; 80:20 is the old 2020 model ([Taxmann](https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/rbi-permits-co-lending-between-regulated-entities-from-jan-2026-with-10-loan-retention/), [Vinod Kothari](https://vinodkothari.com/2025/08/rbi-issues-co-lending-directions-2025/)). Gold co-lending is bilateral today: Indel-IndusInd, Finkurve-Godrej, IIFL with Canara, DCB and DBS, Rupeek-Indian Bank, and IndusInd lists Fedbank, Yogakshemam and Indel ([IndusInd](https://www.indusind.bank.in/in/en/personal/product/financial-inclusion/co-lending.html)). Yubi Co.Lend already matches banks to NBFCs as a platform (gold is not on its listed products) ([Yubi profile](https://altss.com/profile/yubi)). At 35 bps, $100M needs $28.6B co-lent, 45% of the FY28 NBFC book. Median: 2% of the FY28 NBFC book ($1.2B) co-lent through the exchange x 35 bps = **about $4M**. Upside: 10% ($6.2B) x 35 bps = **about $22M**. Deciding reason: small even in the upside, and platforms plus bilateral deals already occupy it.

## 7. Renewal Wall Cure Desk: kill (median $2M, upside $15M)

**False premise.** The pitch values loans at the 29 Jan spike (INR 1.81 lakh) and says a 75% loan "is about 97% now". But para 17 values at the lower of the 30-day average and the previous day, and the spike was a one-day move (up INR 17,900 on 29 Jan, then down 12.4% by 8 Feb). The 30-day average at end-January was about INR 1.45-1.50 lakh (my estimate from the daily prints), against about 1.478 lakh now, so late-January loans have drifted 0-3%. The Aug cohort (valued near 1.63 lakh) drifts up to about 9%. At an 85% cap that is a 0-10% shortfall on loans written at maximum LTV, not the 12-23% the pitch uses. Lenders say short tenor lets them fix this at renewal, and Muthoot's stage III is falling. The pool's own kill trigger is gold above about INR 1.7 lakh; one set of aggregator prints had gold at about INR 1.63 lakh in late August (prints conflict), within about 4% of it.

**Competitors and substitutes.** Lender collections teams, agencies, and the lenders' own renewal reminders. The author concedes lenders may do this in-house and that an NBFC earns interest on delay. A pay-down credit from a lender affiliate may look like evergreening (author's note).

**Revenue math.** The pool's $86M comes from a guessed 10% of the stock needing a cure event. Median: 0.5% of the stock needing a cure ($1.1B) x 20% handled x 1.0% = **about $2M**. Upside: 2% ($4.3B) x 25% handled x 1.2% = **about $13M, call it $15M**, and only if gold falls again.

**Deciding reason.** False premise and a 2027-H1 window; this is lessons item 6 (why-now already used up).

## 8. Gold Bay: kill (median $3M, upside $25M)

**Premise.** The storage rule is not new: RBI's 2013 NBFC circular already barred opening a branch without suitable storage and a vault, and bank tenders show strong rooms are ordinary RCC construction (100-150 sq ft to IS 15369 in an SBI tender). I found no published cost for a compliant strong room, so the pitch's INR 9 lakh a year is an unsupported assumption ([Business Standard on the 2013 circular](https://www.business-standard.com/amp/article/finance/rbi-tightens-norms-for-gold-nbfcs-113091700551_1.html), [SBI tender](https://sbi.co.in/documents/39129/51516783/NOTICE+INVITING+TENDER20250710093458228.pdf)).

**Competitors and substitutes.** Lenders fit out leased premises themselves; vault and security vendors such as Godrej Security Solutions sell the room; entrants buy branches rather than build (L&T bought 130 branches with Paul Merchants, InCred 115 with TruCap, Tata and Godrej bought small NBFCs). Muthoot guides 200-300 new branches in FY27, and Bajaj added 138 gold branches in one quarter (pool figure), which does not look like an industry blocked by build time. Under paras 29-31 a leased room that the lender fits out and staffs is simply a normal branch lease, so there is no service that only a bay company can sell.

**Revenue math.** $100M needs about 10,300 bays at $9.7k, which the author puts at roughly three years of the whole entrant wave at 100% share. Median: 300 bays = **$3M**. Upside: 30% of about 9,000 entrant branches over FY27-29 = 2,700 bays x $9.7k = **about $26M**; I use $25M.

**Deciding reason.** Commodity construction service, no moat, and $100M requires a share the market cannot supply.

## What this pool taught

Regulatory shocks create *mandated* spend, but large lenders convert it into in-house teams and existing vendors within a year. In a pool where incumbents are strong and bank-funded fintechs (Axis/Freecharge) move first, lender-facing software and services top out at tens of millions; only owning loans scales, and that is a capital and legal-structure problem rather than a software one.
