## Title I Reborn  (KEY KILL EVIDENCE)
- Ginnie Mae Apr 2026 RPB report: Manufactured Housing MBS remaining principal balance = $92 MILLION (down from $131M Apr 2024; falling every month, zero new issuance). https://www.ginniemae.gov/data_and_reports/reporting/Monthly%20UPB%20Reports/Apr26_RPB.pdf (downloaded & read)
- 24 CFR Part 201: Title I insurance coverage capped at 10% of lender's portfolio via insurance coverage reserve account; pays max 90% of loss up to reserve; claim reduced by appraised value; FHA can contest claim for 2 yrs. https://syfert.com/cfr/sections/24-201.1.html ; 2006 Senate testimony archives.hud.gov/testimony/2006/test040406b.cfm
- ROAD to Housing Act PL 119-101 (enacted 11 Jul 2026) Sec 303 = Title I modernization (raise limits, max 30yr term, lease flexibility; indexing methods by 11 Jul 2027) — so NOT pending, already law; but no change to 10% cap mentioned in CRS summary.
