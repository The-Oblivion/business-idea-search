- Existing governance competitors that already do invoice validation, service credits, earn-backs for IT-services contracts: Avasant AvaSense managed governance ("validate and reconcile supplier invoices", "service credits, charge backs, earnbacks"; case study up to 25% savings across IT services portfolio) https://avasant.com/what-we-do/contract-supplier-governance/ ; ISG GovernX (vendor performance, contract compliance, invoice review for billing errors, duplicate charges, missed credits) via search result; ServiceNow Vendor Management Workspace (SLA KPI tracking, automated service credit calc, per SoftwareOne listing) https://platform.softwareone.com/product/vendor-management-workspace/PCP-0739-5645 ; BlueOptima pitches "vendor management" + "outsourcing suppliers" breakdown of coding effort https://www.blueoptima.com/blueoptima-what-and-why (funding unclear, headcount 30-105 conflicting).
- ISG $17M governance deal VERIFIED but reframed: "up to $17M", multiyear, up to 8 years, $300M tech spend, 200 vendors, ~ $2M/yr => ~0.7% of spend. That is the observed market price of a full governance service. https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/11/isg-iii-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript/ . So Meter's ask (20% x 4% + $200k on $47M = ~1.2% of spend) is above the observed benchmark; and the 'governance budget line' is ~0.5-1% of spend not 1.2%.
- "25% of contracts renegotiated each year" is Cognizant CFO FORECAST (Nomura call Sept 2025; past 15-20%/yr; expects 25%; 20-25% productivity-linked savings), secondary source Nearshore Americas https://nearshoreamericas.com/?p=106554 . Not a measured rate.
