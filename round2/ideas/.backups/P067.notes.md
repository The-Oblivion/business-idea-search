## Thinking so far (2026-10-18)
- Key structural insight (earned secret): 5-yr auto-renew + termination fee + loss charge exist because custom-embroidered per-wearer garments are STRANDED ASSETS if the customer leaves. A pooled/standard garment fleet (MEWA-style in Europe; CHEP pallets analog) removes the stranded-asset risk => incumbents cannot copy without giving up lock-in revenue. TO CHECK: MEWA US presence.
- 'Ace Hardware for independent launderers': co-op/wholesaler (garments, linens, chemicals, RFID, financing, national-account contracts) vs. merged Cintas+UniFirst. TO CHECK: existing buying groups.
- Kill list so far: contract-audit standalone (consultants exist, ~$79M at 100% share), hospital-linen-only (<$3B), FR jobsite laundering (<$0.3B), cleanroom (<$1B).
