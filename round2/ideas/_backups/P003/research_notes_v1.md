# P003 research notes (append-only, v1) -- written as I go so nothing is lost
Date: 2026-10-18. Pool: England social landlords repairs (Awaab's Law).

## Batch 1 (4 searches)
- Awaab's Law Phase 2 takes effect 30 NOV 2026 (not Oct). Adds electrical, falls/trips, fire/explosion, excess cold, excess heat, structural collapse, domestic hygiene incl pests. (MHCLG confirmed; sources: jmw.co.uk blog; bpca.org.uk; clydeco.com/en/insights/2026/08; plentific.com blog). Phase 3 in 2027 = remaining HHSRS hazards.
- Clocks: emergency 24h investigate+make safe; significant hazard: investigate within 10 working days; written summary within 3 working days of investigation; safety works within 5 working days of investigation concluding; longer works start within 12 weeks; alt accommodation at landlord expense if cannot be made safe.
- Tenants can sue directly: implied term s10A Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 (breach of contract claim) -- separate from RSH + Ombudsman routes.
- Disrepair claims evidence (council FOIs, not national): Hackney 2024/25 ~GBP4.6M legal+compensation, 508 claims, ~GBP4.5k per case (3.6M of that = legal fees for third parties). Lambeth damages 2020/21-2024/25 GBP8.4M, claims 459 -> 1,164 per yr; 17 London boroughs GBP31.8M since 2020 (16,300 claims). Legal Futures: claims vs councils up ~400%. Housing Ombudsman: 26,901 interventions Apr24-Mar25; repairs complaints +43%; GBP5.4M compensation ordered, >40% for leaks/damp/mould. No national total found.
- Plentific (competitor, repairs marketplace + SaaS): raised >GBP100M total (Series C $100M 2021 Highland Europe/Brookfield); social clients incl Lambeth (33k homes), Guinness, PA Housing, Settle, Concept (Mar 2024). Revenue = subscription + commission on marketplace jobs. Claims up to GBP1.8B annual sector savings (own projection).
- Switchee: GBP5M Jul 2024 (Octopus Ventures + AXA IM Alts), earlier GBP6.5M May 2023; devices in tens of thousands of homes at 130+ providers; target 1M social homes; Mould iQ May 2025, 3 HAs 92k homes.

## Batch 2 (4 searches)
- Damp prevalence: EHS 2023-24 damp in 7% of social rented homes (up 3pts since 2019); RICS cites 11% damp or mould (broader); RSH 2023: 3-4% 'notable' damp/mould = up to 160,000 homes; ~8,000 families 'very serious'. Non-decent 17% of social homes.
- Govt IA 'vastly underestimated' Awaab cost (Inside Housing); LGA wants full re-assessment. Lambeth: Awaab's Law could raise damp+mould repair spend 10-40% (~GBP3M/yr); Lambeth had 347 damp+mould cases in first 3 weeks (Brixton Buzz Jan 2026) on ~33k homes. Repairs+maintenance across 200 RPs GBP8.8B in 2023-24.
- VOIDS (HouseMark via Archangel CEO): ~300,000 social homes void in 2023-24; >GBP1B repairs on voids; +GBP220M re-letting; total GBP1.6B incl lost rent. Avg re-let 74 days all voids, ~50 days standard voids (was <30 days five years earlier). Milton Keynes avg void works GBP8,590, loses 4-5 wks rent. Councils' void rent loss 1-2.6%.
- Council HRA repairs ~GBP2.46B (2024/25 budget, 2022/23 prices; CIH-hosted MHCLG Table 70 -- verify). So pool: HA GBP10B (3.9 capitalised) + council HRA repairs GBP2.5B revenue + council major works.
- Surveyor capacity: no workforce census found; indirect: winter bottleneck, landlords letting frameworks for damp/mould surveyors; HA hiring (Karbon, Lewisham). Councils HHSRS: only 1/3 of complaints get inspection (NRLA).
