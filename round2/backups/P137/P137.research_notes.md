## Working arithmetic (mine, to verify)

FACTS FROM NBER WP 35683 (Turner, Aug 2026) - READ IN FULL, sections IV.A-D:
- Gap ~$8B/yr, ~370k borrowers. Current annual graduate PRIVATE origination ~$1.4B; market must grow 6-7x to substitute (p.19).
- Sallie Mae ~64% of overall private student lending, originated $7.4B in 2025 (all private ed). Wells Fargo and Discover exited. Citizens, PNC, Navy Federal active. Nonbank: SoFi (national bank charter 2022), Earnest (Navient; bank partners FinWise, One American Bank), Ascent, College Ave, Monogram LLC. Specialty: MPOWER (400+ partner institutions; intl/DACA/refugee), Ascent outcomes-based product, Funding U.
- Bank capital: student loans 100% risk weight (~10.5% capital) vs 50% for mortgages. SLABS issuance only $6.5-9.3B/yr, nearly all refinancing existing loans. CHICKEN-AND-EGG: ABS investors need performance data on the specific underwriting model; lenders can't get data without scale; fix = standardized new-loan performance reporting, pilots with built-in evaluation, early-stage credit enhancement (mechanisms "undefined").
- ECOA: field-level pricing defensible; institution-within-field pricing = real disparate-impact exposure. Earnest paid MA AG $2.5M (AI model used cohort default rates + institution variables). No statutory safe harbor.
- Field data (PEER Center x College Scorecard): ~80% of dentistry borrowers and 62% of med students exceed their cap (medians $115-173k at 4 yrs); PT: 55% exceed $20.5k cap, $3.2B displaced volume, $80k median; nursing ~$2.9B; counseling psych ~$1.6B ($60-70k); social work, clinical psych similar. Law gradient $16.3k earnings per 10pp more above-cap share; MBA $12.8k. In SW/counseling/PT the gradient is flat (NYU MSW earns ~ state-school MSW).
- Institutions: 3 models in the wild = (1) rate buydown (school pays per-loan fee for program-wide rate), (2) risk-share (school absorbs share of defaults, sometimes funded escrow), (3) lender-of-last-resort (school pays origination fee on $ lent to otherwise-denied students). Legal/admin costs "non-trivial"; "thousands of institutions with variable programs" = lender's negotiation burden. Kansas Law J-HELPS 5% from endowment; WashU Law 7.5%. HEOA code of conduct: preferred lender lists need >=3 unaffiliated lenders, disclose criteria, vary order.
- Perkins (1958-2017): 1,500+ institutions ran revolving funds, ~$2.8B/yr at peak, institution put in 10%; expiry dismantled institutional lending infrastructure.
- State programs: CHESLA MyCHESLA Grad Loan (May 2026, 5.50-7.99% fixed, no risk tiers), RISLA expanded, Minnesota SELF Grad Loan, MEFA nationwide (expanded 2026-27), Bank of North Dakota. Tax-exempt bonds / state capital; need state nexus; limits too low for pro programs.
