## 1. Biggest shifts (last 12-18 months)

- **Regulation turned "trust" into a compliance line item, with dates.**
  - EU AI Act Article 50 applies from **2 Aug 2026**. Providers must mark synthetic audio, image, video and text in a machine-readable, detectable way. Deployers must disclose deepfakes. AI text on public-interest matters needs disclosure unless a human edited it. ([Art. 50](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/))
  - Penalties reach EUR 15M or 3% of turnover for most operator obligations. ([AI Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_Intelligence_Act)) A "Digital Omnibus" delay may have shifted some dates **[memory, unverified]**.
  - US TAKE IT DOWN Act: signed 19 May 2025. Platform notice-and-removal systems were due **19 May 2026**, with a 48-hour takedown clock and FTC enforcement. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Take_It_Down_Act))
  - FTC fake-review rule (final Aug 2024) bans AI-generated reviews, pay-for-sentiment reviews, undisclosed insider reviews, review suppression and bought followers or bots. Civil penalties apply to knowing violators. ([FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials))
- **Age assurance became mandatory infrastructure.**
  - UK Online Safety Act child-safety duties began **25 Jul 2025**.
  - US Supreme Court upheld Texas age verification 6-3 in *Free Speech Coalition v. Paxton* (Jun 2025).
  - Australia set an under-16 social media minimum with fines up to A$49.5M.
  - California signed the Digital Age Assurance Act (Oct 2025).
  - ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_verification_system))
- **Proof-of-human moved from crypto curiosity to consumer product.**
  - World had about 33M app users, roughly 15M of them iris-verified, by Sep 2025. It launched in the US on 1 May 2025 in six cities. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_(blockchain)))
  - Tinder began rolling out a World-backed "Verified Human" badge globally in **Apr 2026**. ([Tinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tinder_(app)))
  - Zoom introduced "World ID Deep Face" real-time verification in 2026. ([Deepfake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepfake))
  - Razer offers human-only game modes. Visa announced a debit-card project.
- **Provenance standards hit critical mass, yet remain leaky.**
  - Content Authenticity Initiative membership is above 5,000. TikTok joined the C2PA steering committee in **Jul 2026**. Google's Clement Wolf now chairs C2PA. ([CAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_Authenticity_Initiative))
  - Researchers have shown metadata stripping and fingerprint forging. C2PA proves origin, not truth.
- **Bots passed humans.** Fortune reported that over half of all web requests were bot-generated as of 2026. ([Dead Internet theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dead_Internet_theory)) Imperva put automated traffic at 51% for 2024 **[memory]**.
- **"Slop" was 2025 Word of the Year** at both Merriam-Webster and the American Dialect Society. A NYT investigation (Mar 2026) found about 40% of videos recommended to kids on YouTube appeared to be AI slop. ([AI slop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_slop))

## 2. Acute, quantified pain points

- **Deepfake fraud.**
  - One estimate puts losses at about $12B globally, heading to $40B within three years and $58.3B by 2030.
  - The average criminal deepfake incident costs over $500K. ([Deepfake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepfake))
  - Those figures cite industry projections, so the source quality is soft.
  - Arup lost about $25M to a deepfake video call in 2024 **[memory]**.
- **Scams at industrial scale.**
  - Chainalysis put crypto fraud at about $12.4B in 2024. Trafficked labor in Cambodia and Myanmar compounds numbers in the hundreds of thousands.
  - US Treasury sanctions came in Sep 2025. DOJ indicted Prince Group in Oct 2025. Chen Zhi was arrested and sent to China in Jan 2026. ([Pig butchering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pig_butchering_scam))
  - IC3 2024 reported about $16.6B in US losses, with victims over 60 hit hardest **[memory]**.
- **Review integrity is failing at the referee level.** Trustpilot removed 4.5M fake reviews in 2024 (90% automated, up from 2.2M in 2020). Italy fined it about $4.6M in **Mar 2026** for misleading users and failing to verify review authenticity. ([Trustpilot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trustpilot))
- **Streaming fraud.** Deezer estimated up to 70% of streams on AI-generated tracks were fraudulent (Jun 2025). One North Carolina man was charged over $10M in royalties from AI songs plus bots. ([AI slop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_slop))
- **False positives from KYC.** UK banks closed over 450,000 accounts by 2024, and removed services from over 140,000 small businesses in 2023. ([KYC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Know_your_customer))
- **Verification vendors are becoming the honeypot.** Breaches listed on the age-verification page: AU10TIX (2024), Discord/Zendesk (2025), Persona (2026), and IDScan (2026), where over **150M driver's licenses** were reportedly stolen. ([Age verification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_verification_system))
- **Hiring fraud and North Korean IT workers.** Fake candidates are a fast-growing problem **[memory]**.

## 3. Where money is flowing, and what is crowded

- **Compliance automation is the mature, well-funded lane.**
  - Vanta raised a $150M Series D (Jul 2025) at **$4.15B**. It reached about **$300M ARR** with over 16,000 customers by Apr 2026. It has bought vendor-review and AI risk-monitoring startups (TrustPage, Riskey). ([Vanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanta_(company)))
  - Drata and Secureframe compete with it. SOC 2 and ISO 27001 automation is saturated.
- **Identity verification and deepfake detection are hot but consolidating.** Persona, Reality Defender, Sumsub, Onfido/Entrust, Incode and Socure all sit here **[memory; no 2026 funding data retrieved]**.
- **World/Tools for Humanity** has raised about $250M through 2023 (a16z, Khosla). No newer valuation appeared in fetched sources.
- **Crowded:** generic deepfake detectors, SOC 2 tools, basic KYC and liveness checks, and "AI detector" apps.

## 4. Underserved gaps and structural blind spots

- **Legal fragility of biometric proof-of-personhood.**
  - Thailand ordered World to halt iris scans and delete data on 1.2M people (Nov 2025). Kenya ordered deletion (May 2025). The Philippines issued a cease-and-desist (Oct 2025). Spain, Portugal, Hong Kong and Indonesia suspended it earlier. ([World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_(blockchain)))
  - Non-biometric, credential-based, or social-graph human proofs have room where iris scanning is illegal.
- **Referees cannot referee themselves.** Trustpilot sells services to the businesses it rates and got fined. Platforms verifying identity must store sensitive data, which turns them into breach targets. Neither problem can be fixed from inside those firms.
- **Provenance is not truth.** C2PA cannot say whether content is accurate, and stripping metadata is trivial. Nobody owns "verified by a human editor at time T" as a portable, auditable claim.
- **Agents.** Agent authentication, liability for unauthorized agent transactions, and dispute resolution are described as unresolved. Singapore IMDA published an agentic-AI governance framework in Jan 2026. ([Agentic commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agentic_commerce)) World has been exploring AI-agent licensing through World ID since Jan 2025. Payment-network agent protocols (Visa, Mastercard) **[memory]**.
- **No portable trust for small players.** SMBs, freelancers, local businesses and seniors have no reusable verified-identity or verified-business credential. Big vendors monetize per check, so they have no incentive to make one reusable.
- **AI assurance is barely serviced.** The AI Act, state AI laws and ISO 42001 all create audit demand, and the audit supply is thin **[memory]**. Nearly 700 AI-related bills were introduced across 45 US states in 2024, up from 191 in 2023. ([Regulation of AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulation_of_artificial_intelligence))

## 5. Weird signals

1. **cURL killed its bug bounty (Jan 2026)** because AI-generated false vulnerability reports swamped it. Any market that pays for reported effort (bounties, grants, reviews, applications) needs a way to price real human effort. ([AI slop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_slop))
2. **Deezer's "70% of AI-track streams are fraud"** suggests AI content is becoming a fraud vector first and an art form second. Human-made certification for music, books and images is emerging.
3. **Verifier breaches in 2026 (Persona, IDScan, 150M licenses)** point to demand for verification that stores nothing, such as zero-knowledge or on-device checks. The privacy wallet may beat the verifier.
4. **Dating apps are the beachhead.** Tinder's global World-backed badge rollout (Apr 2026, after a 2025 Japan pilot) shows consumers accept a "verified human" badge when the risk is romance fraud. The next candidates are marketplaces, hiring, and gig platforms.
5. **Scam-compound crackdowns** (thousands released Jan 2026) may displace operations rather than end them. Watch the tools used against ordinary victims.
