# Briefing: Gaming, entertainment, live experiences, fandoms (as of 2026-09-28)

**Method caveat.** The session's WebSearch budget (200/200) was already spent, so I ran zero searches. This brief comes from about 35 WebFetch pulls, almost all Wikipedia snapshots current to Sept 2026. Newzoo and GamesIndustry.biz were blocked, and Pollstar is paywalled. I had no Reddit or forum access, so the pain points below come from documented events (lawsuits, regulator orders, breaches), not practitioner quotes. Figures are as those pages report them and are not independently verified. Anything marked *(inference)* is my reading, not a sourced fact.

## 1. Biggest shifts (last 12-18 months)
- **Hardware now gets more expensive over its life, because AI data centers are eating the RAM supply.** Valve's Steam Machine launched 2026-06-29 at $1,049 (512GB) and $1,349 (2TB), against an early target of about $800 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_Machine). Xbox Series prices rose on tariffs and RAM shortages, and the next Xbox ("Project Helix") is likely late 2027 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_(brand)). Switch 2 launched at $449.99 (June 2025) and goes to $499.99 on 2026-09-01. Some games are $80, and 23.68M units had sold by 2026-06-30 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nintendo_Switch_2).
- **Leveraged buyouts and mega-mergers are reshaping ownership.**
  - EA's $55B LBO closed 2026-08-04. PIF holds 93.4%, $20B is JPMorgan debt, and the owners plan to "reduce operating costs by incorporating AI throughout" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Arts).
  - Paramount Skydance's $110B all-cash deal for WBD won DOJ approval on 2026-06-12, and California settled on 2026-09-21 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Discovery).
  - Ubisoft took €1.16B from Tencent for 25% of Vantage Studios (a €4B valuation). Headcount fell from 18,666 (Sep 2024) to 16,590 (Mar 2026), FY26 net loss was €1.516B, and six games were cancelled in Jan 2026 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ubisoft).
- **GTA VI launches 2026-11-19 at $79.99.** Preorders opened 2026-06-25, day-one estimates run $1.2B+, and other publishers are rescheduling around it (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Theft_Auto_VI).
- **Age assurance is becoming mandatory plumbing for gaming and social platforms.**
  - Roblox required global age checks in Jan 2026, added age tiers ("Kids" under 9, "Select" 9-15) in June 2026, and was designated an EU Very Large Online Platform in Aug 2026 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roblox).
  - Discord announced mandatory face-scan or ID checks worldwide in Mar 2026, with rollout targeted for late Sept 2026 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discord).
  - The UK Online Safety Act set a 2025-07-25 enforcement deadline. VPN downloads surged (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_Safety_Act_2023).
- **Ticketing hit a regulatory reckoning.**
  - The FTC's all-in pricing rule took effect 2025-05-12. The FTC and 7 states sued Live Nation in Sept 2025 over resale, alleging $3.7B in resale fees from 2019 to 2024 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ticketmaster).
  - A federal jury found Live Nation liable for illegal monopolization on 2026-04-15. The Live Nation page also lists a DOJ settlement on 2026-03-09, and I could not reconcile the two, so the remedies are unclear (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_Nation_Entertainment).
  - Live Nation's 2025 revenue was $25.2B.
- **App-store fee walls are cracking.** Epic won an April 2025 ruling that Apple cannot charge fees on third-party storefronts. The Epic Store returned to US iOS in May 2025, and Epic charges 0% on a game's first $1M, then 12% (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epic_Games_Store).

## 2. Acute, quantified pain points
- **Games disappear when servers die, and buyers have no recourse.**
  - The Stop Killing Games EU petition got about 1.448M signatures, of which 1,294,188 were verified valid. The EU Commission declined a mandatory playability rule on 2026-06-16 and proposed a voluntary industry code of conduct by end of 2026.
  - *Anthem* shut down 2026-01-12. Of 738 tracked games, 68% are unplayable or at risk.
  - French group UFC-Que Choisir sued Ubisoft over *The Crew* (2026-03-31), and a suit was filed against Sony over PS5 physical-disc cessation (Aug 2026) (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stop_Killing_Games).
- **Identity data collected for compliance is itself a liability.**
  - Discord's Sept 2025 breach was claimed to have exposed 2.1M government ID photos, and 70,000 users were affected (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discord).
  - Photo age checks were beaten using *Death Stranding* character images (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_Safety_Act_2023).
  - Small studios and communities face the same mandates with none of the compliance budget *(inference)*.
- **Fans distrust ticket pricing and resale.** Oasis tickets rose from £135 to £350+ under dynamic pricing, and the UK CMA opened an investigation on 2024-09-05. In June 2026 the CMA ordered StubHub to refund 50,000+ customers for drip pricing. StubHub's World Cup 2026 tickets failed to reach buyers, and class actions were filed in New York and British Columbia (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/StubHub).
- **Generative AI use triggers backlash and needs provenance.** *Clair Obscur* lost two Indie Game Awards over AI textures (removed before shipping). Eurogamer scored *ARC Raiders* 2/5 partly over AI voices. NVIDIA's DLSS 5 was called "AI slop." Valve requires AI disclosure on Steam (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence_in_video_games). No standard exists for proving what is AI-made *(inference)*.
- **Release-date collisions hurt indies.** *Silksong* had about 5.2M wishlists, sold 7M+ copies by Dec 2025, and pushed several indie titles to delay after its date was announced (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollow_Knight:_Silksong).
- **Counterfeits hit collectibles.** The US CPSC warned about fake "Lafufu" Labubu dolls on 2025-08-18 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Mart).

## 3. Where money is flowing (and what is crowded)
- **Sovereign money:** PIF's $55B EA deal. It also funds the Esports World Cup Foundation, with prize pools of $71.5M (2025) and $75M (2026, moved to Paris) and a $20M club partner program across 40 orgs (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esports_World_Cup). The Saudi-Olympic Esports Games partnership was terminated 2025-10-30 and the Games moved to 2027 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esports).
- **Prediction markets:** Kalshi went from about $11B (Dec 2025) to $22B (May 2026, TCV-led). 89% of its 2025 revenue was sports, $871M was wagered on Super Bowl Sunday 2026, and it added 3M users for the World Cup. States are fighting it, and Nevada reached a permanent shutdown agreement (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalshi).
- **Live venues and IP-in-space:**
  - Live Nation is buying arenas in Paris and Copenhagen and put Ticketmaster inside Spotify in June 2026.
  - Sphere grossed $420.5M from 1.3M tickets in 2024 and is planning venues in Maryland, Tokyo and Abu Dhabi (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_(venue)).
  - Meow Wolf has Los Angeles (late 2026) and New York in the pipeline (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meow_Wolf).
- **Microdramas:** roughly $11B globally in 2025. ReelShort had about $700M revenue and 45M MAU, and DramaBox had $323M in 2024 revenue. US spend is about $1.3B a year, mostly women aged 30-60, with production at $50k-$250k for 50-70 episodes (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microdrama).
- **Collectibles:** Kickstarter's Cyberpunk trading card game raised $28.35M in April 2026 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kickstarter). Pokémon has sold 85B+ cards (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trading_card_game).
- **Crowded:** AAA publishing, ticket resale marketplaces, sports prediction markets, and Roblox-style UGC platforms *(inference)*. Big money is chasing the same few mass-market pools.

## 4. Underserved segments and structural gaps
- **Incumbents structurally cannot do these:**
  - Live Nation cannot credibly run fan-first, anti-resale primary ticketing while it is in litigation and its resale-fee revenue is under attack *(inference)*.
  - Publishers cannot promise server-life guarantees without admitting liability, as the Ubisoft suits show *(inference)*.
  - EA's owners have a debt-service reason to cut costs, so talent and IP are likely to spin out *(inference)*.
- **Preservation, offline modes and end-of-life escrow for live-service games have no dominant vendor.** Regulators are moving toward a code of conduct, which creates a compliance-shaped demand *(inference)*.
- **Age assurance for small game communities** (privacy-preserving, portable, cheap) *(inference)*.
- **Microdrama supply is thin outside romance and revenge genres**, and cycle times under two weeks favor AI-assisted and low-cost studios *(inference)*.
- **Merch demand forecasting.** Cover Corp (Hololive) FY3/2026 revenue rose 13.7% to ¥49.33B, but net profit fell 45.7% to ¥3.0B after a ¥3.3B write-off on the discontinued holoEarth metaverse and ¥1.8B of unsold merch. Overseas viewers were about 30% of viewership and roughly ¥10B of revenue (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hololive_Production).

## 5. Weird signals
1. **Gaming hardware pricing is now set by AI capex.** A Valve engineer described memory vendors giving a monthly price and a "yes or no" on volume, and saying no means "they never talk to us again" (Steam Machine page). Xbox reportedly discussed "radically different business models" to keep consoles affordable.
2. **The hottest fandom business still overproduces.** Hololive's revenue and concerts (+18.7%) grew while profit fell 46% on dead metaverse spend and unsold merch.
3. **Prediction markets want entertainment and awards.** They are already 11% of Kalshi activity, so fandom outcomes are becoming tradeable *(inference)*, despite the state legal fights.
4. **Country-level platform bans are now a compliance cycle.** Roblox was banned in Kuwait (Aug-Oct 2025, lifted after safety agreements) and in Russia (lifted 2026-06-10). Bahrain is drafting a ban and Indonesia has threatened one. A market for "regulatory-readiness for youth platforms" is plausible *(inference)*.
5. **Physical media is quietly retreating.** Physical Steam gift cards were discontinued in June 2026 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steam_(service)), and the Sony suit alleges PS5 disc cessation. Meanwhile Pop Mart's blind-box toys resell for RMB 1,000+, and VCs are funding that resale layer (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pop_Mart).
