# Briefing: Death, Funerals, Estates, Inheritance, Great Wealth Transfer (as of 2026-09-28)

**Coverage caveat:** The WebSearch budget (200/200) was exhausted before I started, so this briefing rests on about 40 direct page fetches, with no live search and no forum scraping (Reddit was blocked). Tags: **[V]** = read on a fetched page this session; **[B]** = background knowledge, not verified now. Practitioner pain points are therefore mostly [B]. Treat them as hypotheses to test.

## 1. Biggest shifts (numbers)
- **Wealth transfer is huge but concentrated [V].** Cerulli: $124T moves through 2048 ($105T to heirs, $18T to charity). About $100T (81%) comes from Boomers and older. $62T comes from HNW/UHNW households, only 2% of households. $54T is spousal, and about $40T of that goes to widowed women. Millennials get $46T over 25 years. Gen X gets $14T in the next 10 years, versus $8T for Millennials. [cerulli.com](https://www.cerulli.com/press-releases/cerulli-anticipates-124-trillion-in-wealth-will-transfer-through-2048)
- **Estimates vary a lot.** Other widely cited figures are around $84T through 2045, with $53T from Boomers. Households in the top tier (1.5% of households) account for about 42% of transfers. Experts doubt the "typical Millennial" sees much. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Wealth_Transfer)
- **Derived:** $124T over 24 years is about $5.2T per year, against about 3.07M US deaths in 2024 [V] ([CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/fastats/deaths.htm)). The average is misleading. The median estate is small and the top 2% dominates. The first wave is spousal, so widows are the immediate decision-makers.
- **Deaths are flat.** US deaths were 3.07M in 2024 and life expectancy was 79.0 [V]. SCI's comparable funeral volume was -1.4% in Q2 2026 [V]. There is no near-term volume boom. The growth is in dollars per estate, not bodies.
- **Cremation is winning [V].** NFDA projects 63.4% cremation and 31.6% burial for 2025, and 82.3% cremation by 2045. SCI's core cremation rate is 58.0%, up 60bp year over year. [NFDA](https://nfda.org/news/statistics), [SCI Q2'26](https://investors.sci-corp.com/2026-07-29-SERVICE-CORPORATION-INTERNATIONAL-ANNOUNCES-SECOND-QUARTER-2026-FINANCIAL-RESULTS,-CONFIRMS-2026-EARNINGS-PER-SHARE-MIDPOINT-GUIDANCE,-AND-RAISES-2026-CASH-FLOW-GUIDANCE)
- **Green disposal is legalizing state by state [V].** Human composting is legal in 14 states. Georgia (2025) and New Jersey (signed Sept 2025, effective July 2026) are recent, and California starts in 2027. It costs about $3-7K, versus $7-12K for burial and $4-7K for cremation. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_composting) Alkaline hydrolysis was legal in 24 states as of 2022, and sewer-utility permit refusals limit it in practice. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alkaline_hydrolysis_(body_disposal))
- **Medical aid in dying (MAID) is spreading [V].** There are 14 jurisdictions. New York's law was signed Feb 6, 2026. Illinois was signed Dec 12, 2025 and takes effect Sept 2026. Massachusetts and Pennsylvania are pending. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assisted_suicide_in_the_United_States) Scheduled deaths mean a planning lead time that unexpected deaths never had.
- **[B] Federal estate tax exemption.** The 2025 tax law reportedly set it at about $15M per person from 2026. Fewer estates are tax-driven, so planning value shifts toward administration, liquidity and family conflict. Verify before relying on it.

## 2. Pain points
- **Consumer estate tooling exists but is thin [V].** Trust & Will sells online wills and trusts, "probate assistance" and deed transfers. It says it has served "hundreds of thousands" of families. [trustandwill.com](https://www.trustandwill.com/about)
- **Digital assets are messy [V].** The average person has about 150 password-protected accounts. RUFADAA covers 47 states (as of 2021). Apple legacy access lasts about 3 years, and X/Twitter offers no heir access. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_legacy)
- **Preneed sales are a regulatory hotspot [V].** California's AG settled with SCI over Neptune Society and Trident Society preneed cremation marketing. The settlement includes $23M in civil penalties plus restitution (2024). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Service_Corporation_International)
- **Funeral pricing history [V].** From 1990 to 2018, funeral costs rose 3.7% per year against 2.3% general inflation, so 2018 prices were 177% above 1990. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_care_industry_in_the_United_States) The FTC Funeral Rule dates to 1984 and was revised in 1994. Its core is an itemized General Price List. I could not confirm any live rulemaking [V/unknown]. [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-funeral-rule)
- **[B] Unverified practitioner complaints to test:**
  - Banks and custodians are slow to release accounts and demand certified death certificates and letters testamentary.
  - Probate takes 9-18 months and executors are unpaid amateurs.
  - Heirs cannot find accounts (unclaimed property, orphan 401(k)s).
  - Inherited houses sit vacant and get cleared badly.
  - About two-thirds of heirs are commonly cited as leaving their parents' advisor.
  - Funeral homes lack modern software and staff.

## 3. Where the money is flowing
- **Consolidators are healthy [V].** SCI Q2'26 revenue was $1,103.3M (+4%). Average revenue per service was $5,985 (+3.3%). Preneed funeral production rose 7% and cemetery preneed rose 8%. Operating cash flow was $238.7M (+43%), and 2026 cash flow guidance was raised to about $1,085M. Volume fell 1.4%, and pricing and preneed are doing the work. Everstory Partners (stonemor.com now redirects there) calls itself the #2 US deathcare company. It reports 460+ providers in 23 states plus Puerto Rico and about 66K families per year. It has digitized about 400 cemeteries with drone mapping and bought Franklin Memorial Park in June 2026. [everstorypartners.com](https://www.everstorypartners.com/)
- **Fragmentation [V].** NFDA counts 15,401 US funeral homes, about 75% family or privately owned. The funeral sector is $16.3B in revenue with 105,300 employees, plus about $4.3B from crematories and cemeteries. [NFDA](https://nfda.org/news/statistics) The top six operators hold an estimated 25-30% share. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_care_industry_in_the_United_States)
- **Insurer and employer channel [V].** Empathy has raised $162M+ and claims 50M+ covered policyholders and 9M+ covered employees. Partners include MetLife, New York Life, Allianz, Aflac, Munich Re and TIAA. Products are loss support, LifeVault, caregiving and bereavement leave. [empathy.com](https://www.empathy.com/about)
- **Insurtech [V].** Ethos IPO'd on Nasdaq (LIFE) on Jan 29, 2026 at about a $1.2B valuation, versus $2.7B in 2021. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethos_Technologies)
- **Life settlements [V].** About 10M policies lapse per year against a market of about 3.2K policies and $4.6B face in 2020. Coventry Direct and Abacus Life were the largest buyers as of 2025. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_settlement)
- **Crowded:** online wills and trusts, preneed and funeral financing, direct cremation, bereavement benefits (B2B2C), insurtech life. Life-settlement buyers are also crowded.
- **Private banks [V]:** Cerulli (Sept 1, 2026) frames alternatives as a retention tool. 89% of surveyed firms say family meetings are essential during transitions. Wealth managers are already targeting the heir cohort.

## 4. Underserved gaps and structural blind spots
- **Middle-class estates.** Wealth managers chase the $62T from the top 2%. Estates of $200K-$2M, which are mostly a house plus a retirement account, get either a $400/hr attorney or nothing. Those estates are where the executor's workload is heaviest relative to the help available.
- **Executor operations after death.** Empathy handles notifications through insurers. The dedicated "estate close-out" workflow (accounts, property, taxes, distribution) has no dominant software owner. Trust & Will treats probate as an add-on [V].
- **Widows.** About $40T flows to widowed women [V]. Those women are the ones executing an estate while grieving, and advisor firms rarely serve them well [B].
- **Consolidators structurally cannot:** cut prices, since 3.7%/yr price increases and 58% cremation define their margins. They also cannot recommend competitors, and they market preneed to profit from the float.
- **Independent cemeteries** (about 115,000 in 2016 [V], [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death_care_industry_in_the_United_States)) are mostly undigitized, while #2 operator Everstory says it has digitized about 400 of its own. Plot records, deeds and maps are a data problem.
- **Logistics arbitrage.** Recompose claims to serve all 50 states, so remains travel to Washington state [V]. That is a shipping and coordination business where legality is fragmented.

## 5. Weird signals
1. **Lonely-death economy in Japan [V].** In H1 2024, 37,227 people living alone were found dead at home. Nearly 4,000 were undiscovered for over a month, and 130 for a year or more. 70% were 65+. One Osaka moving company says 20% of its jobs (about 300 a year) are removing belongings after lonely deaths. South Korea has the same pattern (godoksa). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodokushi) The US analog, solo agers with no heirs, has no equivalent service layer [B].
2. **Preneed decouples from volume [V].** SCI's preneed sales were up 7-8% while services fell 1.4% and cash flow rose 43%. Funeral pricing power is in prepaid contracts, not at-need decisions, and that contract book is a regulatory target.
3. **First UK NHS request to freeze a corpse (2026) [V].** About 600 people are cryopreserved worldwide as of 2024. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryonics) A tiny tail, but cryonics handling is moving into ordinary hospital procedure.
4. **Consumer appetite outruns supply [V].** NFDA says 61.4% of consumers are interested in green options (up from 55.7% in 2021), and 58.3% have attended services at unconventional venues. Legal supply is still limited to 14 states for composting and hydrolysis's utility permit problems. [NFDA](https://nfda.org/news/statistics)
5. **Griefbots are unregulated but researched [V/B].** Known products include HereAfter, Project December and Eternime. Scholars are calling for oversight. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Griefbot) I found no regulation or 2026 legal cases in this session.

## Suggested angles for other agents (hypotheses, not findings)
- Executor operating system for mid-size estates, sold via banks, insurers or employers, not direct to consumer.
- Wealth-transfer intelligence for community banks and credit unions: retain deposits when the account holder dies, since heirs move the money.
- Cemetery records digitization for the roughly 100K independent cemeteries.
- Solo-ager and "no heirs" post-death services (estate clearing, remote monitoring).
- Cross-state green-disposal logistics.
