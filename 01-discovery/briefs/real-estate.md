# Real estate briefing: office-to-residential, vacancy, property management, affordability (as of 2026-09-28)

**Sourcing caveat:** The session's WebSearch budget (200/200) was exhausted before I started, so I ran zero searches. Everything below comes from about 40 direct page fetches of trade-press and data pages, all dated Sept 2026 unless noted. I could not reach Reddit (blocked), Census, NAR, JCHS or Cushman data pages. Practitioner-pain evidence is therefore from trade press, not forums. Treat figures as reported by those pages and unverified beyond them.

## 1. Biggest shifts (last 12-18 months)

- **Rates are going up again, not down.** Redfin says futures now price the Fed funds rate at 5% by end-2027, 75bp higher than a month ago, with the economy running near 5% annualized in Q3 plus oil and Iran-conflict volatility ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/news/mortgage-rates-rise-economic-concerns/)). The 30-year fixed is about 7.4-7.6% ([HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/are-9-mortgage-rates-possible/), [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/mortgage-rates-have-gone-wild-so-whats-next-for-housing/)). Purchase apps are down 11% YoY and 42.5% of listings have price cuts.
- **Record buyer's market.** August 2026 was the strongest buyer's market since Redfin's 2013 records, with 58% more sellers than buyers. 44.7% of sales included seller concessions (Atlanta 72.8%, San Jose 4.2%), and 15.8% had both a price cut and concessions ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/news/press-releases/nearly-half-of-homebuyers-get-concessions-from-sellers-as-most-markets-tip-in-buyers-favor/), [Redfin HPI](https://www.redfin.com/news/home-price-index-august-2026/)). National prices are still +3.7% YoY, but the split is huge: San Francisco +12%, Chicago +9.2%, Dallas -1.4%, Austin -1.0%.
- **The rental supply wave has crested.** National median rent is $1,388, -0.4% YoY, with 7.0% vacancy (peak 7.3% in Feb) and 34 days to lease, the longest September since 2019 ([Apartment List](https://www.apartmentlist.com/research/national-rent-data)). Aug multifamily starts fell 15.5% YoY and completions fell 35.7%, while input costs are +8.9% ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/multifamily-starts-apartment-construction-august/830679/)). 1.2M units remain in lease-up (peak 1.4M), and concessions run 15.8% of stabilized units (South 21.1%, Austin 37%) ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/multifamily-lease-ups-rent-growth/830227/)).
- **Office is bifurcating.** National vacancy is 17.8%, down 90bp YoY, and asking rent is $33.20 (+1.7%). Manhattan is about 10% and San Francisco 25.9%. 32.4M sf is under construction, concentrated in gateway markets ([CommercialEdge](https://www.commercialcafe.com/blog/national-office-report/)). Office CMBS delinquency hit 13.2%, the highest since at least 2019, with 15.7% in special servicing and $39B maturing in 12 months. 71% of distressed balance is refinance failure, not missed payments ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2026/09/office-cmbs-delinquency-rate-cred-iq/)). $289B of loans on 14,000 office properties mature by 2028.
- **Conversions are moving to public land and affordable mandates.** NYC's 100 Gold Street grew from 3,700 to 4,000 units (1,000 affordable) on city-owned land, fast-tracked by the Mamdani administration ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2026/09/100-gold-street-conversion-housing-mamdani/)).
- **Regulatory stack:**
  - RealPage/DOJ: Pinnacle signed a consent decree Sept 4, and Camden is the only original defendant left unsettled ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/doj-pinnacle-settlement-realpage-lawsuit/830108/)). Private settlements exceed $500M ([tracker](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/algorithmic-rent-pricing-lawsuit-tracker/826178/)). New York State (Dec 2025) and Berkeley ban pricing algorithms. San Diego's ordinance carries $1,000 per violation, and a UDR class action was filed under it ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/udr-rent-pricing-class-action-lawsuit-san-diego/827109/)).
  - Junk fees: Seattle bans admin, pet and package fees from July 1, 2027 with treble damages, and the NAA tracked 140 fee-transparency bills in 2025 ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/seattle-ban-rental-junk-fees/827816/)).
  - ROAD to Housing Act: 350-home cap on institutional single-family-rental owners, build-to-rent exempt, existing owners grandfathered, key definitions and reporting mechanics unspecified ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/housing-law-build-to-rent-investors/825969/)).
  - Single-staircase reform: 7 states in 2025, Michigan in July 2026, with about 10% projected cost savings ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/single-staircase-reform-michigan-whitmer/827120/)). California AB 2074 (150 ft minimum heights near transit in 7 cities) awaits Newsom ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/housing-density-california-cities-bill-newsom/831115/)).

## 2. Acute, quantified pain

- **Renter distress is climbing into the middle class.** 20% of renter households paid late or missed rent in 2025 (16.5% in 2024, a series high). Middle-income households went from 14.3% to 21.6%, and 20.7% of renters could not pay utilities in full ([Multifamily Dive on Urban Institute](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/renters-struggle-rent-utilities-urban-institute/831066/)).
- **AI-armed pro se tenants.** Federal pro se filings topped 41,000 in 2025 vs a 23,000 average for 2005-2022. One tenant filed 22 pleadings in a weekend. Operators pay outside counsel to triage each one, and units sit idle. Some conclude cash-for-keys is cheaper than winning ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/ai-assisted-tenant-lawsuit-pro-se-multifamily/830391/)).
- **Pricing tools are now legal liabilities.** Operators face patchwork bans on nonpublic-data pricing, and consent decrees can require chief antitrust compliance officers and court monitors for "uncertified" algorithms.
- **Underwriting squeeze.** Midwest starts fell 10.8% because rent-growth forecasts sit below inflation while costs are up 8.9%.
- **Wage-rent gap.** A typical retail worker earns $34,436 vs $71,172 needed for a typical $1,779 apartment (Redfin, 2025 data) ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/news/retail-worker-rental-affordability-2025/)).
- **Back office is still manual.** PredictAP processes 7M+ invoices a year for 130+ real estate firms, and its CEO says CAM pools and triple-net coding make integration with Yardi the hard part ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2026/09/podcast-automating-cre-accounts-payable-coding-with-predictap-ceo-david-stifter/)).

## 3. Where money flows / what is crowded

- **Flowing:** AI leasing, tour scheduling, maintenance and renewals. Owners are insourcing management to use it. T2 Capital ($1.6B AUM) launched Base Living with 24 site associates and 8 corporate staff across three states ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/t2-capital-property-management-apartment-technology-multifamily-operations/831406/)). Mainstay raised an $18M Series B (Khosla and others) and bought Truelist ([Commercial Observer](https://commercialobserver.com/2026/09/mainstay-truelist-proptech/)). Lofty shipped an MCP server so brokerages' AI agents can act inside its platform ([HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/lofty-launches-mcp-server-for-brokerages/)). Gateway-market office construction is restarting, for example SF's 536 Mission (385 units or all-office, developer's choice) ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/536-mission-high-rise-downtown-san-francisco/831288/)).
- **Crowded:** AI leasing chatbots, listing tools, revenue management (now constrained by antitrust), and generic conversion feasibility brokers.

## 4. Underserved gaps / incumbent blind spots

- **Compliance-native pricing and fee software.** Vendors named in suits (RealPage, Yardi) cannot credibly sell "audited, public-data-only, all-in-price" tooling. There is an opening for a certified pricing and fee-disclosure layer across the patchwork.
- **Concession and lease-up optimization.** 1.2M units in lease-up, concessions are the new pricing lever, and no one manages them as a portfolio.
- **Landlord-side legal triage** for AI-generated filings, with cost-per-dispute models and cash-for-keys pricing.
- **Middle-income rent flexibility** (utility-inclusive, timing-flex products), since this segment's late-payment rate jumped 7 points in a year.
- **Maturity-wall workflow.** $39B of office debt matures in 12 months and 72% of loans transferred while current later defaulted. Lenders and special servicers need fast convert-or-hold-or-sell decisions, and brokers paid on transactions are structurally conflicted.
- **Missing-middle design and permitting kits** for single-staircase states, and small operators who cannot afford enterprise PM stacks.
- **Structural incumbent limits:** third-party managers are paid a percentage of rent and gain nothing from cutting fees or headcount, and brokers earn on sales, not on optimal holds.

## 5. Weird signals

- **AI is re-inflating one city's housing market.** SF median rent is +25% YoY, the mayor declared a "rent emergency" (10% cap proposal), eviction notices are at a decade high, and home prices are +12%. Meanwhile the national rent is negative ([Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/san-francisco-rent-emergency-daniel-lurie-housing/830200/)). Demand is highly local and job-driven.
- **Approvals with optionality.** SF approved a tower where the developer picks residential or all-office at build time. Zoning-as-a-real-option is a new instrument.
- **Movers are chasing price.** 18.7% of house hunters sought to relocate in Q2, the highest Q2 share on record. Las Vegas is +9,581 net and Florida +38,922, while LA is -28,652 ([Redfin](https://www.redfin.com/news/migration-q2-2026/)).
- **Seller-funded rate buydowns.** With rates rising and concessions at records, concessions are becoming a de facto financing product, and nobody tracks them by lender cost.
- **Trade press is automating itself.** The HousingWire piece on Lofty carried a note that it was generated using "HousingWire Automation."
