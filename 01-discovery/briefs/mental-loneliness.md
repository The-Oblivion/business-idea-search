## 1. Biggest shifts (last 12-18 months)
- **Loneliness is now a measured global health metric.**
  - WHO Commission on Social Connection flagship report (30 Jun 2025): about 871,000 deaths a year (~100 an hour), 1 in 6 people lonely, ~1 in 5 adolescents and young adults. https://www.who.int/groups/commission-on-social-connection
  - US: 24% of high-school-only graduates report zero close friends, up from 3% in 1990. For college graduates it is 10%, up from 2%. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loneliness_epidemic
- **General-purpose AI became de facto mental-health infrastructure by accident.**
  - OpenAI (Oct 2025): about 0.15% of weekly ChatGPT users show explicit suicidal-planning indicators, and 0.07% show psychosis or mania signs. TechCrunch's headline was "over one million people discuss suicide weekly." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot_psychosis, https://techcrunch.com/tag/mental-health/
  - Pew (Feb 2026): 12% of US teens have gotten emotional support from chatbots. https://www.pewresearch.org/topic/internet-technology/artificial-intelligence/
  - Replika: 40M+ users, ~25% paying, and 60% of payers report a romantic relationship with the bot. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replika
- **Emotional attachment to models is now a political and product fact.**
  - OpenAI retired GPT-4o from ChatGPT on 13 Feb 2026, the day before Valentine's Day. A #Keep4o backlash followed ("don't kill the only model that still feels human"). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-4o
- **Regulation is splintering fast.**
  - Illinois banned AI in licensed therapeutic roles (Aug 2025).
  - China proposed (Dec 2025) mandatory human handoff on suicide mentions, with annual safety audits for services above 1M users or 100k MAU.
  - Trump EO 14365 (Dec 2025) seeks to preempt state AI laws. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regulation_of_artificial_intelligence_in_the_United_States
  - Character.ai barred under-18s from chatbots from 25 Nov 2025. Pennsylvania's Department of State sued in May 2026 over bots falsely claiming medical licenses. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character.ai
  - A quiet backdrop: Raine v. OpenAI and at least nine similar suits remain unresolved. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raine_v._OpenAI
- **Age-gating wave for social media.**
  - Australia under-16 ban (10 Dec 2025): 310,000+ accounts deactivated by Mar 2026, yet reportedly 7 in 10 kids still hold accounts.
  - Followers: Brazil (17 Mar 2026), Indonesia (28 Mar), Malaysia (1 Jun), Florida (15 Jun). UK planned for spring 2027. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_media_age_verification_laws
- **US crisis-line politics.** 988's LGBTQ+ "Press 3" ended 17 Jul 2025, and Vibrant announced a restoration in Jun 2026 that excludes Trevor Project. A Harvard study estimates 4,400 fewer youth and young-adult suicide deaths in 988's first 2.5 years. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/988_Suicide_%26_Crisis_Lifeline

## 2. Acute, quantified pain points
- **Public distrust of the thing people are using.**
  - Pew (survey 22-28 Jun 2026, n=3,488, published 25 Aug 2026): on loneliness, 39% say chatbots hurt and 19% say they help. https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2026/08/25/do-americans-think-chatbots-help-or-hurt-people-using-them-for-loneliness-depression-or-stress/
  - Ages 18-29, the heaviest users, are the most negative: 49% hurt vs 23% help on loneliness.
  - Adults 65+ are 40-46% "unsure." That is an unclaimed trust vacuum.
- **AI harm has an emerging clinical footprint.** The Human Line Project (support group, 22 countries) reports 60%+ of members had no prior mental-illness history. UCSF's Keith Sakata treated 12 chatbot-linked psychosis cases. Mechanism: sycophancy plus artificial intimacy. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatbot_psychosis
- **Isolation at the fatal end (Japan).** In H1 2024, 37,227 people living alone were found dead at home, 70% aged 65+. About 4,000 were discovered more than a month after death, and 130 after a year or more. One Osaka mover says 20% of its jobs (~300 a year) are lonely-death clear-outs. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodokushi
- **Dating-app fatigue is a payer-side symptom.**
  - Bumble: 30% layoffs (Jun 2025), 2025 revenue $965M, net loss $895M, and its BFF app relaunched Sep 2025 around "friend groups and community." https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bumble_Inc.
  - Match plus Bumble lost about $40B of market value 2021-24. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_dating_service
- **Practitioner pain (not directly sourced; inference).** Independent clinicians need in-network insurance access, billing and admin help. The market signals are Spring Health buying Alma (Jan 2026) and Grow Therapy's $150M raise.

## 3. Where the money is flowing
- **Mental health is the #1 digital-health clinical category for the 7th straight year** (H1 2026: $7.4B across 244 deals overall; 20 mega deals took 45% of capital). https://rockhealth.com/insights/h1-2026-funding-and-market-overview-durable-roots-shifting-routes/
  - Talkiatry $210M Series D (Feb 2026; over $400M raised; 800+ psychiatrists; ~60 payers). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talkiatry
  - Grow Therapy $150M.
  - 64% of mental-health startups are direct-to-consumer, vs 29% across all digital health.
  - Rock Health says AI is now "sufficiently ubiquitous" that it no longer differentiates.
- **Exits and consolidation.**
  - Universal Health Services agreed to buy Talkspace for $835M (Mar 2026). Talkspace launched the "Tee" AI chatbot in Jun 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talkspace
  - Spring Health ($3.3B valuation in 2024) is acquiring Alma (Jan 2026). It was fined $1M in California in 2024 for running an unlicensed health plan. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spring_Health
- **Psychedelics inflect.** Compass Pathways' COMP360 has positive phase 3 data, was prepping FDA submission in Feb 2026, and named a new CEO (Sep 2026) for its "coming psychedelic drug launch." The FDA rejected Lykos's MDMA in 2024. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_Pathways
- **Crowded (avoid head-on).** Insurance-network teletherapy marketplaces, employer/EAP platforms, meditation apps, generic "AI therapist" chat, romantic AI companions, and mood journals.

## 4. Underserved segments and structural gaps
- **Older adults are the least-served, highest-need segment.**
  - ElliQ (Intuition Robotics): 800+ units with New York's State Office for the Aging, about 20 interactions per user per day. Distribution is government-led, not consumer-led. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ElliQ
- **Physical third places lack a business model.**
  - Men's Sheds: 900+ each in Australia and the UK. In a 2007 survey, 97% of members said "I have a place where I belong." There is still no longitudinal evidence, and funding is charity or government. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Men%27s_shed
  - Social prescribing exists in 17 countries. In Merton, London, GP visits fell 33% and A&E visits 50% (pre-COVID). No US reimbursement path was found in my sources. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_prescribing
- **What incumbents structurally cannot do** (inference).
  - Engagement-maximizing apps cannot sell "get off the app and see friends."
  - Foundation-model labs cannot accept clinician-grade liability.
  - Dating apps are paid by churn, not by success.
  - Meetup has 60M users but drew "controversial policy changes" after Bending Spoons bought it in Jan 2024. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meetup
- **Teens locked out of social platforms** (inference): under-16 bans leave a real demand gap for verified, safe, age-appropriate belonging spaces.
- **Culturally differentiated demand.** Asian adults were the only Pew group net-positive on chatbots for loneliness (37% help vs 25% hurt). Older adults' distrust is unresolved.

## 5. Weird signals
1. **Lonely-death economy.** Movers and cleaners already earn 20% of revenue from post-solitary-death jobs. Monitoring, prevention and "postvention" are unbundled.
2. **Heaviest users are the harshest critics.** 18-29s use AI most but 49% say it hurts loneliness. That is latent demand for an alternative they would trust.
3. **Grief over deprecation.** #Keep4o shows people mourn model retirement, so a "portable/escrowed AI persona" or continuity guarantee is a real unmet need (inference).
4. **Bumble is pivoting from dating to friendship**, and the founder-CEO came back to do it. Friendship may be the bigger, less monetized market.
5. **Ban circumvention.** 7 in 10 Australian under-16s reportedly still hold accounts, so enforcement gaps create a compliance and verification market.
6. **First enforcement on licence impersonation.** Pennsylvania's Department of State sued over bots claiming medical licences (May 2026), which points to a "verified-human-clinician badge" layer.

## Idea hooks (inference only, for downstream agents)
- Sycophancy and crisis-handoff audit and certification, given China-style audits and the state patchwork.
- Elder loneliness delivery paid by Medicare Advantage or Area Agencies on Aging, not consumers.
- Recurring in-person "friendship infrastructure" with a business model that rewards attendance.
- Back-office and insurance rails for independent clinicians (the Alma-style gap, not yet consolidated).
