## 1. Biggest shifts, last 12-18 months
- **The tariff regime flipped.** The Supreme Court ruled on 2026-02-20 that IEEPA does not authorize tariffs. CBP said refunds cover about $166B across 330,000+ importers. Collection stopped 2026-02-24. An automated refund system (CAPE) launched by 2026-04-20, and about $81B had been refunded by July 2026. More than 2,000 suits were filed. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_Resources,_Inc._v._Trump
  - Before the ruling, over 40% of small firms reported tariff cost increases, including 69% of retailers and 62% of manufacturers. https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey/2026/2026-report-on-employer-firms
  - 76% of firms that source internationally passed the costs to customers. Refunds go to the importer of record, so downstream small businesses and consumers who paid the pass-through get nothing directly (my inference).
- **Beneficial-ownership (BOI) reporting is dead for US companies.** FinCEN's interim final rule of 2025-03-26 became a final rule (issued 2026-08-11, effective 2026-08-14). Domestic companies are exempt, and only foreign-formed entities report. https://www.fincen.gov/boi. Wikipedia says Treasury "officially repealed" the implementation in August 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_Transparency_Act
  - The compliance deadline that filing-service startups were built around disappeared.
- **Tax-law reporting thresholds moved** under the 2025 reconciliation law (OBBBA). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_Big_Beautiful_Bill_Act
  - The 1099-K threshold is back to 200 transactions and $20,000. The 1099-NEC/MISC floor rises to $2,000 from 2026, then is inflation-indexed.
  - Section 179 expensing is $2.5M and the QBI deduction is permanent.
  - New tips and overtime deduction reporting falls on employers, and the IRS fact sheet says employers "must file information returns" on them. https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/one-big-beautiful-bill-act-tax-deductions-for-working-americans-and-seniors
- **IRS Direct File is discontinued for 2026.** Reports say it is expected to raise prices and revenue for Intuit and H&R Block. Only 3% of eligible people used Free File. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRS_Direct_File
- **AI adoption crossed the halfway line for basic use.** 46% of employer firms use AI and another 15% plan to within 12 months (Fed SBCS 2026). Among users, 83% use it for writing or marketing and 61% for individual productivity. 46% cite accuracy as their top problem. https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey/2026/2026-report-on-employer-firms
  - Adoption is heavily skewed to content, not back-office workflows (my inference from the use-case mix).
- **Incumbents are rebuilding around LLMs.** Intuit partnered with OpenAI (2025) and Anthropic (early 2026), and cut 3,000 jobs (17%) in May 2026. Xero announced an Anthropic partnership in March 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intuit, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xero_(software)

## 2. Acute, quantified pain points
- **Getting customers is the #1 operational challenge.** Rising costs are the #1 financial challenge (Fed SBCS 2026, link above).
- **Credit gaps:** 60% of firms applied for financing, only 42% got the full amount, and 22% got nothing.
- **Solo businesses (nonemployers) are the most exposed.** They are less likely to be profitable, about half carry no debt, and 31% don't regularly access external financing. 64% use personal funds when in trouble, versus 54% of employer firms. Nearly one-third plan to hire in the next year. https://www.fedsmallbusiness.org/reports/survey/2026/2026-report-on-nonemployer-firms
- **Bookkeeping trust and portability.** Bench shut down without warning on 2024-12-27 with $2.8M cash against $65.4M in liabilities and about 650 employees. Employer.com bought it three days later. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bench_Accounting
  - Customers' books were locked for days. Vendor-failure risk is real for this category.
- **Talent supply is flat.** There were about 678,000 active CPAs in Feb 2025, "slow growing," while demand is projected to rise. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certified_Public_Accountant
- **Payroll and tax complexity is rising.** The tips/overtime reporting and state-level payroll compliance are part of it. Gusto bought payroll-compliance startup Mosey in April 2026. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gusto_(company)

## 3. Where the money is flowing
- **Spend management and payments are consolidating upmarket.**
  - Ramp: $44B valuation (June 2026, $750M raised), up from $16B in June 2025. It reported $1B annualized revenue in Aug 2025 and serves 25,000+ businesses. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramp_(company)
  - Capital One is buying Brex for $5.15B (Wikipedia dates it April 2026), less than half of Brex's 2021 valuation. Brex exited SMB in 2022. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brex
  - Xero closed its $2.5B Melio deal on 2025-10-15.
- **Payroll and HR:** Gusto has $1B revenue and a $9.3B valuation (June 2025). It bought Guideline for about $600M (Aug 2025) and Mosey (Apr 2026).
- **AI bookkeeping and accounting startups.** Finally raised $200M in equity and debt (Sep 2024). InScope raised $14.5M (Feb 2026). Quanta raised $4.7M (Feb 2025). Khosla put $10M into Bench founder Ian Crosby's new venture (May 2026). MAVI (AI accounting services) appeared on TechCrunch on 2026-09-28. https://techcrunch.com/tag/accounting/, https://techcrunch.com/tag/bookkeeping/
  - Crowded categories: AI bookkeeping, expense management, payroll.
- **Incumbent tax players are stable.** H&R Block FY2026 revenue was $3.95B with $734M net income. Intuit FY2025 revenue was $18.8B.
- **Acquisition financing is loosening.** From 2026-07-04, an SBA 7(a) loan can be combined with a 504 loan for up to $10M in SBA-backed financing. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_Business_Administration
  - Search funds are proven but target $5-50M companies. About 100 launched in 2023 in the US and Canada, with a 35.1% IRR and 4.5x return across 681 funds. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Search_fund
  - No comparable formal capital exists for the huge number of sub-$1M cash-flow businesses (my inference).
- **Private equity in accounting firms** is a multi-year roll-up story *(prior knowledge, not re-verified)*. It pushes firm attention toward larger clients and leaves the micro-business tier to shrinking independents.

## 4. Underserved segments and structural gaps
- **Solo and micro operators (the ~30M nonemployers).** Ramp, Brex, and the CPA roll-ups target larger firms. The Fed data shows them financing themselves and less profitable, so they are poor prospects for per-seat SaaS.
- **Outcome-priced compliance and recovery.** Incumbents (Intuit, Xero, H&R Block) monetize subscriptions and upsells, and cannot easily take contingency or fixed-outcome fees.
- **Legacy local-marketing vendors are melting.** Thryv, the ex-Yellow Pages company, had 2025 revenue of $785M, down year over year, with net income of about $0.3M. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thryv
  - Demand ("reaching customers") is still the top small-business challenge, so the supplier base is shrinking while the need persists.
- **Accountable AI.** 46% of AI users worry about accuracy, and adoption is concentrated in writing. Nobody is selling an insured, human-backstopped back-office agent to a tiny business (my inference).
- **Local search data integrity** is a reported problem, but I only found generic Wikipedia-level evidence. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Business_Profile

## 5. Weird signals
1. **Intuit is cutting 17% of staff for AI while opening about 600 offices and 20 TurboTax storefronts in 2026.** The biggest AI bookkeeping and tax incumbent is betting on humans in physical places, which suggests pure-AI trust is not yet solved. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intuit
2. **Census restated its business-formation series in Jan 2026** (moved to 2022 NAICS) **and excluded internet-sales-linked applications from the high-propensity and corporation series.** Formation counts are being polluted by non-real businesses (my inference). https://www.census.gov/econ/bfs/index.html
3. **Bench's founder got $10M from Khosla in May 2026** despite the collapse, so investors still believe in AI-native bookkeeping. https://techcrunch.com/tag/accounting/
4. **$166B of tariff refunds are being processed through an automated system, and CBP said doing it manually would take "millions of man-hours."** Small importers that used brokers or forwarders may not know they are owed money (my inference). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_Resources,_Inc._v._Trump
5. **The 1099-NEC floor rose from $600 to $2,000 and BOI evaporated.** Two sources of small-business filing volume vanished in about 18 months while payroll and tip/overtime reporting grew. Compliance work is moving from filing to interpretation (my inference).
