# Emerging Markets Leapfrogging Briefing (Africa, India, LATAM, SEA) — as of 2026-09-28

Method note: WebSearch quota was exhausted, so this is built from WebFetch of trade press plus Google News RSS. "(hl)" means headline/snippet-level only, not verified in the full article.

## 1. Biggest shifts (last 12-18 months)
- **Hormuz/Iran-war energy shock (from ~Mar 2026).** India LPG shortage made induction cooktop demand jump 20-25x ([Times of India](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=India+LPG+shortage+induction+cooktop+electric+cooking+demand+2026) hl). Government is distributing 500k stoves, and the switch could add up to 27 GW of power demand. Sri Lanka went to a four-day week, SE Asia closed offices, and migrant workers left Indian cities ([Guardian](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=India+induction+cooktop+shortage+LPG+labour+exodus+restaurants+cloud+kitchens+2026) hl). UNDP says 2.5M more Indians are at poverty risk; about $50bn of Gulf remittances is at risk ([Al Jazeera/Hindu](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Gulf+migrant+workers+remittances+Iran+war+Kerala+Philippines+Nepal+Bangladesh+return+2026) hl).
- **Solar plus batteries became private infrastructure.** Africa installs 17 GW in 2026 (+45% YoY); 75% of 2023-25 growth is distributed. Africa has imported more battery dollars than panels from China since 2024. Ember says its estimates are 2x official statistics, and only 14 of 54 countries reported 2025 data ([Ember](https://ember-energy.org/latest-insights/the-take-off-in-african-solar-that-official-statistics-cant-yet-see/)). China shipped a record 68 GW of solar in March ([Electrek](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Africa+solar+panel+imports+2026+Ember+Nigeria+South+Africa+Kenya+surge) hl).
- **AI memory crunch is killing the cheap phone.** Memory is now 30-40% of a phone's bill of materials. GSMA is piloting a $40 4G phone in 6 African countries; about 960M Africans live under coverage but are not online ([Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2026/gsma-cheap-smartphone-africa/)). IDC says sub-Rs 9,000 phones may be "permanently uneconomical." LatAm smartphone shipments fell 10% ([RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=smartphone+prices+memory+shortage+Africa+India+2026+Transsion) hl).
- **India ends zero-MDR UPI on Oct 15.** The fee is 0.4% on P2M payments above ₹2,000, capped at ₹300, and comes with 18% GST. It hits about 4% of P2M volume but about 66% of value, a ~₹24,500 Cr pool at 40 bps (ET BFSI hl). The Supreme Court refused a stay on Sep 28, and retailers plan a "No UPI Day" on Oct 2. The distributor body AICPDF estimates a ₹7,000-9,000 Cr burden ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/no-upi-day-mobile-fmcg-retailers-to-protest-upi-mdr-on-october-2/), [Medianama](https://www.medianama.com)).
- **India insurance distribution shock.** The IRDAI draft (Sep 23) cuts general-insurance commission caps from 30% to 20%, sets 0% on motor third-party, and 5% on own-damage. PB Fintech fell 36% in one day (₹31,500 Cr wiped) ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/features/policybazaar-turtlemint-feel-the-squeeze/)).
- **Stablecoins moved from thesis to plumbing.** Yellow Card raised $40M in Aug 2026, supports 50+ currencies, and is expanding to Canada and hiring in LatAm and SEA ([Disrupt Africa](https://disruptafrica.com/2026/09/24/nigerian-fintech-startup-yellow-card-expands-into-canada/)). Flutterwave hit a $3.2B valuation with Ripple as an investor ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/payments-startup-flutterwave-hits-3-2b-valuation-backed-by-ripple/)). Nigeria opened a central-bank sandbox to stablecoins (Aug 12) and Kenya passed digital-currency legislation (Aug 6) ([RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=stablecoin+regulation+Africa+Kenya+Nigeria+South+Africa+2026) hl).
- **Anti-China-parcel walls.** Temu has 105M LatAm MAUs. Argentina proposes a 30% duty, Mexico charges 33.5%, and Indonesia cut de minimis to $3 ([Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2025/anti-shein-temu-backlash-latin-america/)). Indonesia starts marketplace tax collection on Oct 1.

## 2. Acute, quantified pain points
- **Africa's Series A gap.** There are six seed rounds per Series A (Technext24, Sep 28, hl). Jan-Aug 2026 funding was $2.10B and "highly concentrated" ([Capmad](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Africa+startup+funding+H1+2026+Big+Deal+Partech+debt+equity) hl).
- **Brazil household debt.** The household-debt indicator hit 82% (84.9% for low-income families; exact metric not verified), with record delinquency and over half of households near insolvency. Lula banned online betting and announced debt relief on Sep 25, but relief may reach under 1% of defaults ([Valor/Reuters](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Brazil+household+debt+2026) hl).
- **Indonesia paylater trap.** Paylater financing was Rp43.28T in May and online lending Rp100.69T. Defaults are rising and minimum-wage workers are exposed ([Kompas/Jakarta Globe](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Indonesia+paylater+OJK+2026) hl).
- **Quick-commerce economics.** Zepto lost ₹5,905 Cr in FY26, had 51% attrition, and shelved its IPO after its valuation halved ($7B to about $4.5B) ([RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Zepto+IPO+2026) hl). Amazon's Now/Fresh unit booked ₹3,065 Cr revenue against a ₹1,158 Cr loss ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/amazon-india-fy26-report-card-loss-balloons-48-yoy-revenue-inches-closer-to-%e2%82%b940000-cr/)).
- **Gig-worker strain.** More than 20% of gig workers log 70+ hours a week, and the government forced platforms to drop "10-minute" branding in Jan 2026 ([RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=India+gig+workers+quick+commerce+riders+2026+social+security+code) hl).
- **Cross-border fees.** A $200 US-to-Colombia transfer costs $12.13 on legacy rails versus about $0.01 in stablecoins. Freelancers cannot get portable income records for loans or visas ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/01/31/stablecoins-are-finding-product-market-fit-in-emerging-markets/), [Bloccpay](https://disruptafrica.com/2026/09/25/nigerias-bloccpay-is-a-global-stablecoin-powered-payroll-invoicing-platform/)).
- **Data-center backlash.** Equinix's Cape Town plan is 174 MW and 4.4B liters of water a year. Kenya's 1 GW G42-Microsoft project is stalled ([Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2026/south-africa-ai-data-center-protests/)).

## 3. Where the money is flowing (and what's crowded)
- **LatAm fintech is hot.** Funding rose 2.6x YoY in Q2 (hl). Plata raised $405M at $5B, Ualá $195M at $3.2B, and Kapital $125M ([RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Mexico+fintech+2026) hl). Félix raised $200M ($87M a16z equity plus $113M debt), has processed $8B, and runs on WhatsApp ([LatamList](https://latamlist.com/felix-raises-200m-series-c-and-reaches-unicorn-status/)). Mundi has a $150M credit line, has financed about $2.5B, and is profitable ([LatamList](https://latamlist.com/mundi-secures-150m-credit-line-from-column/)). A5X, a B3 derivatives challenger, raised $70M from Morgan Stanley and Goldman ([LatamList](https://latamlist.com/a5x-raises-70m-series-d/)).
- **Debt is replacing equity.** Examples are Nomba's $3M facility, IFC's $2.4M convertible to Complete Farmer, and Nelo's $100M line.
- **SEA.** H1 equity was $7.25B over 217 deals, but DayOne's $4.5B Series C is most of it. Deal count is the lowest since at least 2018 ([DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/reports/southeast-asia-startup-funding-report-h1-2026)). GCash and Maya each weigh ~$1B IPOs (hl).
- **India.** PE-VC fundraising was $6.8B. Sarvam raised $234M at $1.5B and Mahindra Finance runs 10M voice-AI calls across 12 languages ([RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Sarvam+AI+2026) hl). India targets $200B+ of AI infrastructure by 2028, has 38,000 shared GPUs, and set up a ₹100B venture programme ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/17/india-bids-to-attract-over-200b-in-ai-infrastructure-investment-by-2028/)).
- **Crowded.** Quick commerce, Indian D2C (Oziva spent ₹246 Cr on marketing against ₹463 Cr revenue, per [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com)), and the Shopee/TikTok Shop duopoly (TikTok >40% in Vietnam, per [KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com)). Also Nigerian consumer fintech and Indian voice-AI orchestration (Ringg, Bolna, Gnani, Blue Machines) ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/25/indias-ringg-gets-backing-from-peak-xv-as-it-pushes-voice-ai-past-the-phone-call/)).

## 4. Underserved segments and structural gaps
- **Rural and tier-2/3 India.** PhonePe has 720M users and 50M+ merchants, and plans 20,000 hires and 5M devices in 12 months for rural merchants. It says the MDR "allows us to take a long view" ([DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/phonepe-merchant-network-496175/)). Citymall does next-day delivery with no fees on ₹450-500 baskets across 60 cities, at -30% EBITDA ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/09/01/indian-grocery-startup-citymall-raises-47m-to-challenge-ultra-fast-delivery-giants/)).
- **UPI-data underwriting is now at risk.** SBI and the RBI's cash-flow lending push rely on zero-friction UPI data. ET BFSI asks whether banks lose their transaction-data edge under MDR (hl).
- **Savings groups as a credit rail.** Sandi AI has 10,000+ farmer groups in Uganda, Kenya and Tanzania. Groups buy solar irrigation with pooled savings instead of loans, and revenue is in the low hundreds of thousands of dollars ([Disrupt Africa](https://disruptafrica.com/2026/09/28/ugandas-sandi-ai-helps-farmer-savings-groups-access-solar-irrigation-without-loans/)).
- **Local-language voice and data.** AethexAI has raised $3M and handles 17,000+ calls a day with 300M-1.7B parameter models, because latency and jitter make foreign APIs unusable. It trains on call-center recordings and radio audio ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/03/these-two-founders-left-goldman-and-meta-to-build-voice-ai-for-markets-everyone-else-overlooked/)). Intron covers 57 languages (hl).
- **Ag AI that works locally.** Western models "fail spectacularly" on African crops. One project collected 5M+ crop images, and FarmerChat has answered 8M+ farmer questions ([Rest of World](https://restofworld.org/2026/ai-agriculture-local-data/)).
- **Solar and battery asset finance and data.** The boom is largely invisible to official statistics, so credit bureaus and insurers have little to price it on.

- India's induction stove panic (20x sales), while the Hindu reports low-income uptake is "sluggish". The demand is real but the grid and the financing are not ready.
- Mercado Libre killed its own Mercado Coin (Reuters, Mar 31, hl) just as everyone else went stablecoin.
- Yellow Card is exporting from Africa into Canada, with hiring in LatAm and SEA.

- OpenAI and Google both set consumer AI price floors below $5 in India ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/18/openai-launches-a-sub-5-chatgpt-plan-in-india/)).
- TakeMe2Space plans India's first orbital-computing satellite on Oct 1 ([DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com)).
